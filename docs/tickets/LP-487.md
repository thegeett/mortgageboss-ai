corrections to earlier work: an **amendment to ADR-332** and a **correction to LP-508's coverage table**.

## Phase A — and the decision that changed the rule

**A2 was the finding.** The catalog gives IH-2 lineage `['ins.mortgagee_clause_correct']` and no lender
tag exists — so on the tag layer alone, IH-2 has nothing to compare against. But the *extraction* layer
has both sides:

```
homeowners_insurance.mortgagee_name   14/15
closing_disclosure.lender_name         8/9
loan_estimate.lender_name              3/3
```

And the values make the case by themselves:

```
mortgagee_name  "United Wholesale Mortgage, LLC ISAOA"
lender_name     "United Wholesale Mortgage, LLC"
```

**The perception step is already spent.** The extractor has read the clause off the PDF into a typed
field; what remains is a string compare over ISAOA/ATIMA, a corporate suffix, case and punctuation. The
`ai_fuzzy_match` kind predates typed extraction existing.

**So the A6 question — "can IH-2 be self-calibrated?" — was answered by removing it.** No scoring run,
**$0, zero model calls**, no threshold to validate. The ADR-332 argument that would have been needed is
recorded as an amendment instead (below).

**⚠️ The real risk was never the threshold — it was firing on a correct file.** The corpus's one
binder+CD pairing reads **"Sistar Mortgage Company"** (CD) against **"United Wholesale Mortgage"**
(clause). In broker/correspondent deals the CD names the creditor and the clause names the investor who
will hold the loan. So **IH-2 cannot fire**: a mismatch is `needs_review` — "the clause names X, the
file's lender is Y, confirm". A test pins that no outcome in the spec can produce `fired`.

**⚠️ The corpus cannot pair a binder with its own CD.** It is **303 loose documents in two folders**;
exactly one grouping carries both. Every fill rate here means "the datum exists somewhere", not "two
facts meet on one file" — the same correction LP-485 recorded.

## What was built

**Eight tags.** Six new parsed inputs in `vocabulary_extra.yaml`; the two conclusion tags
(`ins.mortgagee_clause_correct`, `ins.condo_master_policy`) **already exist in the generated
`fact_tags.csv`**, so they are declared in `tag_production.yaml` only — adding them to the overlay fails
loud by design (never shadow an xlsx tag).

**⚠️ Every parsed tag is `document_type`-scoped, and that is the safety of the compare.** `lender_name`
is a field on **ten** document types whose meanings differ: `form_1098.lender_name` is the current
servicer of an *existing* mortgage, `voe.lender_name` is whoever ordered the verification,
`other_property_note.lender_name` is a *different property's* lender. An unscoped declaration matches on
field name alone and would have compared the clause against a name that was never this loan's creditor.
That is why the CD and LE are **two separate tags** rather than one unscoped one.

**Two recipes.** `mortgagee_clause_correct` (per binder, declines on non-binder subjects like IH-1's) and
`condo_master_policy` (loan-scoped).

### IH-2's normalisation — declared data, ADR-376-shaped

Truncate at `c/o` / `isaoa` / `atima` / `its successors` (everything after is addressing, not identity —
`LAKEVIEW LOAN SERVICING LLC C/O LOAN CARE LLC` is Lakeview's clause), strip punctuation, drop corporate
suffix tokens from **both** sides, then compare **whole tokens in order**. The vocabulary lives in the
spec's `reference_values` and a test pins it identical to the recipe.

**Token-prefix, not substring** — substring matching lets a suffix fragment match inside an unrelated
word. The prefix rule is also what absorbs the hardest real value without a special case:
`AMERIHOME MORTGAGE COMPANY, LLC, A DELAWARE LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANY, ISAOA, ATIMA` vs a CD's
`AmeriHome Mortgage Company, LLC` agrees on both tokens the CD states. All **11 real corpus mortgagee
forms** are pinned as tests.

⚠️ **The known false-satisfied direction, stated not discovered:** "First National" would agree with
"First National Bank of Chicago". Two tokens is a real tolerance and `satisfied` is the verdict no human
re-reads. Accepted because demanding equality would route ordinary ISAOA/suffix variance to
`needs_review` on nearly every binder and train a processor to click past IH-2. Raised in
`priya-open-questions.md` §9b.

### IH-7 — and a deliberate widening of ADR-376, stated rather than slipped in

CR-12's rule is **exact** match against a closed set. IH-7's basis field is **prose, not a code**:

```
"Guaranteed Replacement Cost"
"Replacement Cost"
"REPLACEMENT COST AT AGREED VALUE WITH NO CO-INSURANCE"
"Replacement Cost (RCV) at Agreed Value with no coinsurance; 100% replacement cost for portion of ..."
```

An exact match abstains on **three of the four** and leaves IH-7 permanently `couldnt_check`. So
recognised phrases are matched as a **leading phrase**, each value stating its basis first and then
elaborating. **ADR-376's protection is kept in two places:** an unrecognised phrase is `unknown`, never
"inadequate"; and a value naming **both** bases abstains whichever leads, because a mixed basis is a
human question, not a pass.

**Thresholds (ADR-361 — cited, never recalled):**

| value | source | page date |
|---|---|---|
| general liability **≥ $1,000,000** per occurrence | Fannie Mae Selling Guide **B7-4-01** | **08/05/2026** |
| **100% of replacement cost** basis | Fannie Mae Selling Guide **B7-3-03** | **08/05/2026** |

⚠️ **The liability floor is in B7-4-01, not B7-3-03.** The catalog rationale names no section and the two
live in different chapters; B7-3-03 is master *property* insurance and states no liability limit. Worth
recording because the obvious guess is wrong.

**⚠️ The condo scoping is an applicability predicate, not an outcome — and that is load-bearing.** The
spec schema does not permit a `not_applicable` outcome, and it is right not to: the applicability layer
resolves an **absent or "unknown"** predicate tag to `couldnt_check` and only a **definitely-false** one
to `not_applicable`. Encoding the scoping as an outcome would have meant a file that simply does not
state its property type was **skipped silently** instead of surfaced. On LF-6T3N — whose MISMO states no
property type — IH-7 returns `couldnt_check`, which is the honest answer.

### ⚠️ 3 of the 4 undeclared tags were declared — the fourth has no source

`ins.mortgagee_clause_correct` and `ins.condo_master_policy` are declared and materialise.
**`property.is_warrantable_condo` was NOT declared: no extraction field anywhere states it.** No schema
spec carries a warrantability field; it is a project-review conclusion (Form 1076/PERS), not a readable
datum. `fact_tags.csv` also shows it belongs to **CO-1/CO-3/CO-5**, not IH-7 — as does `condo.reserve_pct`
(**CO-4**). Declaring either would have added an unexercised path for rules not in this ticket, which is
the ADR-286/289 shape. Reported rather than fabricated.

## The two corrections to earlier work

### ADR-332 amendment — a self-authored LABEL is a distinct failure mode

ADR-332 names one way self-authorship corrupts a score: the **fixture** encodes the answer. Reading
ground truth off a **real** document escapes that entirely — the carrier had no knowledge of our rule.
**But the same reader would supply both the label and the logic.** On an ambiguous clause
(`UWM ISAOA ATIMA C/O CENLAR` — is the mortgagee UWM or Cenlar?) our label and our matcher encode the
*same* judgment, so the score measures **self-consistency, not correctness** — and does so most strongly
on exactly the cases that matter. A real file fixes the first failure mode and **not** the second.
Recorded as an amendment (house rule: small corrections amend, they do not get a new ADR).

### LP-508's coverage table was wrong — the guard reached 1 of 5

Found by review commit `0ec097b`, amended into `LP-508.md`. The `fields:` list named **parsed** fields,
but ID-5, CR-13 and PR-6 gate on **derived** tags computed from them, so they were never protected. Only
IH-1 was — because its derived tag happened to be named explicitly. **The rule: every derived consumer of
a distrusted field must be named explicitly**; that discipline was written into the file and then applied
to one of five entries.

## ⚠️ The standing rule this ticket adopts

> **A guard test must exercise the guard THROUGH A RULE EVALUATION, not by calling the guard directly.**

LP-508's `test_the_other_seeded_rules_degrade_too` passed because it called `evaluate_gate` **with tag ids
directly**. The mechanism worked; the wiring did not. **A green test over a path no rule exercises is
ADR-286/289 at the test layer.** Every verdict assertion in `test_insurance_pair_lp487.py` runs
`materialize_tags()` → `evaluate_rules()`; the recipe-level tests are additions, never substitutes.

## Assumptions and decisions

1. **IH-2 cannot fire.** The correspondent case makes firing wrong on a correct file. Raised in §9a — if
   a genuine mismatch is usually a real defect, this direction is wrong and should be revisited.
2. **The CD outranks the LE**, with the LE as fallback so a file early in processing is still checkable.
   Disagreement *within* a source abstains rather than picking one.
3. **Two lender tags, not one unscoped one** — `lender_name` means different things on ten document types.
4. **The ADR-376 widening is stated, not silent.** Leading-phrase matching for a prose field, with both
   abstains kept intact.
5. **`property.is_warrantable_condo` left inert** — no source field exists, and it belongs to CO-1/3/5.
6. **Test-count updates are census updates.** 37 pinned assertions moved because two rules genuinely
   activated and six tags were added; each site carries its cause. **No test was skipped, xfailed,
   deleted or weakened.**

**No new ADR** — the one architectural point is an **amendment to ADR-332** (self-authored label vs
self-authored logic), per the house rule that small corrections amend rather than multiply ADRs. The
catalog edit and the ADR-376 widening are recorded in this doc and in the specs themselves.
