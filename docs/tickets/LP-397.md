**Validating IS activating (the LP-393-6 coupling).** `is_eligible` = `validated ∧ measured ≥ threshold`, and the
LP-389 invariant ties eligibility to the active set. So flipping AS-6 to `validated: true` (with measured 1.0 ≥

that when she weighs the caveats below.

## The four caveats — stated, not buried

### 1. n=5 is THIN and ONE-SIDED — the dangerous direction is UNTESTED

All 5 goldens are `yes` (every statement genuinely belongs to a borrower). **The tag has never been tested on a
NEGATIVE case — a statement that does NOT belong to the borrower — which is exactly what AS-6 exists to catch,
and exactly the dangerous FN direction.** 100% on 5 same-direction cases is not proof it can spot a mismatch.
This is the AS-2 untested-trigger shape (its `loan_proceeds` firing value was n=0). **Proposed handling:**
recommend **RATIFY-ONLY until a negative-case scenario exists** — a human ratifies every verdict, so a false
"matches" is caught. Priya decides auto-vs-ratify.

### 2. The name-match strictness is Priya's underwriting call

LP-390-8a chose TOLERANT — middle-initial variants, nicknames (Bob/Robert), maiden vs married surnames, joint
accounts (match if EITHER holder is a borrower). That is the SAFE direction (over-strict would false-flag a
borrower's own account), but HOW tolerant — does a bare initial "J. Rivera" match "Jordan Rivera"? a trust/LLC
in the borrower's name? — is an underwriting judgment. **Flagged for her; not set here.**

### 3. The FP/FN asymmetry points at the untested direction

FP (says "not the borrower's" when it is) → a human re-checks a legitimate statement (annoying). FN (says
"matches" when it does NOT) → **someone else's bank statement accepted as the borrower's assets (dangerous)** —
and that is exactly the direction n=5 never tested. The bar is proposed HIGH (0.95) because the dangerous
direction is FN, but a high bar on a one-sided measurement is not a substitute for testing the negative case;
hence the ratify-only recommendation.

### 4. Three goldens are permanently unscorable (the D5 coverage gap)

The worksheet labels the tag on `investment_account` docs (inv1–3), but `stmt_facts` runs only on
`bank_statement`/`money_market` — so 3 goldens are unscorable AND investment/brokerage statements are **not
ownership-checked at all**, a coverage gap. The likely fix is **widening `stmt_facts`' applicability** (its own
ticket) — until then, **n stays 5**.

## Assumptions, decisions & gaps

- **No new ADR.** AS-6 has a single load-bearing tag (no second-tag trap), so it doesn't stay
  not-calibratable-yet. The one-sided-n caveat is handled per-rule in the rationale (a ratify-only
  recommendation), consistent with how AS-2's untested `loan_proceeds` trigger was handled — not elevated to a
  hard "never auto-ship an untested-negative tag" policy, which would constrain Priya's judgment and is a
  possible future ADR, not decided here.
- **Proposed a defensible default, decided nothing** — `validated: false`, the LP-393-5 pattern.
- **measured_accuracy is 1.0 but the rationale makes clear it is ONE-SIDED** — the 100% must not read as
  validated; the dangerous direction is untested.
- **What AS-6 still needs after the bar:** (1) a **negative-case scenario** (a statement not owned by the
  borrower) to test the FN direction; (2) the **D5 scope fix** (widen `stmt_facts`' applicability) so
  investment/brokerage statements are ownership-checked and the 3 goldens become scorable. Both their own tickets.
