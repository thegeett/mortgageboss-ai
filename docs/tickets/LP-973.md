## 1. Retry a reading from the screen

After an AI failure (an expired AWS login, a stalled task), every condition falls back. The round is
recorded as read, so no condition is "unread" and the page offers no Read again. LP-966's review made such
a row say "We haven't read this one yet", with no control, because the reading action is a product
decision. That decision is to add **Read again** for those conditions.

## 2. The round-2 plan

On round 2 and later, conditions seen again are listed in the plan with the plan they already had.
Decide: list them, or show only the new conditions in the plan.

## What was built

### 1. Read again for conditions the AI did not read

**The real cause.** A failed model call does not leave conditions unread. `read_round` reads every one
anyway, from the library alone:
- `reading_source` is LIBRARY;
- the status is NEEDS_CONFIRMATION;
- since LP-965 there is no type, so at most one generic task from the sheet's owner hint.

The run ends DONE and nothing is unread, so LP-952's Read again had nothing to offer.

**`condition_reading_state.read_without_ai`** finds the conditions a fresh reading can safely replace. Each
must be:
- read from the library, and not confirmed;
- with every task OPEN and from the reading;
- with no `condition_plan_changed` of hers. An earlier Read again's own `read_again` change does not count.

Anything she confirmed, changed, marked, added to or sent is excluded and keeps its plan. Each rule has
a test that only that rule catches. A sent ask is REQUESTED with no plan change, so only the
every-task-OPEN rule catches it.

**`reading_state`** reports `without_ai` (a count, and a state when the reading is otherwise done).
**`POST /condition-rounds/{id}/read`**, with nothing unread, now:
- clears those conditions (`clear_for_reading`): reading and plan reset, the guessed task soft-deleted, the
  drafts followed, and a `read_again` event recorded;
- queues the reading. The task reads them with the AI and plans them as usual (`_run_read`).

If the AI fails again, they are offered again.

**The screen** (`ReadingStatus`) says: "6 conditions were read without the AI. The AI's reading failed,
so their plans are guesses from the sheet. Read again to have the AI read them; anything you have already
worked on is kept." It then offers **Read again**. "Failed" rather than "couldn't be reached", because the
fallback also covers an answer that could not be parsed (`_ask_model`).

### 2. Round 2's plan shows only what is new

The plan lists conditions whose `first_round_id` is this round. Conditions seen again keep their plan
(`build_plan`'s carry-over) and are counted in one line: "N conditions from earlier rounds keep their plan.
You'll see them in the list after you confirm."

**A carried condition that blocks Confirm stays in the plan.** "Confirm 0132's reading first" must name a
row she can click (LP-966). With nothing new, the heading says "Nothing new to plan on this sheet" and
Confirm stays. Round 1 is unchanged.

**Left as the server says:** the drafts named in "When you confirm" and in first moves are the round's
own drafts, which can include a carried condition's code. Those emails are real and unsent, so the
sentence stays true.
