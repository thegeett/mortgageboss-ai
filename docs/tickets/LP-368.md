# LP-368 — Diagnosis: the 95 `tag_production` degradations (READ-ONLY)

## WHAT THIS TICKET IS

A read-only diagnosis of the biggest unexamined finding from the engine's first real run (LP-365): **95
`tag_production` degradations** on DB LF-6T3N. It was mentioned in passing and deferred three times (LP-366-A,
LP-366). The concern: if ~95 declared tags fail to materialize on a real file, the architecture's thesis (AI
structures reality into honest fact-tags; deterministic code queries them) may be resting on a tag layer that
largely did not run. **The only deliverable is this report. Nothing was changed.**

**Bottom line up front:** the 95 are **NOT 95 tags** and **NOT failures**. They are **95 instances** (subject ×
tag) of **5 distinct per-document AI `id.*` tags honestly abstaining** where the fact isn't in the document —
**correct behaviour by LP-326's design, zero bugs among the 95.** BUT investigating them surfaced a **separate,
more serious class the degradation count structurally cannot see**: **parsed field-name mismatches that
silently kill LIVE rules** (confirmed: `id.dob` → ID-3; `id.marital_status` → ID-7). That is this ticket's real
headline.

## PHASE 0 — WHAT A "DEGRADATION" IS (quoted from the code)

The count is `len(degradations)` logged at `verification_run.py:473-478` (`verification_run_done …
degradations=len(degradations)`). Degradations come from four sources, but LP-365's 95 are **all one kind**:
`tag_production`, emitted by `_scan_tag_degradations` (`app/services/verification_run.py:236-260`):

```python
for content_id, tags in snapshot.tags.by_subject.items():
    for tag_id, tag in tags.items():
        if (tag.value == "unknown"
                and tag.produced_by == TagProducedBy.AI
                and tag.confidence is None):
            degradations.append(Degradation("tag_production", tag.reasoning or "unknown",
                                            f"{content_id}:{tag_id}"))
```

**95 of what?** — **95 per-`(content_id × tag_id)` instances.** Not 95 tags; not 95 AI calls. The scan iterates
every subject × tag pair in the tags layer.

**Is it an error or an honest abstention?** — **An honest abstention, by construction.** The marker is
*structural*: `value=="unknown"` **and** `produced_by==AI` **and** `confidence is None` — the exact signature of
an AI producer's fail-closed fallback (per LP-326: a producer that cannot honestly judge abstains to
`unknown`-with-reason). A *genuine* AI "unknown" carries the model's confidence; a parsed/derived tag is
`produced_by != AI`. So the scan counts **only AI-stage abstentions**, and never counts a parsed/derived
failure (those produce an **absent** tag, invisible here).

**Critical corollary:** a tag whose AI group was **never requested** (see gating below) is **absent**, so it is
**not** among the 95. The 95 are groups that **ran** and abstained per-subject — not "not run."

## THE CENSUS (reproduced exactly from persisted `snapshot_records`, run `01039e93`)

Re-scanning the persisted snapshot with the code above yields **exactly 95** — so this is LP-365's real
census, not a re-run. The 95 are **5 distinct tags**:

| tag | mode | subject | AI group | consumer rule | LIVE? | real values | **abstentions** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `id.title_vesting_consistent` | ai | document | `id_title` | ID-7 | **yes** | **0** | **30** |
| `id.current_address_type` | ai | document | `id_address` | ID-4 | **yes** | 2 | **28** |
| `id.address_normalized` | ai | document | `id_address` | ID-4 | **yes** | 9 | **21** |
| `id.name_normalized` | ai | document | `id_name` | ID-1 | **yes** | 18 | **12** |
| `id.poa_present_and_acceptable` | ai | document | `id_poa` | ID-9 | **yes** | 26 (all `n/a`) | **4** |

30+28+21+12+4 = **95**. Every abstention reason is a variant of *"this document doesn't contain this fact"* —
e.g. title vesting: *"This is a W2, not a title/deed document. W2 forms do not contain title vesting
information."*; address: *"Bank statement contains no address field."*; ~4 of each are *"unknown document type
with no fields."* **No reason is an AI failure** (no "failed"/"timeout"/"truncated"/"malformed").

**The other side of the ledger (what DID materialize):** 438 tag instances, **31 distinct tags**, across 83
subjects — **142 parsed / 27 derived / 269 AI**. Of the 269 AI instances, **174 (65%) produced real values**;
the 95 abstentions are the other 35%. Notably **every `txn.*` AI tag succeeded with zero abstentions**
(`txn.is_money_in` 50, `txn.apparent_category` 50, `txn.has_identified_source` 17) — the Stage-A/B transaction
structuring worked perfectly. **All 95 abstentions sit on the 5 per-document `id.*` tags** and nowhere else.

## ROOT-CAUSE DISTRIBUTION — of the 95

**All 95 = one root cause: a per-document AI `id.*` group is run across ALL document subjects, and abstains on
every document that doesn't contain that fact.** Sub-shape from the reasons:

- **~79 — "wrong document type / field not present"** (title vesting on a W2/pay-stub/DL/bank statement;
  address on a W2; name on a mortgage statement). **Correct abstention.**
- **~16 — "unknown document type, no fields"** (~4 per tag on the 4 `unknown`-typed documents the classifier
  couldn't type). **Correct abstention given an untyped doc** — though see the classifier note below.
- **0 — AI-call failures. 0 — orphaned tags. 0 — undeclared-recipe deps.**

**Verdict on the 95: 95 correct, 0 bugs.** This is the fact-tag discipline working exactly as designed: the
producer structures what's present and abstains — with a reason — on what isn't. **The number is inflated by
design**: a per-document group asked about every document *will* abstain on most of them.

## THE VERDICT (the questions this ticket exists to answer)

### Does this invalidate the activation plan?
**Not via the 95** — but the census exposes a real caveat. The **15 declared AI tags that never materialized at
all** (absent, *not* in the 95 — `income.*`, `asset.*`, `stmt.is_reserve_eligible`, `stmt.owner_matches_borrower`,
including `income.qualifying_monthly`) are the income/asset family. They weren't run because
`_required_ai_groups()` (`verification_run.py:108-123`, applied at `:434` `only_groups=`) runs **only the AI
groups a LIVE rule's load-bearing tags consume** — a deliberate cost guard (no Opus pass for a dormant family).
So the ~15 rules believed "gated on calibration" are **also gated on their tag layer never having executed
once.** **Calibration is necessary but may not be sufficient** — their producers are **unproven on real data.**
Activating them auto-requests their groups, but whether those groups *succeed* is unverified. **That is the
honest caveat the activation plan should carry.**

### Is any LIVE rule silently degraded the way AS-1 was? (highest-severity)
**Yes — but the culprit is NOT in the 95.** Two findings, in severity order:

1. **PARSED FIELD-NAME MISMATCHES — silently-dead LIVE rules (a real bug class the 95 cannot show).** Parsed
   producers read a field by the declaration's `data` key: a `subject: document` tag reads the document's
   extraction field of that name (`subjects.py:85`, `raw.fields.get(field)`); a `subject: borrower` tag reads
   MISMO `borrower.{n}.{field}` (`subjects.py:179`). Three declarations point at names/locations that don't
   exist, so the tag is **absent** (invisible to the degradation scan) and its rule couldnt_checks on **every
   file**:
   - **`id.dob {subject: document, data: dob}`** reads a document field literally named `dob`. The driver's
     licenses carry it as **`date_of_birth`** (and MISMO as `borrower.N.date_of_birth`). → **never
     materializes → ID-3 (DOB consistency, LIVE) is silently dead.** LP-365 shows ID-3 `couldnt_check` — this
     is why.
   - **`id.marital_status {subject: document, data: marital_status}`** reads a document field `marital_status`.
     **No document has that field**; it lives in MISMO as `borrower.N.marital_status`. → **never materializes →
     ID-7 (marital/title vesting, LIVE) is starved of it** (on top of having no title doc — see below).
   - **`id.id_expiration {subject: document, data: id_expiration}`** reads `id_expiration`; the DLs carry
     **`expiration_date`**. → never materializes. Consumer ID-5 is **not** live, so lower priority — but same
     bug.
   - **Contrast that proves the mechanism:** `id.citizenship {subject: borrower, data: citizenship}` reads MISMO
     `borrower.N.citizenship`, which exists → **materialized (2 real values)**, and ID-8 got its data.

2. **`id.title_vesting_consistent` → ID-7 (LIVE) got 0 real values** — because LF-6T3N has **no title/deed
   document** (all 30 subjects abstained). Here the `couldnt_check` is **CORRECT** (a genuinely absent source
   document, the same class as the missing insurance binder — NOT a false dependency like AS-1's). But it means
   **ID-7 has never actually evaluated title vesting**, and whether it works on a file that *has* a title
   commitment is **unverified** (this file can't test it).

The remaining live-rule `couldnt_check`s in LP-365 (ID-2, ID-4) have **mixed roots** partially traced here
(ID-4 depends on `id.current_address_type`, which got only 2 real values across the file — thin but not
zero). A full per-rule attribution is its own ticket (below).

## RECOMMENDED FIX TICKETS (value order — NONE done here)

1. **Parsed field-name/location mismatch audit (HIGHEST — fixes silently-dead LIVE rules, cheap).** Fix
   `id.dob`, `id.marital_status` (both feed LIVE ID-3 / ID-7), and `id.id_expiration`; then sweep **every**
   parsed declaration in `tag_production.yaml` against the actual extraction field names / MISMO fact keys.
   Each is a one-line declaration edit (e.g. `id.dob` → `{subject: borrower, data: date_of_birth}` or
   `{subject: document, data: date_of_birth}`). Add a guard test that fails when a parsed declaration names a
   field no producer emits (the parsed analogue of LP-333's classifier-mismatch catch).
2. **Live-rule materialization audit (all 11).** For each live rule, confirm its load-bearing tags actually
   materialize on a real file, and classify each `couldnt_check` as **correct** (absent source document, e.g.
   ID-7 with no title doc) vs **bug** (field mismatch / undeclared dep). AS-1 (LP-366) and ID-3/ID-7 (this
   report) are the first three; finish the set.
3. **Dormant-rule tag-layer smoke test (de-risks the activation plan).** Before investing calibration/Priya
   labels in the ~15 dormant rules, run their 15 AI groups **once** on a real file (temporarily requested) to
   confirm the producers PRODUCE. Today the income/asset tag layer has never executed on real data.
4. **Degradation-count semantics + per-document AI efficiency (LOWER).** `_scan_tag_degradations` conflates
   "abstained: fact not in this document" (expected) with a real AI failure. Split them (or don't count the
   expected per-document abstentions), so the number means something. Related efficiency: the per-document
   `id.*` groups run on **every** document and abstain on most — an Opus cost that scales with document count
   for tags that can only apply to one or two doc types; consider gating a group to relevant document types.
5. **Classifier `unknown`-type documents.** 4 documents typed `unknown` caused ~16 of the 95 abstentions and
   will starve any per-document tag. Worth a look at what those 4 are and why the classifier abstained (its own
   small investigation).

## ASSUMPTIONS + DECISIONS

- **Judged CORRECT behaviour, not bugs:** all 95 abstentions (the reason strings show "fact not in this
  document"), and the 15 never-run AI tags (their groups are intentionally not requested for dormant rules —
  `_required_ai_groups`), and ID-7's `couldnt_check` (LF-6T3N genuinely has no title document).
- **Judged BUGS:** `id.dob` / `id.marital_status` / `id.id_expiration` parsed declarations (field name/location
  mismatch, confirmed against the actual document field lists and the `subjects.py:85` vs `:179` read logic).
- **`dti.qualifying_income_monthly`** appears in the never-materialized list only because the scanned snapshot
  predates LP-366-A declaring it; post-LP-366-A it materializes ($28,168.80, verified in LP-366). Not a gap.

## THE HONEST LIMIT (what I could NOT establish from the persisted artifacts alone)

- **Whether the 15 dormant AI groups would SUCCEED if activated** — they have never run on a real file; I can
  only confirm they weren't requested, not that their producers work. (Recommendation 3 exists to close this.)
- **Whether ID-7 works on a file WITH a title/deed document** — LF-6T3N has none.
- **The exact `couldnt_check` root of every live ID rule** — I traced ID-3 (id.dob mismatch) and ID-7 (no title
  doc + marital mismatch) with high confidence; ID-2 / ID-4 I only partially attributed (recommendation 2).
- This census is from **one file** (LF-6T3N) and **one run** (`01039e93`); the abstention *pattern* is
  file-shaped (it has no title doc, 4 untyped docs), so counts will differ on other files — but the parsed
  mismatch bugs (recommendation 1) are file-independent.
