## What was asked

> *"Each draft list should show stime stamp when it was created on list like an email list and
> opening model should also show that timestamp (date rime)."*

## Why this is one ticket and not a line in three

Three surfaces show a message: the drafts list, the modal, and the popover. **The list and the modal
were named; the popover is the third and was not** — and adding it to two of three is how a processor
learns to trust a time that is sometimes there.

So the requirement is stated as a class: **anywhere a message is listed or opened, it says when.**
That is checkable across the surfaces rather than three times in three places, and a fourth surface
inherits it.

## Which time, because a draft has more than one

A message has up to four moments, and they are not interchangeable:

| | |
|---|---|
| `created_at` | when the draft was composed |
| `sent_at` | when it actually went to the borrower |
| `read_at` | when a processor opened an inbound message |
| the last change | not stored — see below |

**The rule LP-812 already settled**: a message belongs at `sent_at` when there is one, `created_at`
otherwise. *"A draft composed on Monday and sent on Thursday belongs at Thursday — that is when the
borrower heard from us."* The list should order and label by the same rule, or the mailbox and the
timeline will disagree about when the same message happened.

The request says "when it was created", which for a draft is exactly `created_at` — the two rules
agree everywhere a draft is concerned and differ only once a message is sent, where LP-812's answer
is the right one.

**"Last changed" is not available.** `Communication` has no `updated_at`, and a draft is regenerated
on every add and remove, so a processor reading "created 3 days ago" on a draft that changed an hour
ago is being told something true and not what they asked. Adding `updated_at` is a migration and a
decision; it is **not** in this ticket, and the label must therefore say *created*, not *updated*.

## Two clocks, and the list and the modal want different ones

* **The list** wants what an email client shows: relative and short. "2 hours ago", "Yesterday",
  "3 Sep". A column of full timestamps is unreadable at a glance and that is what the list is for.
* **The modal** wants the full date and time, as asked — a processor who has opened one message is
  looking at that message, and "yesterday" is not enough to put in a note or an audit conversation.

Both from the same value. The difference is presentation, and it belongs in one formatter rather than
two components each deciding.

## Done when

- The drafts list shows a time on every row, ordered by the same rule LP-812 uses.
- The modal shows the full date and time.
- The popover's rows show a time. This is the surface that was not named.
- The times are **the same instant** on all three, formatted differently. A test asserts that from
  one value, which is the only way the claim means anything.
- The label says what the time IS. "Created" on a draft; "Sent" once it has gone. A bare timestamp
  beside a message that was composed on Monday and sent on Thursday is ambiguous in exactly the way
  that matters.
- An unparseable or missing value renders as nothing rather than as "Invalid Date". Both the timeline
  panel and the activity feed already guard this, so the third surface should not learn it the hard
  way.

## For the reviewer

The check that is worth running rather than reading: compose a draft, wait, add a second document to
it, and confirm the list still says **created** and says so honestly. If it silently starts showing
the regeneration time, the label is wrong and nothing in the type system says so.
