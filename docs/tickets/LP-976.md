# LP-976 — Conditions added after a plan is confirmed get a plan she checks

- **Ticket:** LP-976 (the owner, 2026-10-09: "yes, do the three follow-ups").
- **Source:** the LP-975 review, finding 3, reported rather than fixed.
- **Status:** to build. The proposal below needs the owner's yes.

## The problem

Since LP-975, a condition's plan is in force when she confirmed a plan it was on (its stamp) or, as a
fallback, when its round's plan is confirmed. The fallback exists for a condition that joins a round
AFTER confirm, which no confirm will ever stamp.

The LP-975 review measured who uses that fallback, and it is not only the condition she types in by hand:

- **Merging a PDF into a round** (`condition_enrich._merge_conditions`, reached from
  `enrich_round_with_pdf`) creates the conditions the sheet lacked. It accepts an IMPORTED round
  (`ENRICHABLE` keys on the round's status), and confirming a plan does not change that status. The
  reviewer attached a PDF to a round whose plan was confirmed and measured four new conditions with no
  stamp and `plan_confirmed() == True`. Their plans are in force at once: the plan the reading proposed,
  shown on no plan screen.
- **Forwarding an inbox attachment into a round** (`inbound_triage.merge_attachment_into_round`, from
  `POST …/inbound/attachments/{id}/condition-round` with `attach_to_round_id`) is the same merge.
  - Correcting the review's wording: this is her action. She picks the round for the attachment; a
    letter does not merge itself. What she is never shown is the plan of the conditions it adds.
- **The condition she adds by hand** (`create_manual_condition`) is the case the fallback was written
  for, and it has the same gap. She wrote the words, but its plan comes from the reading, and she never
  checks it before it is in force.

**Not a regression.** The round-level rule before LP-975 answered the same way. It needs a screen, because
`RoundPlanPanel` returns nothing once the round's plan is confirmed (and nothing when `planned === 0`).

## Proposed fix

**A condition's plan comes into force only when she has confirmed it.** Conditions that arrive after
confirm get the same check as the rest, in a smaller form:

1. **The plan panel stays on screen while any condition on the round has no stamp,** even after the round
   is confirmed. It shows only those conditions, under a heading like "2 conditions were added after you
   confirmed the plan. Check their plan." The layout and edits are LP-974's and LP-969's.
2. **Confirm stamps them.** `confirm_round_plan` on an already-confirmed round stamps the unstamped
   conditions, applies their steps and syncs their drafts. Today it returns early, which is why nothing
   ever stamps them.
3. **The round fallback goes.** `plan_confirmed` becomes the stamp alone. That is safe only once (2)
   exists: without it, a hand-added condition's emails would be held for ever, which is why LP-975 kept
   the fallback.
4. **The backfill:** conditions on a confirmed round with no stamp today (added after confirm) are stamped
   at deploy. Their plans are in force today through the fallback, so stamping them changes nothing she
   sees. Only conditions added after this ships wait for her.

**Owner decision:**
- **Recommended:** the panel above.
- **Alternative:** stamp a hand-added condition at creation (she typed it, so she has seen it), and use the
  panel only for merged PDFs and forwarded attachments. That is less to check, but the hand-added
  condition's plan still comes from the reading, which she has not seen.

## Done when

- **Reproduced first:** a PDF merged into a confirmed round adds conditions whose steps are in force before
  she sees them. The test fails before the fix and passes after.
- The panel shows exactly the unstamped conditions on a confirmed round, and nothing when there are none.
- Confirm stamps them, applies their steps and unlocks their drafts. Until then, the send guard holds
  their emails, now that it reads the stamp alone.
- `plan_confirmed` reads the stamp alone. Every caller of it, and of the send guard, is checked.
- The backfill stamps today's fallback-only conditions, so no condition changes state on deploy. Measured
  with the LP-975 staging query's `kept_only_by_last_seen` column.
- Peer review.

## Reviewer notes on the proposal (peer session, 2026-10-09)

The proposal is the right shape: the fallback can only go once something stamps a late condition, and (2)
is that something. Four notes, three of them measured.

1. **"Measured with the LP-975 staging query's `kept_only_by_last_seen` column" cannot measure this
   ticket's class.** Every writer of a `Condition` emits `CONDITION_CREATED` carrying its `round_id` —
   import, `condition_enrich._merge_conditions`, and `create_manual_condition`, all three read — so a
   condition added to a confirmed round HAS an appearance event on a confirmed round, the backfill's event
   half stamps it, and it never appears in that column. That column counts rows missing their appearance
   event instead, which is a different (and probably empty) set.
2. **And the obvious query cannot be run either:** `plan_confirmed_at` is deliberately not in
   `readonly.conditions`, so "conditions with no stamp on a confirmed round" is not answerable through
   the readonly views. Verified against the deployed view: `readonly.conditions` has no `plan%` column at
   all. What holds it out is `test_readonly_query`'s `EXCLUDED` drift guard, NOT `NEVER_EXPOSED` — and
   that distinction is in this ticket's favour, since it follows the `verdict` / `reading_run` precedent
   recorded there for a column that is not NPI but would need a view rebuild nobody has needed yet. So
   adding it to the view is a decision this ticket may simply take, with a one-line change to `EXCLUDED`.
   Until then, the class is measurable from events, which needs no view change:

   ```sql
   SELECT count(*) AS created_onto_a_confirmed_round
   FROM readonly.condition_events e
   JOIN readonly.condition_rounds r ON r.id = e.round_id
   WHERE e.kind = 'condition_created' AND e.condition_id IS NOT NULL
     AND r.plan_confirmed_at IS NOT NULL AND r.plan_confirmed_at < e.occurred_at
   ```

   That is "created onto a round whose plan was already confirmed", which is exactly the class. Run
   read-only against the local `readonly` views: 0 there, as expected of a dev database. Note
   `occurred_at`, not `created_at` — the view renames it.
3. **Stamp the late conditions with the time of the confirm that covers them, not with the round's
   original `plan_confirmed_at`.** Re-confirming must not claim she confirmed them at the first confirm;
   and the early return guards more than the stamp — one `ROUND_PLAN_CONFIRMED` event, one activity row,
   and `plan_confirmed_by_user_id` — so the second pass must stamp and sync without re-recording those.
4. **The callers to check when the fallback goes** are `_plan_in_force` and `unconfirmed_rounds_for`, plus
   `_plan_confirmed_for` which now delegates to the send guard (LP-975 review finding 1) — so the
   borrower's upload link follows the stamp with no further change, which is a point in this proposal's
   favour. `_plan_drafted` is a separate question (does the FILE have drafts to follow) and is unaffected.
