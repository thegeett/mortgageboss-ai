# bug-022 — nine findings because nobody read the first two words of the line

- **Status:** Fixed
- **Date:** 2026-09-11
- **Found on:** staging `LF-XMB2`
- **Scope:** Backend — `services/tag_production.py` (a deterministic floor under one AI tag)

## The report

Three trivial transactions produced **nine** Couldn't-check findings on LF-XMB2:

| Line | Amount | Rules that abstained |
|---|---:|---|
| `Zel To Mariam Imoisili` | $70 | AS-1, AS-2, AS-12 |
| `Online Transfer To XXXXX8307` | $30 | AS-1, AS-2, AS-12 |
| `Online Transfer To XXXXX8307` | $30 | AS-1, AS-2, AS-12 |

Every one of them said a version of *"the file does not clearly establish the transaction direction"*.
The direction is the first two words of each line.

## The cause

`documents_section._direction` classifies from `transaction_type` **alone**, and that is correct: the
extractor stores a positive amount, so inferring "credit" from it would forge a deposit on every
unlabelled withdrawal. When the type is null it returns `None`, the model is handed `"direction": null`,
and it answers "unknown" — honestly, because it was asked about a field that was empty rather than about
the sentence in front of it.

`txn.is_money_in` is the **applicability predicate** of AS-1, AS-2 and AS-12, so one unresolved
direction is three abstentions. Three lines, nine findings.

## The fix

A deterministic floor **beneath** the model, in `_money_in_tag`:

- the model is asked first and a definite "in"/"out" is **never** overridden;
- only when the judgment is not a definite direction is the line's opening consulted, against a small
  table of prepositions the bank itself printed (`online transfer to/from`, `transfer to/from`,
  `wire to/from`, `zelle payment to/from`, `zelle to/from`, `zel to/from`);
- a line that also says it was undone — reversal, returned, refund, chargeback — stays unknown, because
  the preposition no longer describes where the money ended up;
- the result is emitted **DERIVED**, with no confidence and a reason naming the words it read, so a
  ratifier can tell what was judged from what was printed.

**Why not simply improve the prompt.** The Stage-A prompt is the calibrated artefact and keys the
Stage-A cache (`_stage_a_version`), so editing it re-runs the model over every transaction in the corpus
and invalidates a measured tag. A floor in code is readable, testable and cheap.

## What this touches

`txn.is_money_in` gates **AS-1, AS-2, AS-12 and FR-5** — that is the blast radius.

**The eval corpus is untouched, checked rather than assumed.** Every directional fixture already carries
a definite stubbed direction (`ONLINE TRANSFER FROM SAVINGS` → in, `ONLINE TRANSFER TO CHECKING` → out,
`WIRE TO BROKERAGE ACCT` → out, …), and the only fixture stubbed `unknown` is `AMBIGUOUS LINE ITEM`,
which has no directional opening. The fallback fires only where the judgment is not a definite
direction, so **no eval case changes**. Worth noting the corroboration: on every directional key the
labelled corpus agrees with the table — TO is out, FROM is in.

**It also raises bug-021's merge rate.** Established in that ticket's review: four of the twenty lines on
the duplicated PNC statement fail to merge because `transaction_type` differs between the two copies
("transfer" vs "withdrawal"), so one copy resolves a direction and the other does not, their tag values
differ, and the duplicate guard correctly refuses. Those are these same "…To…" lines. Resolving direction
from the line makes both copies agree, and they merge.

## For the domain expert

**The prefix table is a domain judgement, not an engineering one.** It encodes that a US bank statement
line opening "Online Transfer To" always means money leaving the account. That matches every directional
line in the labelled corpus and every one on LF-XMB2, but it is a claim about how banks print
statements, and the cost of being wrong is asymmetric: a "To" line that is really a deposit would drop
out of AS-1's large-deposit sweep rather than be surfaced. Worth her confirming the list — and worth
her saying whether any other openings should join it.

## Not in this ticket

**FR-5's category enum.** ATM withdrawals and a $24.34 card purchase abstain because
`txn.apparent_category` has no value for cash or a card purchase, so they read "unknown" rather than
something FR-5 can rule out. Fixing that means either a prompt change or a new vocabulary value
(`cash_withdrawal`) — a calibrated-artefact change and a domain decision, so it is its own ticket.

## Tests

The LF-XMB2 lines resolve (`Zel To …` → out, `Online Transfer To …` → out); a definite model answer is
never overridden, in either direction; a reversal stays unknown; a line with no directional opening
stays unknown; the tag is DERIVED with no confidence and its reason quotes the words it read; and the
eval corpus's own keys are asserted unaffected.

## Review — REVIEWED

Peer review of `21ef52e2` (second session). **The floor is correct, conservative and should ship** — but
it clears far less than this ticket implies, and it misses lines on LF-XMB2 itself. Measured rather than
argued. CI is green (2,740 passed, 0 failed here too). One bar note added, one test added; the table
itself left for the domain expert, with data for her to decide on.

### Measured: the floor clears 14 of 48, not "the first two words of each line"

> ✅ **These counts are NOT affected by the rows-across-runs error found in bug-024.** Read once and
> recorded here because this is the section that goes to the domain expert. They come from
> `extractions.extracted_data` — one row per transaction per DOCUMENT, with `is_current` on the
> extraction — so re-verifying a file does not multiply them; a second upload of the same statement
> would, and bug-021 is the ticket that measures that separately. What the error does affect is anything
> counted out of `readonly.findings`, which this section does not use.

Across staging, the transactions whose direction cannot be classified today (`transaction_type` null or
ambiguous — 48 rows on bank statements), grouped by what the line actually says:

|---|---:|---|

| `save as you go transfer debit to xxxxxxxxxxx3627` | 9 | **✗ missed** |

**14 of 48 resolve (29%), and 12 of those are "out".** Three things follow:

1. **The misses include LF-XMB2's own statements.** `trnsfr to standard checking` and `trnsfr fr
   interest checking` are PNC's abbreviations for the very prepositions the table encodes, and they sit
   on the duplicated PNC statement this ticket's sibling (bug-021) analysed. So "the direction is the
   first two words of each line" is true of the nine findings named here and not of the file as a whole.
2. **The largest single family is `online banking transfer to/from …` — 13 rows across 3 files** — which
   states the preposition plainly and is missed because the table anchors on `online transfer`.
   `save as you go transfer debit to …` (9 rows) even contains the word *debit*.
3. **`transfer ila patel` is the counter-example that justifies the trailing space.** A payee whose given
   name begins with the letters of a preposition; a bare `transfer ` prefix would read a person's name as
   a direction, invisibly, because the line looks directional. Pinned in a new test.

**Should it ship before she answers? Yes.** It is strictly better than today (14 abstentions cleared, none
newly wrong), it fails to the model's own "unknown", every claim it makes is the preposition the bank
printed, and it is reversible. What should NOT happen is extending the table without her: each new prefix
is another domain claim with the same asymmetric cost, and the three families above are exactly what she
should rule on — with these counts in front of her.

### The other questions

- **Q2's premise is wrong, and the note belongs elsewhere.** `txn.is_money_in` appears in no bar's
  `load_bearing_ai_tags` — it is an applicability predicate, and the bars for AS-1/AS-2/AS-12 measure
  `txn.apparent_category` and `txn.has_identified_source`. So there is no accuracy number this change
  invalidates and nothing to re-measure in the bug-019 sense. What DOES change is FR-5's population, and
  FR-5's bar is where the claim "all three active transaction rules scope `eq in`" lives — so the note
  went there, about scope: ~12 lines newly reach it, each an AI call and a possible needs_review.
- **Q3: nothing keys on `produced_by` for this tag in a way that bites.** Three call sites read it and
  all three are safe: `gate._authored_sentence` promotes a DERIVED tag's reasoning into the finding's
  message but only on an abstention, and the floor's tag always carries a definite value;
  `deterministic`'s AI-reason path requires a rule gating on exactly ONE tag (AS-1 gates on three); and
  `_scan_tag_degradations` keys on `unknown + AI + confidence is None`, which the floor never emits. The
  last one is worth knowing rather than fixing: where the model FAILED (not merely returned null) and the
  floor resolves the line, that transaction no longer raises a degradation for this tag — but it still
  does through its sibling `txn.apparent_category`, so the production failure stays visible.
- **Q4, measured: ~12 lines enter FR-5's scope** (the "out" resolutions above), and the AS-1/AS-2/AS-12
  side is the win — those same lines become `not_applicable` instead of three abstentions each.
- **The bug-021 interaction is real and confirmed** by that ticket's own measurement: the four lines that
  failed to merge did so because `transaction_type` differed between copies, and they are `trnsfr to` /
  `zel to` lines. Note the sharper consequence: the two `trnsfr` ones will still not merge, because the
  table misses that abbreviation.
