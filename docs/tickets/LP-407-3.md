## D1 — the vacuity traces (the STOP gate, done first)

| rule | operand 1 | operand 2 | independent? | verdict |
|------|-----------|-----------|--------------|---------|
| **PC-2** | `contract.loan_sales_price` ← purchase agreement `sales_price` (a DOCUMENT) | `property.purchase_price` ← 1003/MISMO | **YES** — the contract vs the application | **WRITE** |
| **DT-2** | "HOA detected" ← `hoa_statement` presence | `housing.hoa_monthly` ← the same `hoa_statement.dues_amount` | **NO** — both trace to one field (the DTI auto-includes what it detects) | **STOP (vacuous)** |
| **DT-4** | `housing.taxes_monthly` ← `property_tax_bill.annual_tax_amount` | an ESTIMATE from `assessed_value` × a tax rate | operand 2 is **real but UNWIRED** | **STOP (needs-producer + needs-definition)** |

- **DT-2 — vacuous.** `rule_tags.csv` maps DT-2 to `housing.hoa_monthly` ALONE. There is no independent
  stated-HOA figure in the snapshot (the DTI's HOA line comes only from the `hoa_statement`; the `DtiOverride`
  is not a snapshot fact). "Is the detected HOA in the DTI?" compares the `hoa_statement` to itself — always
  satisfied when present. **Not written** (the DT-5 self-source pattern, second instance — ADR-330).
- **DT-4 — not vacuous, but blocked.** Its independent operand (a tax estimate from the assessed value) needs
  (a) a `property.assessed_value` **tag — NOT PRODUCED** (empty grep across `tag_production.yaml` /
  `vocabulary_extra.yaml` / `fact_tags.csv`), and (b) a jurisdiction **tax/mill rate — a Priya value.** The DTI
  reads `annual_tax_amount` directly and computes no estimate. **Stopped: needs-producer + needs-definition** —
  its own ticket.

## D2 — DT-2's `not_applicable` / presence problem

Moot (DT-2 stopped on D1), but confirmed and generalized: a property with no HOA should be `not_applicable`,
but `housing.hoa_monthly` is a **number** tag — "no HOA" and "unreadable HOA" both collapse to `unknown`, so a
DT-2 keyed on it would `couldnt_check` on every no-HOA file. **No HOA-presence signal exists** (no document-type
presence tag the applicability layer can read). An optional-component rule needs a **presence enum** its
applicability gates on — a producer follow-up, not invented here. (Required components — taxes/insurance —
correctly `couldnt_check` on `unknown`; the gap is specific to OPTIONAL ones.) Recorded in ADR-330.

## D3 — tolerances (Priya's)

- **PC-2 — EXACT, no threshold.** `rule_kinds.csv` classifies it exact / `threshold_needs_signoff=false`. The
  compare is Decimal equality, so a trailing-zero rendering (365000 vs 365000.00) is NOT a mismatch; a genuine
  difference fires. No threshold → PC-2 **activates** via the no-ai-dependency gate (not held). **Priya
  refinement (optional, non-blocking):** *"Should a sub-dollar rounding tolerance ever be allowed, or must the
  contract price and the 1003 price match to the cent?"* — today exact, per the classification.
- **DT-4 — would need a tolerance** (actual vs estimate implies "within X% / $Y", a Priya threshold on a
  `no-ai-threshold-pending` bar). Moot — DT-4 stopped on D1.

## The spec as written

**PC-2** (`specs/PC-2.yaml`) — deterministic structural, `subject_enumeration: loan`, two `tag` operands, one
fired outcome on `contract_price != file_price` (Decimal inequality), reason **interpolating both prices**
(the PC-7 pattern), satisfied default. No threshold. Mirrors PC-7 (number-vs-value operand path) + OC-1 (loan
enumeration). Reused the generic evaluator — no per-rule Python. Curated fact labels added for
`contract.loan_sales_price` / `property.purchase_price` (the couldnt_check reason guard).

## THE PROOF (reported, not predicted)

Keyless (parsed + derived materialization; deterministic evaluator):

| snapshot | verdict | reason |
|----------|---------|--------|
| **LF-6T3N** (both 365000) | **satisfied** | "the purchase contract's sale price matches the purchase price stated in the loan file" |
