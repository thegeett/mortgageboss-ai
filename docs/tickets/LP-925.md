## Design

### The package (S3-10), on the Conditions tab (README rule 9; the Lender package tab stays Phase 6's)

**What goes in.** The newest imported round's conditions that are **Ready to send**, open with the
lender and not information-only, whatever their heading. S3-10 packages 0006, a prior-to-funding
condition that was ready.
- Prior-to-funding conditions **not yet ready** are listed in an info line ("they go with the closing
  package").
- Each open prior-to-docs condition that is not ready is an attention warning: "1228 (prior to docs) is
  still open — …. Docs cannot be drawn until it clears."

**One PDF per condition,** in sheet order, named `{code} - {Category}.pdf`: `7086 - Assets.pdf`,
`0132 - Disclosure.pdf`, `0006 - Invoice.pdf`.
- The category is the library type's category; otherwise the lender's own category.
- The documents are those the condition's items accepted (LP-923 evidence on done items), plus a
  document an item found already in the file (0006's invoice). Their pages are merged in item order
  with pymupdf (`insert_pdf`).
- Pages are counted from the stored PDF, or from the extraction's `page_count_present` when the bytes
  cannot be read.
- A condition with no document (6178, a push-back) has no file, and its note says why.

**One note per condition (decision 4: in the package and in "Copy all notes").** The note is drafted
when she builds the package.
- **One AI call for every row** (`ai/prompts/conditions/package_notes_v1.txt`) gets each row's facts:
  what was asked, the documents' titles, the verified and required figures, the dates and the answers she
  gave. It writes one line per condition.
- **Code checks the numbers.** A note with a number that is not in its row's facts is replaced by the
  code-written note. So is every note, when the model is unavailable.
- A note the AI wrote carries the violet "drafted · numbers checked by code" mark; a code-written one
  says "written by code". She edits any note, and her edit is kept (and marked hers).
- Per-lender fields (§4a change 7) come from the lender's settings. UWM asks for a note. Sun West also
  asks for "Name of source" (the document's bank or issuer) and "Date verified" (when the evidence
  passed); both are filled by code and editable.

**Warnings before submitting:**
- open prior-to-docs conditions;
- evidence expiring before the lender's close-by date (the letter's expiry for the evidence's kind falls
  before its close-by);
- a DU re-run the figures check flagged and she has not marked done ("Mark DU re-run done").

**The cutoff chip:** "UWM upload cutoff 8:00 PM ET · 2 h 14 m left", from the lender's settings
(`upload_cutoff`, `upload_cutoff_tz`) and the clock.

**Actions:**
- **Download package (N PDFs + notes)** is a zip of the merged PDFs and `notes.txt`.
- **Copy all notes** puts "7086 — …" lines on the clipboard.
- **Mark submitted** moves the ticked conditions to **Sent to lender** through Stage 2's
  `move_prep_status`, with `sent_at` now. Each move writes `condition_prep_moved`, the only way our track
  moves. The package records what was sent: the codes, the file names, the pages and the notes. The
  next round's comparison (Stage 2) then shows what the lender cleared. The lender's track is never
  touched.

**Migration:** `condition_packages` holds round, status (`built`, `submitted`), `rows` (JSONB: per
condition the file name, document ids, pages, note, note source and fields), `du_rerun_done_at`,
`submitted_at` and `submitted_by_user_id`. One open package per round; building again keeps her edited
notes and adds newly ready conditions. It is excluded from the readonly layer (notes carry figures).

### Lender settings (S3-11), in Administration → Lenders

The lender page gains a **Condition settings** section (admin only; saves with **Save changes**):
- **Mortgagee clause** (`lenders.mortgagee_clause`), filled from the newest approval letter's clause when
  empty. It is used in insurance emails.
- **Condition upload cutoff**: time and time zone, shown on the package.
- **What the upload asks per condition**: a note, name of source, date verified ("Sun West asks for all
  three.").
- **Who does what at this lender** (decisions 1 and 5): "Lender orders the final inspection / appraisal
  updates", "Lender orders title updates, insurance, payoffs (Processor Assist)", and "New files default
  to 'Lender is processing this file' (Underwriting+)".
- **Lender codes to review**: the lender's codes seen on sheets but not mapped to a library type, with
  an example wording and how many files had them. Choosing a type maps it (status `mapped`); new
  imports use it from then on.

The settings are written to `lenders.condition_settings` (the keys `lender_condition_settings` already
reads) and `lenders.mortgagee_clause`. **Built as its own routes, not through `PATCH /lenders/{id}`** (a
change from the first draft of this design): `GET` and `PUT` (admin) `/lenders/{id}/condition-settings`,
so saving the settings cannot touch the lender's other fields. The codes have
`GET /lenders/{id}/codes-to-review`, `PUT /lenders/{id}/codes/{code}` (admin), and
`GET /lenders/library-types` for the select.

## Review (of `b415c136`)

**No defects.** One boundary recorded, the exemption you asked about judged, and the Stage 2 flake you
fixed confirmed closed.

### The judgment you asked for: the `_UNGATED_BY_DESIGN` entry

**The exemption is right**, on four limbs, each checked rather than taken from the comment:

1. **Nothing to scope.** The path names a lender, not a file, round or condition, so no file gate has
   an id to work with.
2. **The scope is in the query, not a check after it.** `get_scoped_lender` builds
   `select(Lender).where(Lender.id == …)` and applies `scope_to_company(...)` — the pattern LP-919's
   `get_scoped_round` docstring argues for.
3. **The company comes from the caller.** `read_condition_settings` takes `CurrentUser` and passes
   `current_user.company_id`; nothing tenant-bearing comes off the URL or the body, so there is no
   inversion to declare.
4. **It is pinned, and not vacuously.** `test_another_companys_lender_is_not_found` builds two files in
   two companies and asks as the *other* company's admin, getting 404 — not 403, so it is not an
   existence oracle. The write is admin-only by dependency, with 403 pinned for a processor.

Narrowing the walked router to just those two routes is right too: listing the whole lenders router
would demand an exemption for every unrelated route in it, which is how an allow-list stops meaning
anything.

### The Stage 2 flake is closed

`conditions-screens.test.tsx` ran **15 / 15 three times at file scope**, where my Stage 2 measurement
had it failing **4 out of 4**. The fixture was exactly 600,000 ms old against a strict `>`; it is now
eleven minutes. The whole frontend suite is **2124 / 2124** — the first fully green run in this stage,
and the reason every count before this one carried an asterisk.

### The note check: what it catches, and the one thing it does not

Probed with adversarial notes against a row's facts:

| Note | Result |
|---|---|
| invents an amount (`$9,999.00`) | **rejected** |
| invents a page count (`14 pages`) | **rejected** |
| reformats a real amount (`$2850` for `$2,850.00`) | rejected — a false positive that fails SAFE, since the code's note stays |
| restates a real asked amount, a real page count | accepted |
| **swaps required and verified** (`Verified $38,210.40 against $41,914.42 required`) | **accepted** |

The swap is the same re-attribution blindness found in LP-922's polish check: every number is present,
so a presence test passes while the sentence says the opposite. **Recorded, not fixed**, and
deliberately: `unsupported_numbers_in` is shared with the finding-prose path (LP-527), so changing its
semantics reaches well beyond this ticket. The exposure here is also smaller than the email's — the
code's note is the fallback, the row's own figures are on screen beside it, and she edits before
anything leaves. Worth a ticket, not a unilateral edit.
