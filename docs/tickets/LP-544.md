# LP-544 — AS-12 own-account transfers: NOT a defect. LP-516 already ruled, correctly.

**Superseded before it was built.** I opened this on 2026-08-18 believing AS-12's five findings on
LF-WCHG were false positives. They are not. LP-516 had already considered exempting
`txn.apparent_category: transfer_own` and rejected it, for a reason the new evidence confirms rather
than overturns.

## What I proposed, and why it was wrong

I proposed exempting a deposit categorised `transfer_own` — a transfer between the borrower's own
accounts — on the grounds that it is the same money and cannot be borrowed funds.

`test_an_own_account_transfer_is_not_exempt` (LP-516) already answers this:

> The guide's exemption is for a transfer between VERIFIED accounts, and nothing in the snapshot can
> establish that (LP-516-A2): `stmt.account_masked` is documented "display only, non-matchable" and a
> transaction's description has every 9+-digit identifier redacted, so neither side of the match
> survives. Exempting on the category alone would apply the guide's conclusion while dropping its
> condition — and on the file this came from the distinction is real: **those transfers arrive from a
> credit union whose statements appear nowhere in the file.**

My refinement was to exempt only when `txn.has_identified_source` is also `yes`, on the theory that a
named holder establishes ownership. It does not. **"Identified source" means the description says
where the money came from; it does not mean the sending account is documented.** Three of LF-WCHG's
five deposits have `has_identified_source: yes` and name Digital Federal — whose statements are not in
the file. Exempting them would have committed exactly the error LP-516 names.

## What the evidence actually showed

All five findings are tagged `transfer_own` (staging, 2026-08-18):

```
transfer_own  2000.0  has_source=yes  "Way2Save Savings … with the account holder …"
transfer_own  2000.0  has_source=yes  "Digital Federal A2A Xfers from Wells Fargo …"
transfer_own  2000.0  has_source=yes  "Digital Federal A2A Xfers from Wells Fargo …"
transfer_own  2000.0  has_source=no   "Digital Federal Credit Union savings … 0433"
transfer_own  3000.0  has_source=no   "Digital Federal Credit Union savings … 0433"
```

The model is confident these are own-account transfers. The file cannot corroborate it for any of
them. **AS-12 is behaving correctly**, and its composed text already says so: *"no matching withdrawal
appears in the documents on file."*

## What would actually unblock it

Not a category exemption. The guide's condition is that both accounts are **verified**, so the test is
whether the SENDING account is documented on this file — a statement present whose holder matches the
borrower and whose account matches the one the description names.

LP-516-A2 records why that is not available: account numbers are display-only and non-matchable, and
9+-digit identifiers are redacted out of transaction descriptions, so neither side of the match
survives. Revisit only if account matching becomes possible — that is a snapshot/extraction change,
not a rule change.

## The lesson worth keeping

The category tag was right, the file could not corroborate it, and the rule correctly declined to
clear. I read five findings that looked redundant and reached for an exemption without checking
whether the question had already been answered. It had been, in a test whose docstring named the exact
credit union.
