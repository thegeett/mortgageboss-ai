# LP-449 — Step A2: make the catch-all visible to cross-source reasoners — MEASURE FIRST

## What this ticket is

Every extraction captures fields the schema never asked for in `additional_sections`, and that catch-all is
**discarded at the snapshot boundary** (`documents_section.build_document_fields` skips `_CATCH_ALL_KEY`, line
275) — invisible to every rule and every reasoner. That boundary stranded valuable data three times (IH-1's
dwelling basis; nested lists until LP-444; the credit report's ID-risk/employer signals until LP-445). A2 was
scoped to surface it. **This ticket MEASURES the stranded surface post-LP-446 first, then decides whether a
general surfacing mechanism is worth building — or whether promoting a handful of fields per schema is the better
answer.**

### 2. The themed breakdown — what actually remains (real labels, PII-masked)

- **pay_stub (~21/doc, 149 distinct labels) — almost entirely NOISE or duplicated-by-the-LP-446-lists.** Tax
  withholding breakdowns (`Federal/Medicare/OASDI Taxable Wages Current/YTD`, `Additional Withholding`,
  `Work State Withholding`), PTO balances (`Paid Time Off Available/Accrued/Reduced`), direct-deposit **banking
  PII** (`Bank`, `Account Name`, `Account Number`), employee IDs / check numbers, company phone. The only
  income-shaped items (`Summary Totals :: Regular Income YTD / Other Income YTD / Net Pay YTD / Deductions YTD`)
  **duplicate the `earnings_lines` / `deduction_lines` LISTS LP-446 already added.** Nothing rule-worthy here is
  not already typed or listed.
- **homeowners_insurance (~24/doc) — a FEW rule-relevant fields, the rest contact/PII/marketing.** Rule-relevant:
  `Deductibles :: All-peril / Wind-hail deductible` and `Coverages :: Coverage B/C/D/E/F` (sub-limits) — **these
  are exactly Priya's OTHER adequacy conditions LP-447/ADR-340 deferred as named candidates (the deductible
  check; wind/hail → IH-8).** Noise/PII: `Named Insured Address/Phone/Email`, `Additional Insured — <name>
  Address` (real names), `Producer/Agent` block, `Customer Service` phones/websites, `Insurer Address`, premium
  breakdowns, discounts.
- **credit_report (~14/doc) — the rule-worthy ID-risk signals were ALREADY PROMOTED by LP-445.** What remains is
  negative confirmations (`No Fraud Alert on File`, `No Active Duty Alert`), `Score Navigator` potential scores,
  `VantageScore 4.0` models (not the qualifying score), and report metadata (`Instant View Password`,
  `Customer Number`, `Requested By`, `Infile Date <bureau>`).
- **bank_statement (~5/doc) — pure noise.** Customer-service phones, website, Spanish phone, mailing address,
  `Preferred Rewards Tier`, `Annual Percentage Yield`, `Interest Paid YTD`, check images.

### 3. Duplicate-of-typed-core share

Strict (value/label match): **~3% (11/429)**. But that UNDERSTATES it — the remaining catch-all is dominated by
content that is *semantically* redundant with the LP-446 lists (pay_stub `Summary Totals YTD` vs `earnings_lines`
/ `deduction_lines`) or with typed fields (insurance `Coverage A` vs `coverage_amount`). The non-redundant
remainder is mostly the **noise / PII / metadata** above.

### 4. ⚠️ THE VERDICT — PROMOTE a handful of fields per schema; do NOT build the general mechanism

**Recommendation: do NOT build Phase B (the general catch-all-surfacing mechanism). The evidence does not
justify it.**

- **The stranded surface collapsed.** The A2 premise (pay stubs stranding 57–78 rule-worthy fields) is obsolete
  post-LP-446 — 8 of 11 types strand nothing, and the three that remain are dominated by noise, PII, and
  content already captured by the LP-446 lists.
- **The genuinely useful residue is a SHORT, enumerable list that already has typed homes:** insurance
  deductibles / wind-hail / coverage sub-limits (the deductible check + IH-8 candidates LP-447 named); the last
  credit-report ID-risk bits (LP-445 already promoted the load-bearing ones). Typing these is strictly better —
  coerced, guarded, stable, and a rule can depend on them.
- **Geet's own cross-source examples were met by PROMOTION, not a mechanism:** the credit report's employer
  (corroborating the pay stub for IN-5) and the address-usage alert are **already typed** — LP-445 promoted
  `credit_report_current_employer` and `address_usage_alert`. The promote path already served the cross-source
  need three times (IH-1/LP-447, lists/LP-444, ID-risk/LP-445).
- **A general net would ship mostly noise + RAW PII to reasoners on every file** — real home addresses
  (`Additional Insured — <name> Address`), direct-deposit account numbers, report identifiers — pure token cost
  and a new PII surface, for signals better served by a few typed fields. (The catch-all is stored RAW; the
  measurement confirmed real addresses/account numbers sit in it.)
- **The standing caution cuts the same way:** surfacing reduces the pressure to type, and the evidence shows
  typing (LP-446) already captured the value. A safety net is not a substitute for schema work — and here the
  schema work is nearly done.

**So the outcome is "promote instead of surface."** Concretely, spin the residue into small typed-promotion spec
tickets (not this ticket): (a) the homeowners deductible + wind/hail + coverage sub-limits (feeds the deferred
deductible check / IH-8); (b) confirm no remaining credit-report ID-risk field is load-bearing beyond LP-445.
Nothing else measured is worth typing, and nothing measured is worth a general mechanism.

## PHASE B / C

**Not built.** Phase A's measurement retired the motivation: the mechanism is not warranted.

## Assumptions / decisions

- The measurement re-extracted live (the stored extractions predate LP-446, so they would overstate the
  stranded surface); 48/53 docs, the 5 skips having no extractor.
- Duplicate detection was conservative (exact value/label match) — the real semantic-duplication share is
  higher, which only strengthens "promote, don't surface."
- The verdict follows the ticket's explicit invitation: "promote instead of surface is a legitimate and
  possibly correct outcome — do not build a mechanism because the ticket describes one."

## NEW gaps / what remains

- **Two small typed-promotion candidates** (their own spec tickets): the homeowners deductible / wind-hail /
  coverage sub-limits (the deferred adequacy conditions), and a check that LP-445 promoted every load-bearing
  credit-report ID-risk field.
- **No ADR** — an ADR was required only if the mechanism were built. The decision NOT to build, and its
  evidence, lives here.
