# LP-641 — the rental-income citations point at the wrong topic

- **Status:** Done — and it was not mechanical. See *What the re-verification actually found*.
- **Date:** 2026-09-05
- **Trigger:** Fannie Mae announcement **SEL-2026-08**, dated 02 September 2026

## What moved

SEL-2026-08 restructured the rental-income material. **B3-3.8-01** is now *"General Rental Income
Information"*; the subject-property documentation table **and the 75% calculation** moved to
**B3-3.8-02**.

This is the third renumbering of this material in a year:

```
B3-3.1-08  →  B3-3.8-01  →  B3-3.8-01 (general) + B3-3.8-02 (documentation + 75%)
```

Which is the argument for fixing it properly rather than patching the strings: a citation that has
moved three times will move again.

## Verified against the live guide (2026-09-05)

Not taken on relay — fetched and confirmed, because this ticket's whole content is which number is
right:

| Topic | Title | Date | Holds |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B3-3.8-01** | General Rental Income Information | 09/02/2026 | the 12-month experience requirement |
| **B3-3.8-02** | Rental Income from the Subject Property | 09/02/2026 | **the 75% factor** and the Form 1007 / 1025 requirement |
| **B3-3.8-03** | Rental Income from the Subject Property: Short-Term Rental | 09/02/2026 | a separate treatment — see LP-642 |

The 75% wording at B3-3.8-02 reads *"multiply monthly gross rent by 75% for the net rental income
amount"*.

Worth noting for whoever does the sweep: `selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/**b3-3.1-08**/rental-income`
still resolves, and serves B3-3.8-01's content under the old path. So a stale citation does not
*look* broken when checked — it silently lands on the renumbered topic. That is why a citation being
followable is not evidence it is current, and why the two calibration entries need re-reading rather
than link-checking.

## Why two of these are more than cosmetic

`activation_bars.yaml:672` and `:719` cite B3-3.8-01 as the **calibration source** for the 75%
factor — the record of *why that number is trusted* rather than "pending Priya":

> THE 75% FACTOR IS NOW CALIBRATED, not "pending Priya": B3-3.8-01 (page dated 10/08/2025), verified
> verbatim…

A calibration record pointing at a topic that no longer contains the factor reads as verified and
is not. `IN-14.yaml:44` has the same property — it explicitly notes it *supersedes the former
B3-3.1-08 citation, which is stale numbering*, which is exactly the sentence that is now stale
itself.

So this is not a find-and-replace: each of those two needs re-verification against B3-3.8-02 as it
now reads, and the "verified verbatim" claim re-earned rather than carried forward.

## Also worth correcting while in here

`rental_treatment.py:43` quotes B3-3.8-01 verbatim for the 75% factor. Under the restructure that
quotation belongs to B3-3.8-02, and **the factor is path-specific in a way our comment does not
say**: 75% applies to lease and Form 1007 / 1025 market rents, and does **not** apply to the
Schedule E path, which adds back depreciation, interest, HOA dues, taxes and insurance to cash flow.

We apply `QUALIFYING_FACTOR` to whatever gross figure `_subject_gross_rent` returns. Today that
reads only the MISMO owned-property schedule, so a Schedule E figure cannot reach it — but LP-642
proposes widening exactly that lookup, and 75% applied to a Schedule E figure would be wrong
arithmetic. The comment should state the restriction before the lookup widens, not after.

## Proposed approach

1. Re-read B3-3.8-02 and re-verify the 75% factor verbatim; update the two `activation_bars.yaml`
   calibration entries with the new topic **and** a fresh page date.
2. Update the remaining eleven code/spec citations.
3. Add the path restriction (75% is for lease / 1007 / 1025, not Schedule E) to
   `rental_treatment.py`'s module note, beside `QUALIFYING_FACTOR`.
4. Leave the historical tickets alone.

A guard test asserting no source file cites `B3-3.1-08` or `B3-3.8-01` for the 75% factor would stop
the fourth renumbering costing a fourth sweep — worth considering, though a citation string is not
the kind of thing a test can verify is *correct*, only that it is not one of the known-stale ones.


## What the re-verification actually found (2026-09-05)

The ticket assumed a renumber. It was a **split**, and re-reading each citation rather than
rewriting the number changed three things a find-and-replace would have got wrong.

**1. One citation became two.** SEL-2026-08 did not move this material to a single new home:

| Fact | Now lives at |
|---|---|
| the 12-month experience requirement | **B3-3.8-01** General Rental Income Information |
| the lease-in-effect evidence (two months' statements, deposit + first month, management agreement) | **B3-3.8-01** |
| the 75% / ANRI factor | **B3-3.8-02** Rental Income from the Subject Property |
| the mandatory Form 1007 / 1025 | **B3-3.8-02** |

IN-14 and OC-3 each cited one topic for facts that are now in **both**, so each became two
citations. A number swap would have left each of them half wrong, and looking correct.

**2. The verbatim wording changed, so the quotations were re-read, not carried.** The factor now
reads: *"To determine adjusted net rental income (ANRI), the lender must multiply monthly gross rent
by 75% for the net rental income amount, then subtract the PITIA of the subject property from the
net rental income."* **ANRI** is new terminology that appears nowhere in this codebase.

**3. A cited claim was withdrawn.** Both calibration entries asserted, verbatim, that the remaining
25% is *"absorbed by vacancy losses and ongoing maintenance expenses"*. That sentence is in
**neither** restructured topic — B3-3.8-01 and B3-3.8-02 were checked separately. The 75% is still
cited; the 25% stands as arithmetic (100 − 75); the explanation is no longer claimed as guide text.

This is the failure mode a renumber cannot catch: **a claim can stop being true without its topic
number changing at all.**

## Scope note — one thing outside citations

The guard test found a third occurrence nobody had listed: IN-14's `how_to_fix`, which told a
*processor* that "the remaining 25% is absorbed by vacancy and maintenance" as flat fact. Not a
citation, so not on the ticket's list — but the same defect, in the copy someone acts on. The
guidance now says what it needs to ("qualifying income is 75% of the GROSS monthly rent, so the
document has to establish gross rent, not a net figure") without asserting a rationale we can no
longer source.

## The guard

`tests/verification/rules/test_rental_income_citations_lp641.py`, four tests: no live citation names
the superseded topic; the factor's own citation names the topic that holds it; the Schedule E
restriction survives an edit; the withdrawn rationale is never quoted without being marked withdrawn.

Its own docstring states the limit, because a green run must not read as more than it is: **a test
cannot verify a citation is correct** — that needs the guide, which is not in the repo. It verifies
no live citation names a topic already established as superseded, which is the failure that actually
happened.

`registry.py:337` is left as written: it records what LP-495b did, and rewriting it would falsify the
record. A forward pointer sits beside it noting the material has since moved again.
