## Why wiring was deferred (the fork Geet resolved)

Routing extracts only Tier-1 types, and `test_eighteen_tier_1_types` pins **exactly 18** Tier-1 types. Wiring any
generated module live requires promoting its type to Tier-1 and editing that count test — which the ticket forbids
("report rather than editing the test to fit"). Compounding it: **9 of 13 are Tier-3 (0–1 rules)** — wrong to
Tier-1-extract — and **11 of 13 are uncataloged** (new document types + classifier indicators, a taxonomy
decision). Geet chose **generate + report, don't wire**; place in the live tree. Verified safe: no
module-count/registration test exists, and the field-universe scanner tolerates the superset.
