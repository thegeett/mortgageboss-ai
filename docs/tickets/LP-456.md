## The two things a future reader must not miss

### ⚠️ Ruling 2 SUPERSEDES LP-393-6's `is_declining` framing
LP-393-6 recorded *"any YoY decrease = declining, no materiality threshold"*, applied at the **borrower level**
(the `income.is_declining` tag). Priya's August ruling **refines this**: decline is assessed **per component** —
a **component** decline → `NEEDS_REVIEW`; a **total qualifying income** decline is a **separate** result; *"do not
make 'any year-over-year decrease' an automatic failure."* The domain doc records **both**, with this one as
current and the LP-393-6 framing explicitly marked superseded. IN-10/IN-11 were scored under the superseded
framing (13/13 auto) — moving to per-component is a re-architecture + re-score, its own ticket.

### ⚠️ Ruling 4 requires a design decision no current mechanism supports
Agency thresholds are **not flat numbers** — Fannie / Freddie / FHA differ, and the answer is *store all agency
rules and select the applicable one; do NOT use the strictest globally; when the agency is not selected, return
comparative results; store a conservative lender choice as an explicit `LENDER_OVERLAY`, not disguised as agency
policy.* `activation_bars.yaml` holds **one threshold per rule and cannot express an agency-versioned rule with a
selector.** **Six rules (CR-9, DT-1, AS-3, PC-4, PR-1, CR-7's minimum) are gated on that DESIGN decision, not on
a number** — this is the evidence LP-455 Phase A surfaced, now recorded. It is E2's question.
