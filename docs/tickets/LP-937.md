# LP-937 — Superseded failures

- **Ticket:** LP-937 · Phase 4.5 Stage 3 follow-up
- **Source:** the product owner, 2026-09-29: "When a passing document has done an item, earlier failed
  checks for that item stop counting in 'Failed a check' and stop showing in the Next step token. They
  stay in the history and on the detail sheet as 'replaced by <document>'."
- **Closes:** the open item and decision row "A failed upload keeps saying so after a better document has
  done its item" (Stage 3B acceptance).

## Design

**Superseded, defined once.** A failed evidence row (checked, a check failed, not accepted) is superseded
when:
- its item is **done**; and
- another evidence row for the **same item**, which arrived no earlier, **passes**: accepted, or failing
  no check.

The passing row is "the document that replaced it". A failure on an item that is not done yet still
counts. So does a failure whose item another route closed (her "I'll do it" tick has no document to name).

**Two statements of the rule, pinned to agree:**
- `condition_evidence.superseded_by(row, item, rows)` in Python, used by the sheet;
- `conditions.has_failed_check()` in SQL, used by "Failed a check" and its filter. It gains a `NOT
  EXISTS` for the same condition.

A test builds the S3-12-then-S3-08 sequence and asserts both statements.

**What changes on screen:**
- **"Failed a check"** (the summary count and its filter) stops counting the condition. Nothing else in
  the bar moves.
- **Next step:** `ConditionEvidencePublic.failed` is false for a superseded row, so the list's first
  token ("Evidence failed a check — page 6") no longer fires. The client needs no rule of its own.
- **Detail sheet:** the card stays, dimmed, with its checks as they were, and a line in place of the
  re-ask / accept-anyway callout (`ConditionEvidencePublic.superseded`):
  - a rejected upload reads **"Replaced by Capital One statements ··9912 · July and August 2026 · 12
    pages"**;
  - a statement that failed only "Enough for closing" on its own is still evidence (it is in the
    package), so "replaced" would be false. It reads **"Enough for closing together with <document>"**.

  Its findings' buttons are not offered: the answer belongs on the document that is evidence.
- **History:** unchanged. The `condition_evidence_checked` event keeps its failed count, as the record
  of what happened when the document arrived.

**No migration.** Superseded is derived from rows that already exist, so it can never go stale.

## Review (of `770d2513`)

**One finding, and it is the one this ticket's own predecessor closed coming back through the other
door: a superseded card can hide the finding that is holding its condition.** (a) is recorded where the
owner will see it but says something arithmetically false; (b)'s equivalence argument holds and I
verified the premise it rests on; (d) is sound.

### (c) A superseded row that is STILL EVIDENCE can hold the condition, and the sheet hides why

`_settle` holds a condition on any open finding carried by a row that `counts_as_evidence`:

```python
held = any(_open_finding(e) for e in evidence if counts_as_evidence(e))
```

`counts_as_evidence` is true of a statement whose only failed check is "Enough for closing" — that is
the whole point of the "together with" branch: such a statement is still evidence and still goes in the
package. LP-937 now supersedes that same row, and `evidence-section.tsx:102` renders
`(superseded ? [] : evidence.findings)`. It is the **only** renderer of `.findings` in the sheet. So
the deposit that is holding the condition is on no screen and has no answer button.

This is not the Stage 3B case, which stays closed: there the ··4471 upload did **not** count as
evidence, so `held` excluded it. Here the row counts, holds, and is hidden.

**Measured, not argued.** July short on its own and carrying an unexplained $4,000.00 deposit; August
enough by itself and clean:

```
row july  checks: covers_required_funds failed ("verified $36,120.18 against $38,210.40 required")
          findings: [large_deposit 2026-07-21 $4,000.00, status open, needed True]
          counts_as_evidence: True    superseded_by: True
row august checks: all passed ("verified $41,914.42 against $38,210.40 required")
          findings: []                superseded_by: False

card superseded='Enough for closing together with Capital One statement ··9912 · August 2026 · 6 pages'
     findings rendered by the component: []          <-- the open deposit, hidden
prep_status: waiting    waiting_on: borrower         <-- and still held by it
```

The control is in the same file: with the shipped arrangement (the deposit on August, the row that is
**not** superseded) nothing is hidden and nothing is lost, which is why the five new tests are green.

**Ordinary, not contrived.** `_verified` takes the latest ending balance per account, not a sum, so a
later statement that is enough on its own passes "Enough for closing" and does the item; the earlier
one's stored covers check is never recomputed. The Stage 3B propagation does not rescue it: it answers
the same `(account, date, amount)` across the condition's statements, and this deposit is not on the
later statement.

**The two screens now contradict each other.** `nextStepToken` reads findings across *all* evidence
rows, superseded included (`lib/conditions/next-step.ts:167`), so the list still says **"Large deposit
needs sourcing"** in amber. She clicks it, and the sheet shows a done item, a dimmed "Enough for
closing together with …" card, no deposit anywhere, and Waiting on Borrower with nothing to answer.

**Reproduction:** `test_superseded_row_can_still_hold.py` (in my scratchpad; drop it in at
`backend/tests/conditions/`). Its first test fails on `770d2513` — that is the finding — and its second
is the positive control and passes. I have deliberately not left it in the tree, since a red test in a
shared worktree stops other sessions.

**The fix that follows from the ticket's own reasoning.** The card should hide findings on the
**"Replaced by"** branch only. The predicate is already written and already chosen once, for the
wording: a row that `counts_as_evidence` is evidence, its findings can hold the condition, so its
findings must stay visible and answerable. Drive it from a field the server sets (the service already
computes the distinction), not by reading the string on the client. `_settle` must not change — the
statement really is in the package, so the lender really will see the deposit.

### (a) The wording is recorded, and it is not true of the case it is asserted on

The departure from the owner's literal "replaced by <document>" is recorded in the decision register
where he will see it (`phase4.5-progress.md:546`), with its reason. Good.

But **"Enough for closing together with <document>" is false whenever the two rows are the same
account**, which is the case the ticket's own test asserts (··9912 July, ··9912 August). `_verified`
takes the **latest ending balance per account**, not a sum: the reasons the code itself wrote above are
`verified $36,120.18` for July alone and `verified $41,914.42` for August — August's own balance.
$78,034.60 never appears. August is enough *by itself*; July contributes nothing to the total and is
kept because the lender asked for two months, not because it adds funds.

"Together with" is exactly right in the case it was presumably written for — two **different accounts**
on one item, where `_verified` really does sum them. It is wrong in the single-account case.

Something like **"Still evidence — enough for closing was met by <document>"** is true in both. The
phrase is in the decision row, so changing it is the owner's call; the point is that the row currently
records a sentence the arithmetic does not support.

### (b) The mutants, including the three in the SQL half

Python half, both as claimed:

| mutation | result |
|---|---|
| `_takes` drops its `item.status not in _AWAITING` clause | kills `test_a_done_item_takes_no_new_evidence` |
| `superseded_by` drops `and _passes_alone(other)` | kills `test_the_sheet_names_the_document_that_passed_not_a_later_failure` |

SQL half, each run against 619 tests (`tests/conditions` + every `tests/api/*condition*`), anchors
verified by diff rather than by grep:

| mutation | result |
|---|---|
| drop `ConditionItem.status == DONE` | **killed** (`test_a_failure_stops_counting_once_a_passing_document_does_its_item`) |
| drop `later.created_at >= failed.created_at` | survives — declared equivalent |
| drop the `or_(ACCEPTED, no failed check)` | survives — declared equivalent |

**Both equivalence claims hold, and I checked the premise rather than the prose.** The argument is that
`_takes` refuses new evidence on a done item, so every failed row on a done item precedes the row that
did it. That premise needs no route to re-open a DONE item, and there is none: the only reset is the
new-round re-plan at `condition_plan.py:562`, which moves **REQUESTED and RECEIVED** back to OPEN and
leaves DONE alone. Given that, the row that did the item is always a witness with `created_at >=` every
failed row on it, so neither clause can change the answer. `test_a_done_item_takes_no_new_evidence` is
the right pin for it, and it dies under mutation — an invariant test that actually holds the invariant.

## Follow-up — the review's finding fixed (the builder's call)

**The finding:** a superseded row that is still evidence (it failed only "Enough for closing" on its own)
keeps its findings counting in `_settle`, while the sheet hid every superseded row's findings. So an
open deposit on it held the condition out of sight.

**The fix: one server field, `replaced`** (superseded AND no longer evidence), drives both readers:
- **The sheet** hides findings on replaced rows only. A superseded row that is still evidence keeps its
  deposit on screen, with its answer buttons.
- **The Next step** skips findings on replaced rows only. This is a third member of the same class,
  found while fixing the first: since LP-937 made a replaced row's `failed` false, the finding line was
  reachable for it, and it would have said "Large deposit needs sourcing" for a deposit `_settle`
  ignores.
- `_settle` is unchanged: a statement that is still evidence goes in the package, so the lender will see
  its deposit.

**The wording** is now "Still evidence — enough for closing was met by <document>". "Together with" was
untrue for two statements of one account: `_verified` takes the account's latest balance, so August met
the total by itself.

**Tests:**
- `test_the_deposit_holding_the_condition_stays_on_the_sheet` is the review's reproduction, adapted to
  read the sheet as the component now does. It asserts the premise (7086 held), the deposit shown, and
  that answering it there makes 7086 Ready.
- `evidence-section.test.tsx`: a still-evidence superseded card keeps its "Ask the borrower to explain
  it" button.
- `next-step.test.ts`: a replaced upload's deposit is skipped; one on a still-evidence row is not.

**Mutations**, each restored from a copy and killed:
- `replaced` always true (2 fail);
- the sheet hiding on `superseded` again, which is the shipped defect (1 fails);
- the Next step without its filter (1 fails).

**Records:** the review's open item is struck as fixed; the decision row and ADR-414 carry the new
wording; the review's second intermittent test (`test_the_reask_goes_into_a_new_borrower_email`) is
added to the pre-existing failures list.
