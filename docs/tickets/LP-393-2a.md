## What this ticket is

labeled on the occupation-LESS sheet. Her 7 `unknown`/`no_unknown` cells with the note "No occupation given"
answered *"can I judge this?"* (no), not *"is it the same line of work?"* — so scoring the fixed AI against them
measured nothing (the 38% → 31% was a FALSE regression). This regenerates the worksheet from the FIXED fixture
(occupation now in the context), PRESERVES every still-valid label + note, and BLANKS only the stale cells for a
short blind re-label round.
