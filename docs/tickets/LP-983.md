# LP-983 — Delivery events queue + KMS grant for SNS

- **Ticket:** LP-983, the third of the step-1 tickets in `loan-email.md` §15.
- **Status:** applied to staging (account `058190633983`, us-east-1) on 2026-10-20 (US Eastern); proven
  with two SES simulator sends. Receiving stays off.

## What and why

Every email we send already makes SES publish its result (delivered, bounced, complained, rejected)
to the SNS topic `mbai-staging-outbound-mail-events`. Nothing subscribed to that topic, so every
result was thrown away. Now an SQS queue (with a dead-letter queue) is subscribed, and the results
wait there for the worker to read them in step 2.

Our queues are encrypted with the shared customer managed key from `modules/secrets`. SNS can only
deliver to a queue encrypted that way if the key policy lets `sns.amazonaws.com` use the key, and it
did not (found in LP-981). The key policy now has that grant, limited to our own mail topics.

## What changed

- `infra/modules/secrets/variables.tf`: new `sns_topic_arns` (list, default empty).
- `infra/modules/secrets/main.tf`: new key-policy statement `AllowSNSDeliveryToEncryptedQueues`,
  present only when `sns_topic_arns` is non-empty, built like the SES statement.
- `infra/modules/outbound_mail/variables.tf`: new `kms_key_arn`, `message_retention_seconds` (default
  14 days) and `max_receive_count` (default 3).
- `infra/modules/outbound_mail/main.tf`: new `aws_sqs_queue.events_dlq`, `aws_sqs_queue.events`,
  `aws_sqs_queue_policy.events` and `aws_sns_topic_subscription.events_queue`, copied from
  `inbound_mail`'s queue pattern. The events topic also gets a comment on why it is not encrypted.
- `infra/modules/outbound_mail/outputs.tf`: new `events_queue_url`, `events_queue_arn`, `events_dlq_arn`.
- `infra/envs/staging/main.tf`: `module "secrets"` gets `sns_topic_arns`, built from names (reading
  them from the mail modules would be a cycle, as with `ses_receipt_rule_arns`); `module
  "outbound_mail"` gets `kms_key_arn = module.secrets.kms_key_arn`.
- `infra/envs/staging/outputs.tf`: new `outbound_events_queue_url` (null when outbound mail is off).

The two topic names in `sns_topic_arns` were checked against the resources:
`${var.name_prefix}-inbound-mail` (`inbound_mail`, `aws_sns_topic.this`) and
`${var.name_prefix}-outbound-mail-events` (`outbound_mail`, `aws_sns_topic.events`).

## Decisions

- **Events topic not encrypted; events queue encrypted.** The topic only passes events through, and
  an encrypted topic would need its own key grant for SES to publish to it. Events are stored only in
  the queue, and the queue uses the key. Written as a comment on `aws_sns_topic.events`.
- **`max_receive_count` is 3 for the events queue and stays 5 for the inbound queue.** Both values are
  intentional, as confirmed at the apply checkpoint: the events queue uses 3 as this brief specified,
  and the inbound queue keeps its existing 5.

**End to end.** Two sends with `aws sesv2 send-email` from `lp983-test@mail.staging.mortgageboss.ai`,
configuration set `mbai-staging-outbound`, to SES mailbox-simulator addresses only. Both events were
in the queue within about 11 seconds of sending (second long poll):

| eventType | destination | mail timestamp (UTC) | event timestamp (UTC) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Delivery` | `success@simulator.amazonses.com` | 2026-10-21T02:56:11.091Z | 2026-10-21T02:56:11.549Z |
| `Bounce` (`Permanent`) | `bounce@simulator.amazonses.com` | 2026-10-21T02:56:11.809Z | 2026-10-21T02:56:12.219Z |

- The DLQ was empty afterwards: `ApproximateNumberOfMessages` 0, `ApproximateNumberOfMessagesNotVisible` 0.
- The subscription is listed on the topic with the events queue as its endpoint.
- The events queue was purged (`aws sqs purge-queue`, exit 0) so the test events do not stay in it.

Delivery through a CMK-encrypted queue working is the evidence for the key grant. Before this ticket,
the key policy had no SNS statement, and the queue did not exist.

## What this does NOT do

- The app does not read the events queue yet. That is step 2.
- The inbound topic's ARN joins the SNS grant automatically when `inbound_mail_enabled` is switched on
  (LP-989). It is built into `sns_topic_arns` already, behind that flag.
- No alarms on the queue or the DLQ yet (LP-987).
- No change to the events topic, the configuration set, or what SES publishes.
