# LP-963 — The import bar sits above the rows, not at the bottom of the window

- **Ticket:** LP-963 (the owner, 2026-10-06)
- **Source:** the owner, on the conditions review screen: "Nothing is saved to the file until you import.
  Import never removes or clears a condition. this panel is very bottom of the screen, which is easily miss
  by user."

## What was wrong

The bar holding the two trust sentences, the "I checked the flagged rows" box, **Discard** and **Import N
conditions** was `fixed inset-x-0 bottom-0`, as the mocks drew it (S1-04, S1-07, S1-10, S1-11). In the app it
sat at the very bottom of the window, below the rows and across the sidebar and context rail. The cost of
missing it is more than the sentence: a processor who never sees Import can leave believing the conditions are
on the file when nothing was written.

## The change (`round-review.tsx`)

- **Placement, the owner's call.** The bar now comes after the round summary ("Review · not imported yet"), the
  "Just some" notice, "Check before importing" and the "This sheet may be for another file" warning, directly
  above the rows. She reads what the round is and whether it belongs to this file, then meets Import before the
  rows. I first put it above the summary; the owner moved it below the summary and the warning.
- **Pinned.** It is `sticky`, so it stays in view while she scrolls through the rows. `-top-4` cancels the
  file layout scroller's `p-4`, and an opaque `pt-4` fills that gap once the bar is stuck. `-mt-3` gives back
  the column's `gap-3` at rest.
- **Visible.** It is now a warning-tinted strip (`border-warning/40 bg-warning/10`) with the sentences at
  `text-sm font-medium text-foreground`, where before they were `text-xs text-muted-foreground`. The wording is
  unchanged.
- **The `pb-20` reserve** the root kept for the fixed footer is gone.

This departs from the mocks' sticky footer on purpose; the mocks are not updated.
