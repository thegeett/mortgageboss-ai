## Design

**The prompt** (`read_v3.txt`, `READING_VERSION = "read_v3"`):
- every condition's answer carries `clauses`: each clause of the lender's text copied exactly, with the key
  of the item that covers it or a `note` saying why it asks for nothing;
- for a condition WITH a library type, the type's items come first as before, and an item may be added after
  them, with a new key, for a clause they do not cover. The rule names the case: "possibly a Change of
  Circumstance" is an item for the LO.

**Code checks it** (`condition_reading.uncovered_conditionals`). For every conditional word in the lender's
text (`possibly`, `if applicable`, `and/or`: the owner's three, whole words, case-insensitive), the word is
covered when a clause the model quoted contains it, really is in the lender's text (a quote the lender never
wrote covers nothing), and names an item the reading has or carries a non-empty note. Anything else is
reported in the lender's own words.

**The reading.** An uncovered conditional caps the confidence just below the bar (`bar - 0.01`), so the
reading is `needs_confirmation` and nothing is drafted for it until she confirms (README rule 3). The words go
in `reading.uncovered`, which the reading box shows as "Not covered by an item yet: “…”". An added item never
replaces a library item (a key the type already has is ignored), and confirmed readings are left alone.

**The plan: the lender does the type's work, not every clause's.** UWM orders the final inspection (decision
1), so `build_plan` used to give EVERY item of 1228 "Lender is doing it". It now gives that only to the library
type's items. An added item keeps its own step: 1228's re-disclosure is an ask in the LO email. When the lender
is not doing all of a condition, its items carry their own steps (`next_step` is null) and the reason still says
"Ordered through the lender — confirm on your files". Two readers follow the items rather than the step:
- the package's "open prior-to-docs" sentence still says "the final inspection is ordered through the lender
  and not back yet" when the lender is doing any live item (it would otherwise have said "nothing has been
  asked for yet", which is false);
- the plan panel's "Lender is doing it" pill counts a condition the lender does part of.

**Draft side column order.** "Other drafts this round" is now listed by recipient (borrower, title/attorney,
LO, …) as S3-05 shows it, not by creation order. Which draft a plan creates first follows the sheet's order,
and LP-954 put 1228's LO ask ahead of the borrower's.
