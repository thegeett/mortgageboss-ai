## The three things stated honestly

### 1. THE SYNTHETIC-DATA CAVEAT — on every one of these bars

All four were measured on **CLEAN SCENARIO data** (the LP-393-1 fixture). That validates the AI's **REASONING**,
NOT its robustness to real-document messiness (OCR noise, odd layouts, missing/garbled fields). **The only
real-data check is LF-6T3N at n=2 — indicative, not validating.** Every rationale carries this verbatim.
**Proposed handling:** for the FN-dangerous auto rules (IN-10, IN-11, AS-11), the rationale recommends
**RATIFY-ONLY until real files confirm** (the IN-1 precedent's language) — Priya decides auto-vs-ratify. AS-11
carries an extra thin-n flag (n=6). IN-7 already ships ratify.

### 2. IN-11's 85% UNDERSTATES the tag (reported 85%, NOT rounded to 100%)

`measured_accuracy: 0.85` is recorded honestly. Both "misses" are **Priya applying documentation standards the
tag does NOT model**, not reasoning errors:

- **B14:** VOE contract ENDED 2026-06-30, two W-2s present. AI `yes` (history exists — the tag's question);
  Priya `no` ("currently unemployed… need a new offer letter + a paystub").
- **B12:** pay-stub-only, no W-2. AI `yes`; Priya wants a W-2/1099 check first.

**On the core question — is there two years of history — the AI is right BOTH times (100% on what the tag
models).** The proposed 0.90 bar sits ABOVE the measured 0.85; the entire 15-point gap is those two
documentation-standard rows. This is surfaced, not gamed: Priya decides the bar AND whether the documentation
standard belongs in this tag or a separate check.

### 3. B14 is an OPEN definitional question that GATES IN-11's bar

**"Does a TERMINATED job's two years still count as HISTORY?"** Priya answered `no` (reading continuation); the
tag asks history (which exists). **Her ruling changes what the tag MEANS on every file — and thus whether the
0.85 is effectively 100%.** Flagged in IN-11's rationale as a question she must settle WITH the bar. **Not
decided here.**
