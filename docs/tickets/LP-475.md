# LP-475 — fix ONLY the clear reclassification regressions

The last of the v2 remediation. LP-463 made declining legitimate (T4s, HOA budgets, portal screenshots are no
longer force-fit) — correctly — but the classifier now abstains where a **specialised type exists and its
extractor demonstrably works on a sibling**. LP-471 lowered the stakes (these fall back to Tier 3 and are read,
untyped). So the bar is narrow and evidence-backed: **a working extractor, proven on a sibling, and genuinely
that type — one document type at a time. No general loosening of the decline behaviour** (that would trade this
regression for the worse one — a T4 emitting plausible US W-2 numbers).

Triaged against the **stored v2 records** (`8c999b3f7a1b/_records.jsonl`) — the classifier's own reasoning + the
extractors' sibling results are the gate of record. No model calls in Phase A.

A regression qualifies **only if all three hold**: (1) the lost type has a **registered, working extractor**,
(2) **proven on a sibling** in this corpus, (3) the document is **genuinely that type** — not a package, not an
addendum, not a near-miss.

### A2 — the mechanism (positive cues only; no threshold / no `type_matches_document` change)

Both fixes are **positive indicator cues using the documents' own printed separators** (the LP-465/468
approach). The types already exist in catalog + indicator, so the catalog↔indicator CI guard stays satisfied
(text edit only, no type added/removed).

**`earnest_money_receipt`** — the classifier declined 208/209 because it read "Standard Form 2-T" as a generic
contract. The document's own heading is "ACKNOWLEDGMENT OF RECEIPT OF MONIES … receipt of the Initial Earnest
Money Deposit". Proposed:
- from: `"a receipt or canceled check evidencing the earnest-money deposit on the purchase"`
- to: `"a receipt, an escrow/agent ACKNOWLEDGMENT OF RECEIPT OF MONIES, or a canceled check evidencing the earnest-money deposit (and any due-diligence fee) on the purchase — a signed acknowledgment that the INITIAL earnest money deposit was received (e.g. a Standard Form 2-T receipt/acknowledgment page) IS this type, not a generic contract"`

**`title_commitment`** — the classifier declined 202 as a "bundled title-insurance set" (its lead page is the
invoice). Its siblings 201/203 (same invoice+CPL+commitment bundle, larger) classified fine. Proposed:
- from: `"a title insurance commitment listing vesting, liens, and exceptions to clear before closing"`
- to: `"a title insurance commitment or BINDER (ALTA) listing vesting, liens, and exceptions to clear before closing — carrying a Schedule A (vesting/legal) and Schedule B (exceptions); it counts even when bundled with the title INVOICE and a Closing Protection Letter (CPL), the commitment/binder being the lead document"`

Both cues are narrow and type-specific, so they cannot loosen declining generally: a T4, a compensation letter,
and a portal screenshot share none of these printed separators (verified as the Phase C force-fit acceptance
test).

### A3 — the near-misses, honestly

- **177/178 (lease addenda)** and **236 (occupancy email)**: the honest answer is **not** to force them into a
  neighbour. An addendum is not a lease; an email is not a formal LOE with a printed identity. Either they get
  a **new type** later (missing-types work) or they stay in **Tier 3**, which reads them. Forcing is the exact
  failure LP-463 fixed.
- **211 (evidence_of_payment)**: registered but never exercised in the corpus — we have **no evidence the
  extractor works**, so a cue would be unbacked. Leave declining.
- **204 / 196 (packages)**: the splitter's job, not classification.

**Sign-off (received):** proceed with the **3 qualifiers (202, 208, 209)**; **236 stays declining** —
confirmed. The reason 236 is excluded is worth keeping: it is an occupancy explanation **by content** but a
Gmail thread **by form**, so any cue tight enough to catch it must key on content, which is exactly how generic
correspondence gets pulled back in. Tier 3 reads its 16 occupancy facts. **Untyped beats a cue that risks
re-opening force-fitting** — the LP-471 principle earning its place: making the failure cheap is what lets a
marginal case be left alone.

## Phase B — the fix: two indicator cues, nothing else

**One file changed:** `backend/app/ai/classification_prompt.py` — two entries in `DOCUMENT_TYPE_INDICATORS`.
**Nothing else was touched:** no confidence threshold, no `type_matches_document`, no catalog entry, no
extractor, no rule. Both types already exist in catalog + indicator, so the catalog↔indicator parity guard is a
text-only edit (`tests/ai/test_classification_prompt.py` — 11 passed).

**`earnest_money_receipt`** (verbatim; the whole diff for this entry)
- **from:** `"a receipt or canceled check evidencing the earnest-money deposit on the purchase"`
- **to:** `"a receipt, an escrow/agent ACKNOWLEDGMENT OF RECEIPT OF MONIES, or a canceled check evidencing the earnest-money deposit (and any due-diligence fee) on the purchase — a signed acknowledgment that the INITIAL earnest money deposit was received (e.g. a Standard Form 2-T receipt/acknowledgment page) IS this type, not a generic contract"`

**`title_commitment`** (verbatim)
- **from:** `"a title insurance commitment listing vesting, liens, and exceptions to clear before closing"`
- **to:** `"a title insurance commitment or BINDER (ALTA) listing vesting, liens, and exceptions to clear before closing — carrying a Schedule A (vesting/legal) and Schedule B (exceptions); it counts even when bundled with the title INVOICE and a Closing Protection Letter (CPL), the commitment/binder being the lead document"`

Both name **printed separators the document itself carries** ("ACKNOWLEDGMENT OF RECEIPT OF MONIES";
"Schedule A/B"; "BINDER (ALTA)"). Neither says anything about confidence, abstention, or how sure the model
must be — so neither can loosen declining generally. The force-fit candidates carry none of these strings.

rather than dressed up as a bigger number.**

ticket, and the classification fix is what was asked for. (Note also that 201's `schedule_b_items` moved 3 → 9

## Assumptions and decisions
1. **The stored v2 records are the gate of record for triage** — the classifier's own `classification_reasoning`
   and the siblings' extraction results, not the ticket's prior labels. Phase A cost **$0** (no model calls).
2. **202's v2 tag was NOT stale** — v2 already had it at `unknown @ 0.85` with an explicit "bundled
   title-insurance document set" reason. It was a live decline, and the fix addresses that reason directly.
3. **Positive cues only.** Every alternative mechanism (threshold, `type_matches_document`, a generic
   "prefer a type when close" instruction) was rejected as the force-fit risk, per the ticket's constraint.
4. **A count-parity improvement is still an improvement** when it converts untyped items into rule-readable
   named fields — but it is reported as such rather than as a volume win (208/209).
5. **Extraction quality was not tuned.** Where the proof surfaced an extraction weakness (202's empty
   `schedule_b_items`), it is filed as a gap, not fixed — this ticket is classification only.

## Deliberately left declining — and why (the expected outcome)
`204` and `196` are **multi-document packages** → the **splitter's** job; classification cannot name a document
that is five documents. `177`/`178` are **lease addenda/renewals** — an addendum genuinely is not a lease.
`132` is a **portal screenshot** — the LP-463/470 correct decline. `211` fails triage rule 2: **zero** documents
in the whole corpus ever classified as `evidence_of_payment`, so its extractor is registered but **never
proven**, and a cue for it would be unbacked. `236` is the **near-miss** — an occupancy LOE by content, an
email by form. **All seven keep being read by Tier 3 (ADR-368/LP-471); leaving them conservative costs untyped
data, not all data.** The honest destination for a near-miss is a new type later or Tier 3 now — never a
neighbour.

**Open follow-ups filed by this ticket:** (a) `title_commitment` **Schedule-B list routing/stability** on
bundled files (202 empty, 201 varying 3→9); (b) the **splitter** for packages (204, 196) — the one change that
would recover the URLA package; (c) a possible **lease-addendum** type and a home for 236 (missing-types work);
(d) `evidence_of_payment` has **no corpus evidence at all** — it needs a proven example before any cue.

**ADR:** ADR-373 in `decisions.md` — the two-sided lesson (we measured declining's benefit, not its cost; the
remedy was to make the failure cheap, not to loosen the classifier) and the three-part triage rule.
