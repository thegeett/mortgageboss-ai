# LP-848 — a draft written before its template existed

Status: spec. Third of four.

## What the report was, and what it actually was

> "Looks non borrower parties still showing 'We are working through your loan file…'"
> "I am talking about new draft, it does not shows new changes."

Measured on staging: the lender and employer drafts on that file were created **2026-09-12
01:11:39 UTC**. LP-843 was committed at **03:39 UTC** — two and a half hours later. Staging confirms
it from the other side: no draft anywhere has ever carried `document_request_third_party`, and those
rows still record `template_version = v4`, which is the BORROWER template's version.

So a genuinely new party draft does get the new voice. The drafts in front of the processor predate
the fix. **That is not a reason to close this**, because the reason they predate it is a defect:

## Two real defects behind it

**1. A stored body is never corrected.** `_regenerate` runs only when documents are added or
removed, so an open draft composed before any template change keeps its old words forever. There is
no path by which those lender and employer drafts become right — a processor either notices and
deletes them, or sends the borrower's words to a lender.

**2. `_regenerate` updates the body and the version but NOT the `template_key`.** So when one of
those drafts IS regenerated, it becomes `document_request_lender` + `v1` — and no such template has
ever existed at any version. That is precisely the ADR-401 breakage LP-843 was written to fix,
reintroduced through the back door on exactly the rows most likely to hit it.

Defect 2 is the sharper one: LP-843 asserted that every party's **new** draft names a template that
resolves, and said nothing about a draft that already existed.

## The change

- `_regenerate` re-stamps `template_key` from the party, not only the version. Key and version are
  produced by one render and must be written by one statement.
- A stale open draft is brought current. The options are to regenerate on read, or to migrate the
  existing rows once. **Decided at build time, on one question: whether a processor may be reading a
  draft mid-edit when it is rewritten** — regeneration that discards an unsent edit would be a worse
  bug than the one being fixed.
- A test that a draft carrying a pre-LP-843 key and an old version does not stay that way.

## The class, not the instance

Any template change has this shape: new sends are fixed, open drafts are not, and nothing says so.
That is worth naming in the ticket rather than fixing generically — a version bump is rare enough
that a per-bump decision is honest, and a generic "regenerate everything on deploy" would throw away
processor edits.
