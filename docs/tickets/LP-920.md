## Design

**Migration** `6d517ee261d7` (raw SQL, the DDL `create_all` prints, so every constraint has the model's
name):

- `condition_items` — one row per item: `key`, `name`, `acceptable`, `performer` (+ `performers` for
  "Borrower + LO"), `option` (`PlanOption`), `status` (`open`, `requested`, `received`, `done`,
  `not_needed`), `origin` (`reading`, `manual`, `carried`), `documents`, `checks`, `specifics` (NPI),
  `need_id`, `document_id` + `document_page`, `waits_on_condition_id`, `due_date`, `sequence`. Excluded
  whole from the readonly layer (`EXCLUDED_TABLES`).
- `conditions.next_step` (+ CHECK) and `conditions.plan_reason`.
- `condition_rounds.plan_ready_at`, `plan_confirmed_at`, `plan_confirmed_by_user_id`.
- `loan_files.lender_processing`; `lenders.condition_settings`.
- Event kinds `condition_planned`, `condition_plan_changed`, `round_plan_confirmed` (24 listed); activity
  types `condition_plan_ready`, `condition_plan_confirmed` (36 listed).

**Service** `app/services/condition_plan.py`: `build_plan` (run by the reading task straight after the
reading), `carry_plan` (called when a reworded pair is resolved as the same condition), her edits
(`set_next_step`, `update_item`, `add_item`, `remove_item`), `set_lender_processing`, `confirm_round_plan`,
and the read side (`items_public_for_file`, `round_plan_summary`, `recipient_for`),
`lender_condition_settings` (stored settings or the canonical lender's defaults).

**Endpoints:** `GET /condition-rounds/{id}/plan`, `POST /condition-rounds/{id}/plan/confirm` (409 with
"Confirm 0132's reading first — one condition still needs you."), `PUT /conditions/{id}/next-step`,
`POST /conditions/{id}/items`, `PATCH`/`DELETE /conditions/{id}/items/{item_id}` (the item is looked up
under the tenant-scoped condition), `PUT /loan-files/{id}/lender-processing`. `ConditionPublic` gains
`next_step`, `plan_reason` and `items` (loaded once per file for the list).

**Library:** invoice items gain `match_words` ("credit", "processing", "inspection"), so "already in the
file" tells a credit invoice from a processing invoice of the same document type.

**Frontend:** `RoundPlanPanel` (S3-02) under the round strip while the newest round's plan is
unconfirmed; the detail sheet's items come from the plan once it exists, with "Same statement as … —
asked for once", "Waits on", "Already in the file" and "Add an item" (S3-01).

**Reuses:** the needs model and `create_needs_item` (origin `CONDITION`, reserved since LP-68), the
activity log, the library, the reading, business days from `app/conditions/facts.py`.

**Fixtures:** the test and the harness add one fictional document record, "Credit invoice 07/15".

## How the plan is built (per condition, in order)

1. **Whole-condition step:** a push-back the reading found (6178) → `push_back`, items `not_needed`,
   "Reason from the letter"; a PA-03 type at a lender that orders the final inspection (UWM) →
   `lender_doing_it`, "Ordered through the lender — confirm on your files" (decision 1); the reading's
   `lender_doing_it` or a `lender_to_clear` heading → `lender_doing_it`; information only.
2. **Item options** from the reading, then the lender's Processor Assist setting and the file's switch
   turn third-party asks into `lender_doing_it`.
3. **Already in the file:** an "I'll do it" item whose document type AND library words match a document
   the file holds → `already_in_file`, linked, page 1, done; "Found: Credit invoice 07/15, page 1".
4. **Waits on:** IV-03 waits on PA-03 → 0007's items point at 1228; "Waits on 1228".
5. **Reasons:** shortfall → "Shortfall computed by code"; TI-04 → "Added to the title email"; a reading
   that needs her → "Please confirm how we read this" (wins).
6. **Needs:** asks with documents get a need; same (who, document, account) share one; a borrower
   statement item with no account joins the round's single account; open needs for the same document
   are reused. Asks are due 4 business days after the plan is made (08/28 → 09/03).
7. **Events and activity:** `condition_planned` per condition; the round's `plan_ready_at` and "Plan ready
   for round 1: 11 conditions".

## Decisions

1. **Due dates** are set only on asks (4 business days); tasks and lender items carry none. The plan's
   "worked back from the lender's dates" beyond that (lock expiry, close-by) is left to LP-923/925, which
   have the expiry checks; recorded, not built.
2. **Push-back items are kept as `not_needed`**, not deleted, so the history shows what the condition
   would have asked.
3. **"Review one by one"** opens the detail sheet on the first row; each row's code also opens its sheet.
4. **Recipients:** title and attorney share one email (S3-05); an item with the LO among its performers
   goes in the LO email (0132's disclosure).
