# LP-959 — The upload card names no lender

- **Ticket:** LP-959 · Phase 4.5 Stage 3 follow-ups (the owner, 2026-10-03)
- **Source:** the owner asked whether condition PDFs are accepted only from UWM and Champions, because the
  upload card named them.

## What was wrong

The card read "The lender's PDF (UWM 'Loan Approval Conditions', Champions 'Conditional Approval
Certificate'). We read every condition plus the lender team, loan figures and document-expiry dates."

- **Any lender's sheet is accepted.** `readers/detect.py` sends UWM and Champions layouts to their own rule
  readers and everything else to the generic reader. The generic reader splits on numbering, codes or
  paragraphs, with the AI splitting only as a fallback and every row checked to be a substring of the PDF.
- **The extras are read only for UWM and Champions.** The lender team, loan figures and expiry table come
  from those two readers. For any other lender the sentence promised what we do not do.

## The change

`conditions-empty.tsx` now reads:

> **Upload the lender's conditions**
> Upload the conditional approval or condition sheet PDF you received from the lender. We pull out each
> condition and set it up for you to review. Nothing is added to the file until you confirm it.

The owner chose this wording. "Nothing is added until you confirm it" holds: an upload makes a draft round,
and its conditions are created only on Import.

## Tests

- `conditions-dashboard.test.tsx`: a new test checks the card's two sentences and that it names no lender and
  none of the UWM/Champions-only extras.
- Two tests that looked up the old heading now use the new one.
- Mutation: adding "UWM" back into the copy fails the new test.
- Runs: the conditions component suite, 194 / 194 plus the dashboard file; tsc and biome clean.
