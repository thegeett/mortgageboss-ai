# LP-433 — Write the pay-stub-only documentation rule (IN-16)

## What this ticket is

Priya's **B12 ruling** (LP-393-6): *"W-2 or 1099 is required to show 2 year history."* Her LP-393-6 ruling carved
a documentation standard out of the `has_2yr_history` judgment — whether 2 years EXIST is IN-11's concern, and
whether the history is adequately DOCUMENTED is a **separate check**. This builds that check as **IN-16**, the
sibling of IN-15's B14 terminated-employment rule (LP-430). LP-432 scoped it `write-now` (not a duplicate, both
document types exist, deterministic). It is deterministic (a document-type presence read → ADR-334, no
calibration) and **activates (ACTIVE 35 → 36)**. **This is the last rule reachable without new document
capability** (LP-432).

## D1 — the trigger scope (Option 1, flagged for Priya)

Implemented **Option 1** (the LP-432 recommendation, the ADR-339 flag-the-qualifier pattern): fire when a
borrower has pay-stub income and **no W-2 and no 1099** — pure document-type presence, so `no-ai-dependency`,
deterministic, activates immediately. **Flagged in the bar rationale:** it may **over-fire** on a borrower
documented another way (e.g. a VOE) who is not relying on pay stubs for history; narrowing to Option 2 (gate on
`income.has_2yr_history`) is a one-line applicability change, but makes it AI-dependent (inherits IN-11's
calibration) — deferred to her confirmation. **Also flagged for her:** whether a VOE should ALSO satisfy — she
said "W-2 or 1099", so IN-16 is **not** broadened to accept a VOE (a VOE-only borrower is `not_applicable`, not
satisfied). The over-fire risk stayed **theoretical** on the fixtures (no VOE-documented borrower fired), so no
ADR was warranted.

## D2 — fail closed; the reason asks for the document

| case | verdict |
|---|---|
| pay-stub income, **no W-2 and no 1099** | **`fired`** — *"this borrower's income is evidenced only by pay stubs — a W-2 or 1099 is needed to establish the two-year income history"* (asks for the document, never accuses the borrower) |
| a W-2 **or** a 1099 attributed | `satisfied` |
| **no pay stubs** (no income docs, or a VOE only) | `not_applicable` — never a finding on an empty set |
| the documents cannot be read | `couldnt_check` (the gate) |

## D4 — the producer

A derived per-borrower enum **`income.history_documentation`** (tags describe, rules judge — ADR-324):
- `w2_or_1099` — a W-2 or 1099 is attributed → **satisfied**.
- `pay_stub_only` — pay stubs attributed, no W-2/1099 → **fired**.
- `no_pay_stubs` — no pay stubs attributed (no pay-stub-only history to challenge — the `not_applicable`-enabler,
  the AS-8/LP-407-2 lesson that an enum can express what a number cannot) → **not_applicable**.
- `unknown` — the documents cannot be read → **couldnt_check** (fail-closed).

It reads **document-type PRESENCE** (`w2` / `1099` / `pay_stub`) over the borrower's attributed documents — not
extracted 1099 fields (the IN-8/IN-9 discipline), so the unvalidated `form_1099` extractor does not narrow the
ruling to W-2-only. No tags, no AI, no threshold — simpler even than IN-15.

## D5 — the subject match

## D6 — the boundary (observed on the same borrower, not assumed)

On the pay-stub-only borrower B1 (real reasoner), and — the canonical case — Priya's **labelled B12 borrower**
from the income-scenario fixture:

| rule | on B12 (Priya's pay-stub-only borrower) | why distinct |
|---|---|---|
| **IN-6** | **`not_applicable`** — *"income.employer_coverage ne 'one_sided' is false"* | confirms LP-432's prediction: `one_sided` (lacks a document type) scopes IN-6 OUT — it is about employer-NAME coverage across two present types |
| **IN-11** | **`satisfied`** — *"the income has an adequate (2-year) history"* | judges whether 2 years EXIST (they do, per her B12 relabel) — a different question |
| **IN-16** | **`fired`** — *"only pay stubs — a W-2 or 1099 is needed"* | judges the DOCUMENTS that establish the history — her separate check |

This is exactly her B12 ruling in action: **the history exists (IN-11 satisfied), but the documentation standard
is a SEPARATE check (IN-16 fires)** — complementary, never a duplicate finding. The three read **disjoint**
load-bearing tags (`income.employer_coverage` / `income.has_2yr_history` / `income.history_documentation`).
**IN-6/IN-11/IN-15 unchanged.**
