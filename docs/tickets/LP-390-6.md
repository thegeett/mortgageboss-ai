# LP-390-6 — Propose the activation bar for `has_identified_source`

## What this ticket is

LP-390-5 measured `txn.has_identified_source` at **93.8% (15/16, n=16)** — the one income-wave tag that
cleanly passed. This proposes its activation bar for Priya's sign-off (like LP-380 did for IN-1/IN-5), with the
accuracy, n, the FP/FN asymmetry, and the memo-richness caveat. It **sets no bar** (Priya's) and **activates
nothing** (LP-390-7). The other measured tags are HELD, each with its reason.

## Phase 0 — the gate of record reshaped the ticket

The ticket assumed `has_identified_source`'s consumer could be flipped to `status: calibratable-now`. The specs
say otherwise, and the honest proposal is different.

### Where `has_identified_source` is a load-bearing (gated) tag

| Rule | Status | Its role | Other load-bearing AI tags |
|------|--------|----------|----------------------------|
| **AS-1** | **LIVE (base-active)** | the fail-closed sourcing gate — `fired` = `has_identified_source != yes` AND `amount > threshold` | `txn.is_money_in`; `txn.amount` (parsed). NOT `apparent_category`. |
| **AS-2** | inert, auto | `gated_tags: [has_identified_source]` — the fail-closed gate | **`txn.apparent_category`** (load-bearing, UNGATED) |
| **AS-12** | inert, ratify | load-bearing | **`txn.apparent_category`** |
| AS-5 | inert, auto | **NOT load-bearing** — AS-5 gates only `apparent_category == gift` | — (`fact_tags.csv` over-lists AS-5 as a consumer) |

### The three findings

**1. No inert rule reads `has_identified_source` alone — so none can honestly be `calibratable-now`.** Both
inert consumers (AS-2, AS-12) also read `apparent_category`, which is **still unmeasured** (relabel-blocked,
LP-390-5). The activation-bar mechanism is **per-rule**: `is_eligible` needs one `measured_accuracy >=
threshold`, and the yaml's own definition of `calibratable-now` is *"every load-bearing AI tag has a measured
accuracy."* One passing tag out of two does not meet it. Flipping AS-2 to `calibratable-now` would also break
the mechanism's own test (`by["calibratable-now"] == 3`) — the honest state keeps it at 3 (IN-1/IN-5/IN-3).

AS-2 is the sharp case: `apparent_category` is **ungated**, so a wrong category makes AS-2 fall through to
`satisfied` (default) — it **silently misses** a loan-proceeds EMD. That is the dangerous FN, and it rests on
the unmeasured tag. So AS-2's trustworthiness genuinely gates on `apparent_category` too; it cannot ship auto
on `has_identified_source` alone.

**2. `has_identified_source`'s largest consumer, AS-1, is already LIVE** — and has been shipping an auto
verdict gated on this AI tag with **no prior measurement**. LP-390-5's 93.8% is the first evidence supporting
AS-1's live use. AS-1 has no bar entry (the loader covers only non-base rules), so this is **documented here,
not encoded** — and it is the strongest argument that `has_identified_source` is trustworthy: the tag the whole
large-deposit sweep already depends on now measures well.

**3. FP/FN — confirmed safe.** AS-1/AS-2 are FN-dangerous: a **missed unsourced deposit** (AI says
`yes`/sourced when it isn't) lets an undisclosed source through. LP-390-5's single miss was the **safe
direction** — AI said `no`/unsourced where Priya said `yes` (a generic `INBOUND PAYMENT RECEIVED` memo the AI
could not source; Priya knew the counterparty from outside the memo). **No dangerous-direction miss** (AI
`yes` where Priya `no`) appeared in the 16. An AI that under-claims "sourced" over-flags for human review —
the conservative, safe error for a source-verification rule.

## Phase 1 — the proposed bar (recorded, `validated:false`)

Because no inert rule is fully calibratable on `has_identified_source` alone, the bar is recorded as the
**prose proposal + measurement** on its consumers, not a false `calibratable-now`/`threshold` (the loader
forbids a `threshold` on a not-calibratable rule, and it would falsely read as *met*):

- **AS-2** (auto) — rationale now records: `has_identified_source` MEASURED 93.8% (n=16), safe-direction miss,
  the memo caveat, and the **PROPOSED bar for Priya: ~0.90 auto** (over-flagging is safe) **or ratify-only**
  while n=16 is one file. Status stays `not-calibratable-yet` — **advanced from both-tags-unmeasured to
  one-measured-and-passing, blocked only on `apparent_category`** (the LP-379-F relabel).
- **AS-12** (ratify) — `load_bearing_ai_tags` **fixed**: dropped `txn.counterparty` (the spec's
  `load_bearing_tags` is `[apparent_category, has_identified_source]`; counterparty is `reasoned_over` context
  only, and an orphan with no producer). Judgmental → ships ratify (a human ratifies every verdict, LP-376-B),
  so 93.8% is more than enough for the tag to *surface* for ratification; still blocked on `apparent_category`
  for sound category facts.
- **No `validated` flipped, nothing activated.** `is_eligible` unchanged; `eligible_rule_ids()` unchanged
  (`AS-9, AS-10, ID-5, IN-1, IN-4, IN-5`); `ACTIVE_RULE_IDS` unchanged (**17**).

## Phase 2 — tests + equivalence

- **The bar loads** (LP-380's loader accepts every edit): 23 bars, statuses unchanged
  (`calibratable-now: 3`, `not-calibratable-yet: 14`, `needs-producer: 1`, `no-ai-dependency: 5`); all
  `validated:false` except the pre-existing IN-1/IN-5.
- **Equivalence:** nothing activated; `eligible_rule_ids()` and `ACTIVE_RULE_IDS` (17) unchanged; full suite
  green. The edits are rationale/documentation + one load-bearing-list correction (AS-12) — no status,
  threshold, or `validated` change, so the activation gate is byte-identical.

## What Priya needs to confirm

1. **The `has_identified_source` bar height** — the proposed ~0.90, and **auto vs ratify-only** for AS-2 given
   n=16 is one file. (The safe-direction miss + AS-1's already-live dependence argue a moderate auto bar is
   defensible; the thin n argues ratify-only first. Her call.)
2. That **below-bar / `unknown` → `needs_review`** (LP-376-B) is the right routing for the memo-richness

3. Once `apparent_category` is relabeled + scored, AS-2/AS-12 become genuinely calibratable — the bar recorded
   here is what applies then.

## Assumptions, decisions & gaps

- **No new ADR.** The governing principle — a per-rule bar requires *all* the rule's load-bearing AI tags
  measured, so a single passing tag records a proposal but does not flip `calibratable-now` — is the existing
  LP-380 mechanism (per-rule `measured_accuracy`; the yaml's `calibratable-now` definition), applied honestly.
  This ticket is that application + the report, not a new architectural decision.
- **Deviation from the ticket, on purpose:** the ticket's "status calibratable-now" does not hold against the
  gate of record (AS-2/AS-12 read an unmeasured second tag). Encoded as the honest `not-calibratable-yet` +
  the proposed bar in prose, flagged for Priya — the ticket's intent (a decision surface, `validated:false`,
  no activation) preserved.
- **A reported spec/registry discrepancy:** `fact_tags.csv` lists AS-5 as a consumer of
  `has_identified_source`, but AS-5's spec gates only `apparent_category == gift` — has_identified_source is
  not load-bearing for AS-5. Left as-is (the tag→rule reference is documentary, not the load-bearing gate);
  noted for a fact_tags cleanup.
- **Never guessed a bar height** — 0.90 is a *proposed default* flagged for Priya, exactly like LP-380's
  IN-1/IN-5 defaults; she sets the real height and the auto-vs-ratify call.
