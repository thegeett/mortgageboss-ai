# LP-969 — Add a step, and "I'll handle it my own way"

- **Ticket:** LP-969 (the owner, 2026-10-08). Series LP-967 to LP-973; the overview is in `LP-967.md`.
- **Source:** the owner: "we have to provide a free way to processor, because what if it require upload
  thing, write draft, or something else." Agreed design: the "Plan screen explained" artifact, board "The
  plan as a path".
- **Status:** to build.

## What it adds

- **"Add a step"** on every path, with these building blocks:
  - write an email (to anyone; a blank or suggested draft);
  - ask someone to upload (an upload link);
  - upload it myself;
  - check a document;
  - ask the underwriter;
  - something else (a to-do in her words, with Mark done).
- **"I'll handle it my own way"** as the last route on every condition: the path starts empty, and the app
  still sends the emails, watches for replies and checks documents for each step she adds.

## It also closes the push-back gap

After LP-966's review a task cannot carry push back or ask the underwriter, because the server reads those
only from the condition. The whole-condition select appears only where the app proposed one. "Ask the
underwriter" and "push back" become steps she can add to any condition.

## What exists underneath

- **Exist:** drafts to any recipient (`email_draft`); upload links (`upload_links`); evidence checks;
  underwriter drafts.
- **New:** an item created by her with a free-text task, and a step order.

## Done when

- Each building block creates a real step that does what it says.
- "My own way" starts empty.
- Every addition is recorded in the condition's history.
- Peer review.
