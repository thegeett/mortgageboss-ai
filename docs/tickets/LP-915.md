# LP-915 — Round comparison: "What changed in round N" (S2-06, S2-07, S2-08, S2-10)

**Goal:** when a new sheet is imported, show exactly what changed, and let her confirm what cleared in
one click.

Built in three parts, in the order the dependencies allow: the engine that computes and saves a
comparison, the three doors that act on it, then the screens that draw it.

## Part 1 — the engine, and the column it is saved in

`app/services/condition_compare.py` compares an imported round against **everything open before it**
(A6: lender status `open` or `not_cleared`, origin `sheet`, not superseded) — never against the previous
round alone, because a condition can skip a sheet and return, and comparing round 3 with round 2 would
call such a condition "new".

**It is computed once and saved,** which is the spec's own requirement rather than an optimisation:
"opening the panel later shows the same result". `condition_rounds.comparison` (JSONB, migration
`a7c31e6d94b2`) holds the five outcomes, the letter changes and the reworded pairs, and `compare_round`
is wired into `import_round` for every round N >= 2.

**`comparison` is NPI and the readonly layer gets a boolean.** Most of the blob is analytic — codes,
counts, ids, dates — but `letter_changes` carries verified income, verified assets and the housing/debt
ratios, derived from `header`, which LP-904 already dropped from `readonly.condition_rounds`. A JSONB
column cannot be half-exposed, so the view gained `(comparison IS NOT NULL) AS has_comparison` and
nothing else. Deliberately **not** `jsonb_array_length(comparison -> 'probably_cleared')`:
`style_profiles` made exactly that trade and recorded choosing the guarantee over the metric, because a
derived scalar still names the column in the select list.

**It stores ids, never the lender's wording.** S2-08 draws the reworded pair's old and new text, but the
screen already holds both conditions and resolves them itself.

**Nothing here decides anything.** "Probably cleared" is a suggestion with a button;
`confirm_probably_cleared` is what turns one into a recorded verdict (ADR-404).

## Part 2 — the three doors, and the guard that arrived with its producer

| Endpoint | What it does |
|---|---|
| `POST /condition-rounds/{id}/confirm-cleared` | Records the **ticked** suggestions as `{cleared, round_comparison, source_date: the sheet's date, round_id}` |
| `POST /condition-rounds/{id}/reworded` | Answers S2-08's *Same condition* / *Different conditions* for one pair |
| `PUT /condition-rounds/{id}/completeness` | A7's switch between *Full list* and *Just some* |

All three refuse with `RoundComparisonRefused` -> 409 carrying `exc.reason`, the same shape as
`RoundNotImportable` and its four siblings: the round exists and the caller may see it, and it is the
round's STATE that refuses.

### `condition_was_replaced` — the item LP-916's review handed forward

LP-916's review asked for "refuse verdicts and moves on a Replaced condition, adding the guard **with
the producer**". Until this ticket nothing wrote `superseded_by_id` — `app/schemas/condition.py` said so
in as many words ("ONE field below has **no producer yet**") — so the guard would have defended a state
nothing could reach.

Both now land together. `_guard_not_replaced` is applied to **all four** writes (`move_prep_status`,
`record_verdict`, `reopen`, `set_owner`), with a ninth refusal code and the sentence *"This condition
was replaced by a later one. Open the condition that replaced it instead."*

- **It checks the column, not the status.** `superseded_by_id` names the successor; `lender_status ==
  superseded` is a rendering of it. A row could carry one without the other, and the pointer is the one
  that makes the sentence answerable. `test_the_replaced_guard_reads_the_pointer_and_outranks_nothing_to_reopen`
  pins this by setting the pointer while leaving the status `open` — a guard written against the status
  would pass every other test in the file and let that write through.
- **In `reopen` it sits ABOVE the `_REOPENABLE` check,** because `superseded` is already outside that
  set: without the ordering the refusal would read "there is nothing to reopen", which is true and does
  not say where the work went.
- **All four, not just the verdict.** The verdict is the dangerous one, but a prep move on a row the
  list shows collapsed under "Replaced" is invisible work, and an owner there keeps the old row in the
  Owner filter's Title group after everyone moved on.
- Adding a code costs four places, all guarded by tests: the enum, its sentence map, the refusal census
  (`known_elsewhere` is still empty — every one of the nine is triggered by a request in that file), and
  the mirrored `ConditionRefusalCode` TS union. The bulk bar's short form is "replaced by a later
  condition".

### `pending_suggestions` had no producer for three tickets

`ConditionSummary.pending_suggestions` shipped as a hardcoded `0`, honestly labelled *"0 until LP-915
writes one"*, with the schema field, the route and a `== 0` assertion all already in place. It is a
ROUND-level fact — nothing on a condition row says "round 2 thinks this cleared", and all five rows are
plainly `open` — so `summarise_conditions`, which is pure over conditions, cannot compute it. It is now
passed in from `pending_suggestion_count`, as a **required** keyword: a default here is exactly how
`condition_count` came to read "0 on sheet" for as long as anyone looked.

A producer added without a test asserting a NON-zero value would have looked identical from every
existing test, because 0 is a valid count. So
`test_the_summary_counts_pending_suggestions_until_they_are_answered` asserts 5, then 0 after a confirm
— and picks up §7.2's other Done-when, "the summary becomes Open 6 · Cleared 5", on the way.

### A stale confirm would have withdrawn every suggestion and recorded nothing

Found while writing the endpoint, not by a test. `confirm_probably_cleared` filters the request to ids
the round actually suggested and then resolves the round's suggestions **either way** — so a request
whose ids were all already resolved (a double press, a second tab, a reload after someone else
confirmed) would have emptied the pending list while writing no verdict: the questions lost without one
being answered. It is now refused, and the test asserts the pending list is still there afterwards.
`ConfirmClearedRequest.condition_ids` also requires at least one id, so the empty-body form of the same
mistake cannot be sent; *Not now* sends no request at all.

## Part 3 — the screens

`round-comparison-panel.tsx` (S2-06, S2-07, S2-08, S2-10), mounted above the summary bar in
`conditions-list-view.tsx` and dismissable per round, plus:

- **The round card's "5 probably cleared — review"** (`round-strip.tsx`), read from the round's own
  comparison. A round with nothing pending carries no mark, which is S2-10's card.
- **The list row's "Probably cleared in round 2 — review"** (`conditions-list.tsx`), beside a status
  column that still reads Open — which is the whole distinction between a suggestion and a verdict.
- **The detail sheet's pending-suggestion block and Replaced link** (`condition-detail-sheet.tsx`),
  which LP-916 left out for the right reason and explicitly handed here: both were branches nobody
  could reach until this ticket wrote the producers. The spec asks for the first by name ("the
  suggestion is per condition, so she can also confirm one from the detail sheet").
- **A replaced condition's writes are suppressed on that sheet.** Found while reading it: it rendered
  live status and owner selects and a "Record lender's answer" button, every one of which the server now
  refuses with `condition_was_replaced`. Four controls whose only possible outcome is a 409 are the same
  dead-branch trade LP-916 refused; what replaces them is the link to the row that does take the work.

`RoundComparisonPublic` is a typed nested schema following `ParseReportPublic`, not a raw
`dict[str, Any]`: `reworded` is stored as `[[old, new]]`, a shape no client should be left to interpret.
Exposing it adds no NPI this response did not already carry — `ConditionRoundPublic.header`, which
`letter_changes` is derived from, is already served whole to the authenticated processor, and ADR-405
governs the readonly layer.

## Decisions taken without the product owner

| # | Question | Choice, and why it is the safe one |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | §LP-915 writes the completeness body as `{completeness, updated_at}`. | **`expected_updated_at`.** Every other concurrent write in this feature spells it that way, and `_ConcurrentWrite` records the same choice being made once already. The name says it is the value the caller READ, not one it is setting. |
| 2 | The spec gives **no path** for either confirm. | **`POST .../confirm-cleared` and `POST .../reworded`,** both on the round: the suggestion and the pair are that sheet's evidence, and the panel already holds the round id. |
| 3 | "Different conditions — keep both" — what becomes of the QUESTION? | **The pair is resolved without touching either condition.** The spec's "leaves both" is true of the two conditions; left pending as well, the panel would ask again on every visit and "these are different" would be unanswerable. `confirm_probably_cleared` already resolves the suggestions it was not given, for the same reason. |
| 4 | A confirm naming nothing still pending. | **Refused, changing nothing.** The alternative withdraws every question and records no verdict — see Part 2. This is the option that never removes a suggestion without a person's click. |
| 5 | *Full list -> Just some*: recompute, or withdraw? | **Withdraw the unconfirmed suggestions; recompute nothing.** `_open_before` reads current statuses, so after five confirms a recompute would find five fewer conditions open and "Compared with the 11 conditions that were open before it" would quietly become 6. That number is a fact about the past. Recorded verdicts are never unpicked (ADR-404). |
| 6 | S2-06 orders the count pills Probably cleared first; S2-08 orders them Came back first. Neither lists pill order under *May differ*. | **S2-06's fixed order,** because that screen states its pill line explicitly and pills that reshuffle between rounds are harder to read. **This is a difference from S2-08 that is not on a May-differ list** and is the one item here a product owner may want to overturn. |
| 7 | S2-08's came-back line ends "· our status moved from Sent to lender back to To do". | **The clause is omitted; the line says which dated note set it.** The pair of statuses lives in the `condition_came_back` event's `prep_status_from` / `prep_status_to`, and reading it means a history fetch per came-back condition from inside the panel. |
| 8 | S2-06's "line naming what didn't change". | **Omitted.** The saved comparison deliberately stores only the values that MOVED, so the unchanged ones are not available to name. Adding them would put more of the lender's letter into an NPI column to decorate one line. |
| 9 | The stored letter labels are the tickets file's prose ("note rate", "close by expiry"); the design table prints "Note rate", "Expiry · Close by". | **The UI formats them; the stored labels stay the spec's.** Rewriting the data to match one table would make the saved comparison a rendering of one screen rather than a record of what moved. |
| 10 | S2-10 draws **Attach the lender's PDF** beside **Switch to Full list**. | **Only the switch is in the panel.** The round strip already owns that button on the same round, with the rule for when it may appear; offering one action from two places is how the two come to disagree. |
| 11 | S2-08's round-3 card reads "Review what changed" rather than a count. | **The count form only.** S2-06's Must-match states its card line explicitly; S2-08's Must-match does not mention its card text beyond the counts. |
| 12 | The detail sheet's suggestion block — a "Keep open" button? | **No button.** Keeping it open is what happens if she does nothing, and a dismissal there would withdraw the suggestion from one row while the panel still offered it — two places disagreeing about one question. |
| 13 | Where the panel lives. | **Above the list, dismissable per round** (the pack allows a sheet instead). Hidden by round id, not by a boolean, so importing a newer sheet raises the new round's panel instead of staying hidden because she closed the previous one. |

## Noted, not changed

- **LP-914 (board) is deferred** and nothing here anticipates it.
- The panel resolves condition ids against the rows the list already holds. A suggestion whose condition
  is outside the current page is skipped rather than half-drawn; with the 500-row cap that is reachable
  only on a file far larger than any fixture.
- `confirm_probably_cleared` resolves a round's suggestions wholesale, so confirming one condition from
  the detail sheet also clears the pending mark from the others on that round. The toast says so. A
  per-condition resolution would need the suggestion to live on the condition rather than the round,
  which is a data-model change and not this ticket's.
