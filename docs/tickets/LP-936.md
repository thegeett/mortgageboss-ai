### The three choices

**(a) Over-50% firing on the level alone is right.** "Always" is the owner's word, and the alternative
— requiring movement — would leave a file sitting above 50% silently unflagged while evidence lands on
it. Flagging more is the safe direction for a prompt whose only cost is a second look. One cosmetic
note: at 52 → 52 the reason renders "the DTI is over 50% (52% → 52%)", an arrow between two identical
numbers; dropping the arrow when nothing moved would read better, and nothing depends on it.

**(b) Both reasons at 44 → 51 is right.** Both limbs are true, they do not contradict, and the panel
lists reasons rather than picking one.

**(c) The wording under the existing heading is right**, and it costs no deviation: the README pins
only *"DU re-run not needed… (Fannie Mae B3-2-10)"* with the middle elided, so the sentence between is
free.

### The S3-09 callout, and a correction to my own LP-924 review

The old text — *"DTI stays at or under 45% and rose less than 3 points"* — was true of the seeded state
(40.36% → 40.97%) and false as a statement of the rule: a 46% → 48% file would have shown "DU re-run
not needed" beside a sentence claiming the DTI stays at or under 45%. **I reviewed that sentence in
LP-924 and passed it**, because I checked it against the screen in front of me and the README's pinned
fragment, and did not ask whether it stayed true in the other states where the callout renders. That is
the "UI copy is an untested claim" trap, and I walked into it.

The new text reads *"DTI did not cross 45%, is not over 50%, and rose less than 3 points"* — true as a
rule, not just here — and the explaining line names all three limbs with the parenthetical: *"If DTI
crossed 45%, went over 50%, or rose 3 points or more (at 50% or less)…"*. Read on my own re-shot
S3-09; the rest of the screen is unchanged and still matches.
