## What and why

Send button. None of it had sent a real email from staging. This ticket turns it on for the staging
test company only, sends one email to a real inbox and one to the SES bounce simulator, and records

## What changed

**Label fix (commit 48c6fb76).** A Send still `queued` or `sending` after the 1.5-minute window now
reads **"Still sending: check back in a few minutes"**. **"Not confirmed: check before sending
again"** shows only when `error_detail` starts with `NOT_CONFIRMED_PREFIX`, which is the worker's own
unknown outcome. The reason: a merely queued email may still be sent, because the worker re-enqueues

duplicate from her own mail. The comment is in `frontend/lib/communication/send-status.ts`. Tests
updated: `send-status.test.ts`, `timeline-panel.test.tsx` (including the clock test, now "turns

### Message-ID format

`<{SES MessageId}@email.amazonses.com>`. SES replaces the Message-ID that the app writes, and the id
part equals the row's `external_message_id`. Example from 5a:
`<010001a192217695-d74569ea-1228-41f2-b3c9-3ceecae9f7ff-000000@email.amazonses.com>`. Reply
threading should key on this value, not on the id in the stored `.eml`.

## Findings (recorded, not fixed)

1. **SES drops the From display name.** The stored `.eml` has `From: "Geet Thaker, MortgageBoss"
   <lf-afcw@…>`, but Gmail received a bare `lf-afcw@mail.staging.mortgageboss.ai`. The cause is in
   `app/services/mail_sender.py:128`, which passes `FromEmailAddress=parseaddr(...)[1]` (the bare
   address), and SES writes that over the header. A borrower sees a file code, not the processor's
   name. The likely fix is to pass the full From header, or to leave `FromEmailAddress` out for raw
   content. This needs its own ticket.
2. **`mbai-staging-scheduler-dlq` holds 5 messages.** It isn't a mail queue and has no alarm. I
   didn't read or touch it. It is probably the US-hours start/stop schedule failing now and then.
   Worth a look.

## How to demo

1. Start staging with `./scripts/deploy staging up --yes` (staging runs on US hours).
2. Sign in as a MortgageBoss user and open any file's **Communication** tab. The file's address
   shows under "Drafts & messages".
3. Open a document-request draft, set the recipient to your own address, and click **Send**. It
   reads Queued, then Sent, then Delivered within a few seconds.
4. Do the same with `bounce@simulator.amazonses.com`. It reads "Failed: smtp; 550 5.1.1 …", and the
   address is added to the suppressed list.
5. Files of any other company show no address and only "Mark as sent".
