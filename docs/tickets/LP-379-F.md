# LP-379-F — Map Priya's free-text apparent_category labels onto the enum, then score

## What this ticket is

LP-379-D scored 72 of Priya's 122 labels and HELD 50 — her `apparent_category` labels were free text
(`"transfer to some one"`, `"Credit card payment"`) because the old enum couldn't hold them. LP-379-E widened
the enum. This proposes a mapping of her free text onto the new values — **Priya-confirmed, never guessed** —
and scores the confirmed subset. It is the FIRST measurement of `apparent_category` against a domain expert's
labels.

## Acceptance criteria

- A proposed mapping table, split CONFIRMED-obvious vs NEEDS-PRIYA; the non-obvious held, never force-mapped.
- The confirmed subset scores; failing cases + reasoning reported.
- Uncertainty preserved (an uncertain note → `unknown`, held).
- Priya's original free text preserved (the mapping is additive, her words not destroyed).
- `ACTIVE_RULE_IDS` unchanged; LP-379-D's stable scores unchanged; full suite green.

## Phase 0 — the finding that reshaped the mapping

**The AI sees the transaction MEMO, not Priya's judgment.** Reading all 50 labels with their descriptions
surfaced the decisive fact: **30 of the 50 sit on the SAME memo — `CARD PURCHASE / PAYMENT`** — which carries
no payee. Priya gave those 30 eight different categories from the **amounts** + the un-redacted file she opened
via LP-379-C's real filenames (her note on a $14,316 debit: *"Must be American express CC payment"* — inferred
from the amount, not the memo). LP-379-E's prompt CORRECTLY tells the AI to categorize from the payee in the
description, **not the amount** — so the AI cannot (and must not) reproduce her call. The memos are the AS-1
"PII-redacted transaction memo": the redaction that protects PII also removes the categorization signal.

**So a label is scorable only where the DESCRIPTION supports the category.** Everything else is HELD — needs
Priya + a descriptive memo — never guessed. A guessed mapping would score the model against a category the
prompt forbids it to produce.

## The mapping table (proposed — Priya confirms)

Keyed by the memo the AI sees (which determines scorability), with her free text + note:

| memo (AI's signal) | Priya's free text | → enum | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PAYROLL DIRECT DEPOSIT` | "payroll" ×8 | `payroll` | **CONFIRMED** (memo states payroll) |
| `INTEREST EARNED` | "interest" ×4 | `interest` | **CONFIRMED** (memo states interest) |
| `ONLINE TRANSFER … OWN ACCOUNT` | "transfer to own account", "transfer in" ×3 | `transfer_own` | **CONFIRMED** (memo states own-account) |
| `INBOUND PAYMENT RECEIVED` | "transfer" (note: Ravi) | `transfer_third_party_in` | **CONFIRMED** (a named inbound) |
| `ONLINE TRANSFER … OWN ACCOUNT` | "transfer" / "transfer in" — note *"not sure it's own transfer"* ×2 | `unknown` | **HELD** (uncertainty preserved) |
| `ONLINE TRANSFER TO OWN ACCOUNT` | "big paymrny out" (typo) | — | **HELD** (don't guess a typo) |
| `CARD PURCHASE / PAYMENT` ×30 | "Credit card payment/bill", "Some kind of mortgage payment", "Scheduled payment", "Some payment", "fee", "transfer to some one", "trasfer" | — | **HELD** (generic memo — no payee; her category is an amount/judgment call) |

**Split: 17 CONFIRMED (scored), 33 HELD.** Of the 33: 30 generic-memo, 2 uncertain (→ `unknown`), 1 typo.

## Phase 2 — the score (first apparent_category measurement on a domain expert's labels)

Confirmed subset, real reasoner, against the fixture:

- **`txn.apparent_category`: 17 labels, accuracy 100% when concrete (16/16).** payroll ×8, interest ×4,
  transfer_own ×4 — all correct.
- **The 17th** (the inbound Priya knew was from "Ravi"): the AI **abstained** — `unknown`, reasoning
  *"'INBOUND PAYMENT RECEIVED' is generic and provides no information about the sender."* An **honest
  abstention**, not a miss — the prompt working exactly as designed (don't guess a payee the memo doesn't
  name). It reinforces the finding: even a "confirmed" third-party label isn't perceivable from the redacted
  memo.

**No prompt bug.** The structuring layer is 100% on the categories the memo supports and correctly abstains
where it doesn't. There is no systematic mis-categorization to fix (no FINDING-1-class prompt bug).

**Of the held 50:** 17 now scored, 33 still NEEDS-PRIYA (30 unscorable on the generic memo, 2 uncertain, 1
typo). The 33 are held with a clear reason, never silently skipped.

## The finding for a future ticket (data, not prompt)

`apparent_category`'s widened categories (`debt_payment`, `transfer_third_party_*`) are **unmeasurable on
LF-6T3N** because its transaction memos are generic/PII-redacted (`CARD PURCHASE / PAYMENT`). The AI correctly
abstains rather than guessing from the amount — which is right, but means the file cannot exercise the new
categories. LP-379-E's own probe showed the AI DOES categorize them correctly when the memo names the payee
(`AMEX EPAYMENT` → `debt_payment`, `ZELLE TO ANAND` → `transfer_third_party_out`). **Calibrating the widened
categories needs transactions whose memos name the payee** — a calibration-DATA gap (weighed against the PII
the redaction removes), its own ticket. Not a prompt or model fix.

## Assumptions & decisions

- **Confirmed = description-supported only.** A generic memo is held — a guessed mapping scores the model
  against a category the prompt forbids, worse than holding (the ticket's own rule).
- **Uncertainty preserved** — *"not sure it's own transfer"* → `unknown`, held; a golden is never more certain
  than the labeler was.
- **Her words preserved, maximally.** The mapping is a scoring-time TRANSLATION LAYER
  (`apparent_category_relabel.relabel`), applied to a copy — her committed free-text golden column is
  **untouched** (a test pins it). The proposed mapping is Priya's to confirm; nothing in her data is rewritten.
- **`has_identified_source` still held** — a different question (its own worksheet-clarity ticket), unlabeled.
- **No ADR was strictly required (label mapping), but the finding earned one** — ADR-304 records that
  apparent_category is unmeasurable on redacted memos and that this is a data gap, not a prompt bug.

## What's built

- `app/verification/eval/apparent_category_relabel.py` — the disciplined mapping (`relabel(free_text,
  description, note) → Relabel(enum, confirmed, rationale)`): confirmed only where the memo supports it, held
  otherwise, `unknown` for uncertainty, never an off-enum value.
- Tests pin the confirmed/held split, the generic-memo hold, the uncertainty rule, the enum safety, and that
  Priya's committed free text is preserved.
