# LP-1011 — Clean up old settings, and six small fixes

Step 4 (Receive), fourth ticket. Code, tests, a dev check and one Terraform change. Nothing committed.

## What and why

**Cleanup**

1. **`FILE_ADDRESSES_ENABLED` is gone.** It hid every file address until step 4 could route them. Step 4 routes
   them now (LP-1008), so a file has an address exactly when its company's mail domain is `active` or `problem`.
2. **`inbox_domain` / `INBOX_DOMAIN` / `PRODUCTION_INBOX_DOMAIN` are gone.** The old token address and its
   resolver went in LP-991 and LP-1008. The last reader was Route B's `co-<token>@<INBOX_DOMAIN>` address.
3. **Route B's screen is hidden.** Administration → Email (connect a company mailbox, `FORWARDED_ALIAS`) is off
   the menu and answers 404. Its code stays. ADR-395 and the Phase 4 design docs now say Route B is superseded
   by `docs/email-communication/loan-email.md`.

**Fixes** (from LP-1009 and LP-1010 "Open")

1. **Dismiss** an unmatched email in the Inbox. Spam to a made-up address on a company's domain can now leave the
   list.
2. **Arrival time.** The email card and the document label ("Email · from … · <date>") show when we received
   the email, not the `Date` header, which the sender writes.
3. **Copy From name** is `"<sender address> via <company>"`. A sender chooses their display name, and the copy
   is signed by our domain, so "Summit IT Support – Password Reset" could no longer appear there.
4. **Line breaks** in the subject or the sender's name become spaces. Before, they failed the copy, or crashed
   it before anything was recorded.
5. **Bcc: not fixable, so not changed.** The brief's premise (from LP-1010 Open 12) was that SES's delivery
   list names a Bcc'd processor. It cannot: SES only receives for the company mail domains, and the Bcc to her
   own mailbox goes straight to her mail host. An envelope check was built, then removed in review; see Open 6.
6. **Deleted file:** no copy for a file deleted before the copy runs.

## Review (one round)

1. **The Bcc fix could not fire in production.** Removed, with its test; fix 5 above and Open 6.
2. **A failed Dismiss said nothing.** A 409 (someone moved it first) or 404 re-enabled the button silently.
   `DismissMessage` now shows the error as Move to file does; test
   `says why when Dismiss fails, e.g. someone moved it first (LP-1011)`, which fails with the error line
   removed (mutation, restored from a copy).
3. **`scripts/visual-check/run.sh` still exported `INBOX_DOMAIN`.** Removed.
4. **"Only dismissed messages are rejected with no file" is true of today's routing, not yet checked against
   staging's old rows.** A row written before LP-1008 (Route B attached a company) could be rejected with a
   company and no file, and would now leave the Inbox. Check, read-only:
   `./scripts/deploy staging query -c "select count(*) from readonly.inbound_messages where routing_state='rejected' and company_id is not null and loan_file_id is null"`.
   Result (2026-11-06): `0`. No such rows, so the Inbox filter hides only dismissed messages.

## Open

1. **Rate limit on copies:** deferred by decision (LP-1010 Open 8).
2. **Arrival time is ingest time,** not SES's receipt time, which is not stored. They differ by the ingest lag:
   about a minute, or hours if the staging worker is off overnight.
3. **Subjects are stored encoded.** Ingest stores `Subject` as written (`email.message_from_bytes`, compat32), so
   a subject with an accent or an emoji shows on the email card as `=?utf-8?q?…?=`. The copy now decodes it;
   the card does not. Found while testing fix 4. Not in the brief.
4. **No undo for Dismiss,** and no column for who dismissed (a log line has it).
5. **The Route B component, API and service stay** with a null address, and eight of their tests are skipped.
   Delete them when Route B is removed.
6. **A Bcc'd processor still gets a second copy.** Bcc is invisible by design: the Bcc to her mailbox never
   reaches SES, so neither the headers nor `envelope_recipients` can show it. LP-1010 Open 12's "though SES did
   deliver to her" was wrong. She could filter on our `X-MortgageBoss-Copy` header in her own mail client.
7. **The other LP-1010 review items stay open:** 11 (bounce or complaint about a copy not recorded), 14
   (attachment count includes unsafe ones), 15 (hard kill between send and commit), 16 and 17 (tidy-ups).
