# LP-379-B — Wire borrowers into the calibration fixture so income_stability is calibratable

## What this ticket is

LP-379-A found the LF-6T3N calibration **fixture** (`build_lf6t3n_snapshot`) had **no wired borrowers**
(`mismo.absent=True`, zero `borrower.{n}.borrower_id`, `belongs_to=None` on every document). So LP-385's
per-borrower `income_stability` producer enumerated nobody and materialized **0 rows** on the fixture — its
four tags were `no_subject`, and the 5 rules it was built to unblock (IN-7/10/11/13/14) could not be
calibrated. The real DB LF-6T3N has **2 wired borrowers**. This ticket mirrors the DB's borrower structure
into the fixture so `income_stability` materializes and becomes calibratable for Priya's LP-379. **Fixture
data only — no producer/rule/spec/prompt change.**

## Acceptance criteria

- The fixture carries 2 wired borrowers (MISMO `borrower_id` + `belongs_to` attribution), mirroring the DB.
- `income_stability` materializes per-borrower on the fixture — same borrowers, no cross-feed.
- The worksheet emits per-borrower `income_stability` rows; coverage shows `labelable`, not `no_subject`.
- Additive: the 30-document shape, the txn/id/asset capacities, and the 70 filled mechanical goldens survive.
- `ACTIVE_RULE_IDS` unchanged; full suite green. Only the fixture, the worksheets, and tests change.

## Phase 0 — the mirror spec (read the DB, then the fixture)

**The DB is the source of truth.** Read via `build_snapshot` on the real DB LF-6T3N:

| | Borrower 1 (idx 1, **primary**) | Borrower 2 (idx 2, **co**) |
|---|---|---|
| `borrower_id` | `59e173ce-…` (Akash Patel) | `16cf50cc-…` (Bansari Patel) |
| income docs (4 each) | pay_stub ×2 (Wells Fargo); **w2 2024** America Nat'l Assoc $77,988; **w2 2025** Wells Fargo $224,364 | pay_stub ×2 (Novant); **w2 2024** Novant $138,091; **w2 2025** Novant $138,696 |
| trend shape | 2-yr history, **rising**, **employer change** (BofA→WF) | 2-yr history, **flat/rising**, **same employer** (Novant) |

**The fixture's gap:** `mismo.absent=True`, zero `borrower_id`, `belongs_to=None` on all 30 docs. It already
holds two de-identified people — **Jordan A Rivera** (dl1, ps1/ps2 Acme, w1 Acme, w2 Northgate, inv1/inv2) and
**Taylor M Nguyen** (dl2, ps3/ps4 Sterling, w3 Sterling, w4 Cafe Bluebird, inv3) — but unwired.

**The structural discrepancy (gate of record vs the ticket):** the fixture's four W-2s were **all
`tax_year=2025`** — each person had two W-2s from *different employers in the same year* (concurrent
employment), **not** the DB's *two consecutive years*. A bare "add borrower_ids + belongs_to" would have left
`income_stability` seeing a single W-2 year — the exact "fixture says one year, DB says two" fiction the
ticket's governing rule condemns.

**Blast radius (small):** only two test files read the fixture (`test_lf6t3n_worksheet.py`,
`test_worksheet_refresh_lp379a.py`) — neither runs the rule engine or derived producers on it, so flipping
`mismo.absent` wakes nothing in a fixture test. Exactly one assertion broke — LP-379-A's
`test_no_income_stability_rows_on_the_borrowerless_fixture`, which **encoded the borrower-less fiction**; it
was updated to assert the wired truth (see "Tests"). The full suite confirms nothing else moved.

## Phase 1 — what was wired (mirror the DB, additively)

1. **2 MISMO borrowers** — `borrower.1` (Jordan, `is_primary=true`) + `borrower.2` (Taylor, `is_primary=false`),
   each with a fixed **de-identified** `borrower_id` UUID, names, and `employer.{n}.name` traced to their own
   W-2s. The fixture stays de-identified: it mirrors the DB's **shape** (2 borrowers, primary + co), never the
   DB's PII. Stated income *amounts* were deliberately **not** invented (the derived shortfall producers that
   would read them stay dormant/untested here; fabricating stated income is out of scope and not the goal).
2. **`belongs_to` attribution** — each document is attributed to the borrower whose own identity field carries
   their resolved name (`full_name`/`employee_name`/`account_holder`/`account_holder_name`), exactly LP-202's
   evidence model. Jordan → 12 docs (his DL, pay stubs, W-2s, investments, and the 5 Jordan-held bank
   statements); Taylor → 6. Joint/property documents (mortgage, property-tax, purchase agreement, the empty
   brokerage, unknowns) resolve to no single borrower → `belongs_to=None`.
3. **A 2-year, non-declining W-2 history via `tax_year` assignment only** — each borrower's **lower-wage** W-2
   is set to **2024**, the higher to **2025**, so both show a non-declining 2-year history mirroring the DB
   (Jordan: Northgate $12,200 [2024] → Acme $76,500 [2025]; Taylor: Cafe Bluebird $8,300 [2024] → Sterling
   $50,400 [2025]). **`tax_year` is the only field changed** — no golden reads it; `employer_name` and
   `wages_tips_other_comp` are untouched, so the `employer_normalized` / `documented_monthly` goldens stay
   valid. (The change ripples into the *context* text of those rows — a labeling aid — never their
   `golden_label`.)

**MIRROR, never invent — and the residual divergence, reported not forced:** the DB's co-borrower (Bansari)
held **one** employer across both years; the fixture's Taylor changes employer (Cafe Bluebird → Sterling).
Mirroring that would require rewriting w4's `employer_name`, invalidating its filled `employer_normalized`
golden ("Cafe Bluebird"). Per "additive / don't disturb goldens," it is left and reported. The de-identified
fixture mirrors the DB's shape on everything additively reachable; this one same-employer detail it cannot,
without breaking a human-entered golden. **The DB wins in principle; this is a documented de-identification
limit, not an invention.**

## Phase 2 — the probe on the WIRED fixture (reported, not predicted)

Off-path, real model (`claude-sonnet-4-5`), persisting nothing, on `build_lf6t3n_snapshot()`:

```
Jordan/B1 (mirrors Akash):  has_2yr=yes  is_declining=no  same_line=yes      continuance=unknown
Taylor/B2 (mirrors Bansari): has_2yr=yes  is_declining=no  same_line=unknown  continuance=unknown
income_stability: 5 real / 8   (DB probe was 6/8)
```

`income_stability` **now enumerates both fixture borrowers and materializes** — each call carried only that
borrower's income docs (no cross-feed). The fixture matches the DB on **7 of 8** tag-values. The single
divergence is **Taylor's `same_line_of_work` = `unknown`** (fixture) vs the DB's Bansari **`yes`** — and it
traces **exactly** to the one documented structural divergence: Taylor changes employer (Cafe Bluebird →
Sterling, different fields → the model can't confirm same line), while the DB's Bansari held one employer
(Novant → obviously same line). Jordan mirrors Akash **4/4**. The 5/8-vs-6/8 gap is fully explained by the
reported divergence — **the fixture mirrors the DB**; it is not a hidden inconsistency. (This proves
production; whether the judgments are *correct* is LP-379's calibration.)

## What LP-379 can now calibrate

The income layer is now fully materializable on the fixture: the id/income-structuring/stmt/asset families
(LP-379-A) **plus** `income_stability` per-borrower (this ticket). Priya can label all four stability tags for

## Assumptions & decisions

- **Mirror the DB's shape, de-identified.** Fixed synthetic `borrower_id`s + the fixture's existing
  de-identified people, not the DB's PII — the fixture is a de-identified mirror, by design.
- **2-year history via `tax_year` only** — the one additive lever that gives the DB's 2-year non-declining
  shape without touching a golden. Assigning the lower wage to 2024 mirrors the DB's non-declining direction;
  it is not tuning to a predicted AI output (the model was run, not predicted).
- **The co-borrower same-employer detail is a reported divergence**, not forced — fixing it would break w4's
  filled golden. The DB-vs-fixture difference is surfaced (Phase 2), fully explaining the 5/8-vs-6/8 gap.
- **No stated MISMO income amounts invented** — not needed for `income_stability` (it reasons over
  documents), and fabricating them is exactly the "invent data not in the DB" the ticket forbids.

## ADR

None — fixture data. The two findings the ticket asked me to watch for are recorded above rather than as an
ADR: (1) the DB-vs-fixture structural divergence (fixture W-2s were single-year; the co-borrower's employer
differs) — reported and traced to the exact tag it moves; (2) flipping `mismo.absent` changed no other tag's
behaviour on the fixture (small blast radius, confirmed by the full suite).
