# LP-971 — Describe it: a sentence becomes steps and drafts

- **Ticket:** LP-971 (the owner, 2026-10-08). Series LP-967 to LP-973; the overview is in `LP-967.md`.
- **Source:** the agreed design ("Or describe what you want to do · Turn it into steps").
- **Status:** to build, after LP-969.

## What it adds

She types what she wants, for example "Ask the underwriter to move 1228 to closing, it's new
construction". The AI proposes steps from LP-969's building blocks, with the drafts written. She checks
them before anything is sent.

## Done when

- Steps come only from the real building blocks.
- Every draft passes the same checks as the reading: amounts must be the lender's, and nothing invented.
- A refusal or failure says so.
- Peer review.
