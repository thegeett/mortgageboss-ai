# LP-514 — An insurance renewal beside the policy it replaces blinds IH-3 and IH-9

**Status:** open (not started) · **Severity:** medium — a correct upload makes the file report LESS
**Found by:** the LP-509 verification run

## The defect

Uploading a renewal policy alongside the superseded one causes BOTH insurance date rules to abstain:

```
IH-3  couldnt_check  the insurance policy's effective date could not be read from the documents
                     (it is present but unclear)
IH-9  couldnt_check  the insurance policy's coverage period could not be read from the documents
                     (it is present but unclear)
```

LF-WCHG now holds two `homeowners_insurance` documents: an ACORD 27 running 06/25/2024 to 06/25/2025 and
its renewal running 06/25/2025 to 06/25/2026. `_loan_effective_date` and `_ins_policy_expired` each
dedupe by date and abstain when binders disagree:

> `the file's homeowners insurance binders disagree on the effective date (…) — ambiguous`

Two binders, two different dates, so both abstain.

## Why this is a design question, not just a bug

The multi-binder abstain is deliberate and correct in its original case: two binders that genuinely
disagree about the same policy period is ambiguous, and picking one would be a guess. **A renewal is not
that.** It supersedes. The dates do not conflict — they are consecutive, and the later one is the policy
in force.

The perverse outcome is what makes it worth fixing: the file went from having ONE (expired) policy that
IH-9 could report on, to having the CORRECT current policy plus its predecessor — and the system reports
less than it did before. Supplying more accurate documentation made the output worse.

## Suggested approach

Prefer the LATEST policy period rather than abstaining, when the binders form a **succession** — each
one's effective date at or after the previous one's expiration, within a tolerance. Keep abstaining when
periods genuinely OVERLAP or conflict, which is the ambiguity the rule was written for.

Both recipes share the shape, so the succession test should be one helper used by both — the ADR-375
discipline (one matcher, two consumers) already applied to RE-1/DT-6.

Open questions for the domain expert: does a lapse between two periods change the answer, and should a
gap in coverage between them be a finding in its own right?

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A renewal succeeding a prior policy resolves to the LATEST period; IH-3 and IH-9 evaluate
- [ ] Genuinely overlapping or conflicting periods still abstain
- [ ] One shared succession helper, not two implementations
- [ ] A test built from the real LF-WCHG pair (2024-2025 ACORD + 2025-2026 renewal)
- [ ] Decide whether a coverage GAP between two periods is itself reportable
