# LP-968 — Edit the path: already done, not needed, I'll do this step myself

- **Ticket:** LP-968 (the owner, 2026-10-08). Series LP-967 to LP-973; the overview is in `LP-967.md`.
- **Source:** the owner: "I think there should be override or an option like I will do it myself." Agreed
  design: the "Plan screen explained" artifact, board "The plan as a path".
- **Status:** built; see "What was built" below.

## What it adds

Controls on each step of a condition's path:
- **Already done:** an optional note, with Undo.
- **Not needed:** a one-line reason, for example "no new fee was charged", with Undo.
- **I'll do this step myself:** on a step the app prepares. The draft is not made, and the step reads
  "Contact {who} yourself to {what}", with "How are you doing it?".
- **Remove step.**
- **"Changed by you · Go back to the suggested plan".**

## What exists underneath

- **Not needed:** the item status `not_needed`.
- **Do it myself:** an ask turned into `i_will_do_it`.
- **Already done:** item status `done`.
- **New:** a note or reason on an item status change; the condition's history recording each change.

## First question: drafts before confirm

The mockup opens a prepared email inside the unconfirmed plan. Today drafts are made on confirm
(`sync_round_drafts`). The choice: make drafts when the plan is ready (unsent, and resynced as the plan
changes), or keep "we'll draft it when you confirm" in the unconfirmed plan. Decide before building.

## Done when

- Each control works, records who, when and the reason in the condition's history, and can be undone.
- A condition is never Ready without proof: a document, or done with a note.
- Peer review.

## Decision on the first question (the owner, 2026-10-08)

"go with drafting when the plan is ready".

## What was built

### Drafts when the plan is ready (backend)

- **The reading task drafts right after the plan is built.** `tasks.conditions._run_read` calls the new
  `condition_drafts.draft_ready_plan(round_id)` after `build_plan`, in the same commit. It runs
  `sync_round_drafts` when the round's `plan_ready_at` is set.
- **Plan edits before confirm reach the drafts.** `condition_plan._resync_drafts` now gates on
  `_plan_drafted` (plan ready or confirmed), and `resync_file` takes rounds that are ready or confirmed.
  **Status moves stay behind confirm:** `apply_step_status` keeps `_plan_in_force`.
- **Nothing is drafted past a reading that needs her** (README rule 3). Drafting at confirm kept that rule
  by construction, because confirm is refused while a reading needs her; drafting early does not.
  `sync_round_drafts` now skips conditions in `needs_confirmation` (`_needs_her`), for asks and for
  questions. Confirming the reading, or using the library default, re-syncs through the routes
  (`follow_drafts`), and the draft appears.
- **Nothing is sent before confirm.** `email_send.send_draft` refuses a condition draft whose rounds are
  not all confirmed: "Confirm the plan for round 1 first: nothing is sent before you confirm it."
  (`condition_drafts.unconfirmed_rounds_for`). The guard is in `send_draft`, not in Mark sent, because
  `send_draft` is the one function every send passes through. The general
  `/communications/draft/{id}/send` can send a condition draft too, and before this ticket that route also
  bypassed Mark sent's bookkeeping.

### Her edits to a task (backend)

- **`condition_items.plan_note`** (migration `f392190637fa`) holds her reason, proof, or method.
  - It is excluded from the readonly views, recorded in `EXCLUDED` and `NEVER_EXPOSED`
    (`test_readonly_query`), because free text can name a person.
  - Events record THAT a note was given (`"note": true`), never the text.
- **`update_item`** (LP-921's rule, widened):
  - `done` on her own task as before;
  - `done` on anyone else's task only with a note or a linked document. The old refusal "Only your own
    tasks…" becomes "Say how you know it's already done, or link the document…";
  - `not_needed` always needs a reason;
  - `open` only undoes her own marks (done or not needed), and clears the note;
  - a task asked or answered keeps its record.
- **"I'll do this myself"** is the existing option change to `i_will_do_it`, now with an optional note.
- **"Go back to the suggested plan"** is `POST /conditions/{id}/plan/revert`, through
  `revert_to_suggested`.
  - It replays the condition's `condition_plan_changed` history: the first recorded `from` of each task's
    option and status, and of the condition's own step, is what the plan suggested. Nothing else stores the
    suggestion: the reading's options come before the plan's own adjustments.
  - It skips tasks already asked or answered, and clears her notes on what it restores.
  - It writes one `reverted` event, then re-applies status and drafts.
- **"Remove task" is not built.** Delete is permanent, and "Not needed" covers the same need with a reason
  and an undo.

### The plan panel (frontend)

- **An ask's first step reflects the real draft:**
  - "We drafted the email to Title / escrow", with **Review draft** opening the existing draft dialog;
  - "…once you confirm how we read this condition", when the reading needs her;
  - "…when you confirm the plan", for a plan built before LP-968.
- **The send step** reads "Review the email, and send it once the plan is confirmed".
- **Each task card gets Already done, Not needed, and I'll do this myself** (on asks only).
  - Each opens one line for her words; the button stays disabled until a REQUIRED reason is typed.
  - A done task shows its note and **Undo**.
  - A not-needed task stays on the card, crossed out with its reason and **Undo**. Dropped tasks are no
    longer hidden.
- **"Go back to the suggested plan"** sits on each opened condition.
- **Wording:**
  - the Confirm button reads "Confirm the plan";
  - "When you confirm, we will:" says it unlocks the drafted emails for sending;
  - first moves reads "Review the N drafted emails".
- **The drafts list (`RoundDrafts`) is hidden while the plan holds the page,** so the same drafts are not
  shown twice.
