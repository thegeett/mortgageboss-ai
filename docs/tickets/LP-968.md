# LP-968 — Edit the path: already done, not needed, I'll do this step myself

- **Ticket:** LP-968 (the owner, 2026-10-08). Series LP-967 to LP-973; the overview is in `LP-967.md`.
- **Source:** the owner: "I think there should be override or an option like I will do it myself." Agreed
  design: the "Plan screen explained" artifact, board "The plan as a path".
- **Status:** built; see "What was built" below.

## What it adds

Controls on each step of a condition's path:
- **Already done:** an optional note, with Undo.
- **Not needed:** a one-line reason, for example "no new fee was charged", with Undo.
- **I'll do this step myself:** on a step the app prepares. The draft is not made, and the step reads
  "Contact {who} yourself to {what}", with "How are you doing it?".
- **Remove step.**
- **"Changed by you · Go back to the suggested plan".**

## What exists underneath

- **Not needed:** the item status `not_needed`.
- **Do it myself:** an ask turned into `i_will_do_it`.
- **Already done:** item status `done`.
- **New:** a note or reason on an item status change; the condition's history recording each change.

## First question: drafts before confirm

The mockup opens a prepared email inside the unconfirmed plan. Today drafts are made on confirm
(`sync_round_drafts`). The choice: make drafts when the plan is ready (unsent, and resynced as the plan
changes), or keep "we'll draft it when you confirm" in the unconfirmed plan. Decide before building.

## Done when

- Each control works, records who, when and the reason in the condition's history, and can be undone.
- A condition is never Ready without proof: a document, or done with a note.
- Peer review.

## Decision on the first question (the owner, 2026-10-08)

"go with drafting when the plan is ready".

## What was built

### Drafts when the plan is ready (backend)

- **The reading task drafts right after the plan is built.** `tasks.conditions._run_read` calls the new
  `condition_drafts.draft_ready_plan(round_id)` after `build_plan`, in the same commit. It runs
  `sync_round_drafts` when the round's `plan_ready_at` is set.
- **Plan edits before confirm reach the drafts.** `condition_plan._resync_drafts` now gates on
  `_plan_drafted` (plan ready or confirmed), and `resync_file` takes rounds that are ready or confirmed.
  **Status moves stay behind confirm:** `apply_step_status` keeps `_plan_in_force`.
- **Nothing is drafted past a reading that needs her** (README rule 3). Drafting at confirm kept that rule
  by construction, because confirm is refused while a reading needs her; drafting early does not.
  `sync_round_drafts` now skips conditions in `needs_confirmation` (`_needs_her`), for asks and for
  questions. Confirming the reading, or using the library default, re-syncs through the routes
  (`follow_drafts`), and the draft appears.
- **Nothing is sent before confirm.** `email_send.send_draft` refuses a condition draft whose rounds are
  not all confirmed: "Confirm the plan for round 1 first: nothing is sent before you confirm it."
  (`condition_drafts.unconfirmed_rounds_for`). The guard is in `send_draft`, not in Mark sent, because
  `send_draft` is the one function every send passes through. The general
  `/communications/draft/{id}/send` can send a condition draft too, and before this ticket that route also
  bypassed Mark sent's bookkeeping.

### Her edits to a task (backend)

- **`condition_items.plan_note`** (migration `f392190637fa`) holds her reason, proof, or method.
  - It is excluded from the readonly views, recorded in `EXCLUDED` and `NEVER_EXPOSED`
    (`test_readonly_query`), because free text can name a person.
  - Events record THAT a note was given (`"note": true`), never the text.
- **`update_item`** (LP-921's rule, widened):
  - `done` on her own task as before;
  - `done` on anyone else's task only with a note or a linked document. The old refusal "Only your own
    tasks…" becomes "Say how you know it's already done, or link the document…";
  - `not_needed` always needs a reason;
  - `open` only undoes her own marks (done or not needed), and clears the note;
  - a task asked or answered keeps its record.
- **"I'll do this myself"** is the existing option change to `i_will_do_it`, now with an optional note.
- **"Go back to the suggested plan"** is `POST /conditions/{id}/plan/revert`, through
  `revert_to_suggested`.
  - It replays the condition's `condition_plan_changed` history: the first recorded `from` of each task's
    option and status, and of the condition's own step, is what the plan suggested. Nothing else stores the
    suggestion: the reading's options come before the plan's own adjustments.
  - It skips tasks already asked or answered, and clears her notes on what it restores.
  - It writes one `reverted` event, then re-applies status and drafts.
- **"Remove task" is not built.** Delete is permanent, and "Not needed" covers the same need with a reason
  and an undo.

### The plan panel (frontend)

- **An ask's first step reflects the real draft:**
  - "We drafted the email to Title / escrow", with **Review draft** opening the existing draft dialog;
  - "…once you confirm how we read this condition", when the reading needs her;
  - "…when you confirm the plan", for a plan built before LP-968.
- **The send step** reads "Review the email, and send it once the plan is confirmed".
- **Each task card gets Already done, Not needed, and I'll do this myself** (on asks only).
  - Each opens one line for her words; the button stays disabled until a REQUIRED reason is typed.
  - A done task shows its note and **Undo**.
  - A not-needed task stays on the card, crossed out with its reason and **Undo**. Dropped tasks are no
    longer hidden.
- **"Go back to the suggested plan"** sits on each opened condition.
- **Wording:**
  - the Confirm button reads "Confirm the plan";
  - "When you confirm, we will:" says it unlocks the drafted emails for sending;
  - first moves reads "Review the N drafted emails".
- **The drafts list (`RoundDrafts`) is hidden while the plan holds the page,** so the same drafts are not
  shown twice.

## Review (of `27a19454`)

**Two findings, both fixed here. The send guard — the thing this ticket had to get right, since drafts
now exist before confirm — is complete, and provably so rather than by enumeration. Both findings are
side effects of drafting EARLIER that the guard does not cover, because they are not about sending: a
clock that starts too soon, and a note that disappears without being counted.**

### Finding 1 — the borrower's 72 hours now start when the plan became ready

`mint_upload_link` stamps `expires_at = utcnow() + timedelta(hours=ttl_hours)` **at mint**, with
`DEFAULT_TTL_HOURS = 72`, and `render` mints only `if not message.upload_link_url`. Until LP-968 the
first render happened inside `confirm_round_plan`, so the borrower's three days began when she
confirmed — minutes before she sent. Now the first render is the reading task's, right after
`build_plan`, and confirm's re-render reuses the link it finds.

She confirms when she next gets to the file. Two days later the borrower has 24 hours; three days later
the link in the email he receives is **already dead**, and the email is otherwise perfect. Nothing in
the suite could catch it: no test asserts how much life the link has left at send.

**Fixed:** the link is minted only once the draft's round has a confirmed plan. Three things make this
the cheap fix rather than a re-mint dance:

- `render_borrower` already omits the whole "Upload them here" paragraph when `upload_url` is None, so
  the pre-confirm preview needs no special case;
- `sync_round_drafts` "re-renders every open draft", and `confirm_round_plan` calls it **after** setting
  `plan_confirmed_at` (`condition_plan.py:1329` then `:1372`), so the link is minted exactly at confirm;
- nothing can be sent before confirm (`unconfirmed_rounds_for`), so a link that does not exist yet costs
  her nothing — and it means no live token exists for a plan she may never confirm, which is a smaller
  surface than before.

`test_the_borrowers_upload_link_waits_for_confirm` asserts the property rather than the plumbing: before
confirm the message has no url, **no `UploadLink` row exists for the file at all** (so no clock is
running), and the body says nothing about uploading; after confirm there is exactly one row and the
paragraph is back. Reverting the gate fails it.

### Finding 2 — "Go back to the suggested plan" deletes her note and says nothing changed

`revert_to_suggested` ends each item with `if touched or item.plan_note: item.plan_note = None` and then
`reverted += int(touched)`. Clearing the note is right — the note is one of her changes. Not counting it
is not: a **note-only edit is reachable**, because `update_item` writes `plan_note` outside the status
block (`changed["note"] = True` on its own) and the request's `note` is an independent field. Such an
edit records no option and no status `from`, so the replay finds nothing to restore, `touched` stays
false, and the function returns 0 — while the note is gone.

The count's one consumer is the guard `if reverted:` around the `condition_plan_changed` event (the
route discards the return value, which I checked rather than assumed), so the consequence is an
**audit trail that is silent about it**: her note is deleted and the condition's history has no line
saying a revert happened. On a mixed revert the recorded `count` also understates what changed.

**Fixed:** a note-only clear is counted. One line, and the docstring now says the note is cleared on
every item and counted.

**Also documented, not changed:** a **split part is left alone**. The replay matches on the live item's
key and LP-946 gives a part the key `<base>.<performer>`, so the `from` recorded against the base key
finds no part to restore, and the split itself is not undone. That is defensible — inventing revert
semantics for parts is a product decision, not a review's — but it was undocumented, and the
"WHAT IT LEAVES ALONE" list is exactly where a reader looks for it. It is now in that list.

### Checked and not findings

- **The send guard is complete, and by construction rather than by listing doors.** There is exactly ONE
  writer of `CommunicationStatus.SENT` in the tree — `email_send.py:386` — and it sits inside
  `send_draft`, below the `unconfirmed_rounds_for` check at `:273`. `mark_sent` calls `send_draft`
  (`condition_drafts.py:1412`) rather than setting status itself, so both doors pass the guard and a
  third door would have to add a second SENT writer to escape it. That is a much stronger answer than
  "I checked the doors I could think of".
- **The round choice holds for a draft spanning two rounds.** `unconfirmed_rounds_for` collects each
  condition's `last_seen_round_id` and refuses if ANY of those rounds is unconfirmed, so a draft that
  grew to carry round 2's asks cannot be sent on round 1's confirmation. The reuse case is the same
  shape: `resync_file` re-points the draft's items when round 2's plan goes ready, and the conditions it
  then holds drag their own rounds into the check.
- **The error reaches her as a sentence.** The guard raises `CannotSendError("Confirm the plan for round
  1 first: nothing is sent before you confirm it.")`, and the mark-sent route catches
  `(DraftRefused, CannotSendError)` into a 409 with `reason or str(exc)` — so both exception shapes
  surface the same way.
- **The signer is right at send, and I nearly reported that it was not.** `draft_ready_plan` passes
  `actor_user_id=None`, so the task's first render signs "Your processor"; `draft_view` reads the stored
  body and does not re-render. I had the finding half-written when I checked the rest of
  `confirm_round_plan` and found `sync_round_drafts(..., actor_user_id=actor_user_id)` at line 1372,
  inside the same function, with `sync_round_drafts` documented as "every open draft is then
  re-rendered". So confirm re-signs with her name, and nothing can be sent before confirm. I had read
  the first 35 lines of a 60-line function and drawn a conclusion from them.
- **`route_ask` cannot corrupt the replay.** It is a pure mutation that writes no event, so it
  contributes no `from` at all — the first recorded `from` for a key is still her first edit's, which is
  the suggestion.
- **A revert after a revert is idempotent.** The replay takes the FIRST `from` per key, and the revert's
  own event is a later one, so a second revert still restores the original suggestion rather than her
  undo.
- **Drafts in the timeline before confirm are accurate.** `timeline.py` includes DRAFT messages with
  "being prepared" wording, never "sent", and already filters a blank compose draft. They simply appear
  earlier than they used to, which is what the ticket intends.
- **The widened done rule is coherent, and the owner's call is safe to take.** `ready_because` still
  requires every live item DONE and `_goes_in` still requires READY, open and not info-only — the
  widening changes which items can reach DONE, not what Ready means. And the package does not claim a
  document that is not there: `_code_note` returns **"No document."** for a row with no documents, so a
  condition made ready by a note goes to the lender saying exactly that. **One thing for the owner,
  not a defect:** her `plan_note` does not travel into the package note, which is built from the row's
  facts. So if she writes "spoke to the LO, he confirmed the payoff", the lender sees "No document." and
  her proof stays internal. That may be right — it is her working note, and it is `NEVER_EXPOSED` in the
  readonly views — but if the note is the proof we accept, somebody may expect the lender to see it.
- **The legacy case is handled and says so.** `plan-path.ts` keys the drafted step on
  `item.draft?.status === "draft"`, falling back to "We draft the email … when you confirm the plan" for
  a plan built before LP-968 and to its own sentence for a reading that still needs her. Nothing assumes
  a draft exists pre-confirm.
