## 10a — "Waiting on Processor"

**What was measured.** There is no separate waiting-on picker. A move to Waiting sends the condition's OWNER
as who it waits on (the row's select, the move-back dialog, and the bulk move, which falls back to each row's
owner). 0006's owner, from UWM's code map, was `processor`, so it became "Waiting on Processor".

**The fix, at every door, leaving the processor a valid owner (her task):**
- **Server:** `move_prep_status` refuses `waiting_on = processor` (`waiting_on_self`, "You can't wait on
  yourself. Choose who you are waiting on, or keep it in To do."). The bulk move goes through it, so a
  processor-owned row is refused with that sentence while the others move.
- **Screen:** a move to Waiting on a condition whose owner is the processor opens "Who is 0006 waiting on?"
  (`WaitingOnDialog`), offering every owner but the processor, instead of sending her. The move-back dialog
  carries her reason into it. `waitingOnFor(condition)` is the one rule both moves use.
- The owner pickers (`OFFERED_OWNERS`) keep Processor, as asked.
- `imported-view.tsx` still sends the owner, but it is unreachable since LP-913 (nothing renders it); the
  server refusal covers it.

## 12 — the readonly views

Migration `5d8b2f6a3c91` rebuilds two views and adds one, each re-granted to `mbai_readonly` inside
`upgrade()` (a dropped view drops its grants; `test_readonly_query.py` cannot test privileges, so the grant
is in the same DO block as the other two):
- `readonly.conditions` + `reading_status`, `reading_source`, `reading_confidence`, `next_step`,
  `plan_reason`. `reading` stays out.
- `readonly.condition_rounds` + `plan_ready_at`, `plan_confirmed_at`, `plan_confirmed_by_user_id`,
  `reading_state`, `reading_read`, `reading_used_ai`, `reading_fell_back`, `reading_model` (from
  `reading_run`). The raw `reading_run` stays out: its `error` is free text.
- `readonly.condition_items` (new): ids, key, who acts, step, status, origin, documents and checks (codes),
  the linked document and page, waits-on, draft, part, timestamps. NOT `name`, `acceptable`, `specifics`.

**Guards.** `condition_items` leaves `EXCLUDED_TABLES` and gets a per-column EXCLUDED set. `acceptable` and
`specifics` join `NEVER_EXPOSED` (absent from every view). `name` cannot (other views carry a `name`
legitimately: companies, lenders, rules, saved views), so `test_the_items_view_never_selects_name` checks the
one view. Measured: before that test, adding `name` to the new view passed every test in the file. The
`condition_evidence` exclusion reason, which said "Excluded whole, like `condition_items`", is corrected.

**Docs.** `docs/querying-staging.md` gains a "Conditions (LP-956)" table: what each condition view carries and
leaves out, and that `condition_evidence`, `condition_drafts` and `condition_packages` stay out.
