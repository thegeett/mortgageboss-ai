# LP-430 — Write the terminated-employment documentation rule (IN-15)

## What this ticket is

Priya's **B14 ruling** (LP-393-6) split a documentation standard out of the `has_2yr_history` judgment: a
terminated job's two years still **count as history** (IN-11's concern), but whether the employment is
documented as **current** is a **separate check**. This builds that check as **IN-15**, on her exact
refinement: **"terminated" = ANY employment end date in the past** (no grace period); **one subsequent pay stub
clears it.** It is deterministic (two date facts, no judgment → ADR-334, no calibration round) and activates
(**ACTIVE 34 → 35**).

## D1 — redundancy + the rule id

No existing rule covers this — the `end_date`/terminated matches in the specs are incidental (IN-13 other-income
continuance, etc.). No product surface shows it (the LP-416 lesson checked). The catalog (`rule_kinds.csv`,
IN-1…IN-14) reserved no id and lists no terminated/continuation entry → genuinely new, next free id **IN-15**.
The spec loader **requires** an `IN-15` row in `rule_kinds.csv` (raises otherwise), so one was added
(`structural`, `deterministic_only`) and `docs/stage2-rule-classification.md` regenerated. The CSV is
hand-editable/version-controlled (only the `.md` is generated from it) — the LP-424 "care" flag; the per-kind
count guards were bumped (structural 60 → 61, total 133 → 134).

## D2 — the IN-11 boundary (both verdicts observed)

IN-11 fires on `has_2yr_history=no`; per B14 a terminated job's years **count** → not IN-11's concern. IN-15
asks whether that (terminated) employment is documented as current. **Observed on the same terminated borrower
(B1), live reasoner:** IN-11 = `couldnt_check` (its concern: *"the two-year income history could not be
read…"*) and IN-15 = `fired` (*"employment shown as ended 2026-05-01; a pay stub dated after that is
needed…"*). They read **disjoint tags** (`income.has_2yr_history` vs `income.terminated_employment`) and produce
**provably distinct reasons** — a terminated job never surfaces two overlapping findings (the IN-11/IN-12,
AS-8/AS-10 complementary-not-duplicate discipline). **IN-11/IN-12 unchanged.**

## D3 — what "one pay stub clears it" means (argued; flagged for Priya) → ADR-339

Her ruling is exact on the count (one) but silent on three qualifiers — each a documentation-sufficiency
judgment that will recur:

- **Dated AFTER the end date?** **Yes** — a pre-termination stub proves nothing. Decided (`pay_date > end_date`).
- **Same vs ANY employer?** **Permissive (any employer)** — a new-employer stub clears it *more* convincingly,
  and a same-employer-only test would wrongly fail a job-changer (a false FIRE on a genuinely-employed
  borrower — the wrong direction). A defensible default **flagged for Priya** in the bar, not a silent
  narrowing.
- **VOE / offer letter also acceptable?** She said "pay stub" — IN-15 does **not** broaden her ruling
  (pay-stub-only); the question is noted for her.

## D4 — fail closed, never accuse on a missing document

| case | verdict | why |
|---|---|---|
| past end date + **no** pay stub | **`fired`** | the documentation is missing — **asks for the pay stub**, interpolates the end date, never asserts unemployment |
| past end date + a pay stub dated **after** it | `satisfied` | cleared (any employer) |
| **future** end date (fixed-term still running) | `not_applicable` | a continuation concern (IN-13's territory), NOT a termination |
| **no VOE** at all | `not_applicable` | the rule cannot apply — never a finding on a missing document |
| unreadable end date on a present VOE | `not_applicable` | an absent `income.employment_end` tag is indistinguishable from no-VOE without the AI `voe_present` tag — the never-accuse choice (a documented no-AI limitation) |

## D5 — expressibility + the derived producer

The check is *"is there a pay stub dated after the VOE's end date?"* — a **cross-document date comparison, per
borrower**. Not native to a single-tag read → it needs a **derived producer** (the LP-421/422 shape, deliberately
chosen, in scope). Two derived borrower-subject tags:
- **`income.terminated_employment`** — the status enum (`cleared | needs_pay_stub | not_terminated | unknown`),
  computed from `income.employment_end` (parsed off the VOE) + `income.pay_date` (parsed off the pay stubs) over
  the borrower's attributed documents.
- **`income.terminated_employment_end_date`** — the most recent past end date, for the reason interpolation (the
  IH-3 operand pattern).

Deterministic — no AI, no threshold, no calibration round (ADR-334 / ADR-339).

## D6 — the subject match

Per-borrower (IN-11's shape); the VOE/pay-stub facts are per-document → promoted via
`_borrower_attributed_documents` (LP-385). Verified end-to-end: the derived tag materializes at the borrower
subject, and IN-15 (per_borrower) reads it there. B2's pay stub is attributed to B2 only, so it never clears
B1's termination (per-borrower isolation).

## D7 — provability

No fixture had a VOE with a *past* end date, so a standalone scenario was built:
`build_terminated_employment_snapshot` (95… namespace, file date 2026-07-01), four borrowers — one per branch.

## The spec as written

`per_borrower`, `kind: structural`. Applicability `income.terminated_employment ne not_terminated` (so no
past termination → `not_applicable`; `unknown` → the gate `couldnt_check`s). Outcomes: `needs_pay_stub` →
`fired` (reason interpolates `{end_date}`, asks for the pay stub); default → `satisfied`. Bar:
`no-ai-dependency`, `ships: auto`, `input_resolves: true` (verified on the fixture), activated via
`_LP430_ACTIVATED`.

## The proof (reported, not predicted)

Materialized parsed + derived (deterministic, keyless), IN-15 evaluated per borrower on the fixture:

| borrower | scenario | `income.terminated_employment` | IN-15 verdict | reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | past end date (2026-05-01), no pay stub | `needs_pay_stub` | **`fired`** | *"employment shown as ended 2026-05-01; a pay stub dated after that is needed to confirm current employment"* |
| B2 | past end date + a pay stub dated 2026-06-15 (new employer) | `cleared` | `satisfied` | *"employment ended 2026-05-01, but a pay stub dated after it confirms current employment"* |
| B3 | future end date (2027-01-01) | `not_terminated` | `not_applicable` | out of scope |
| B4 | no VOE, only a pay stub | `not_terminated` | `not_applicable` | out of scope |

**The reason wording:** it asks for the document and interpolates the end date; it contains no "unemployed" /
"not employed" / "no longer employed" claim (asserted in the tests). **On LF-6T3N:** IN-15 is `not_applicable`
for both borrowers — the root is an honest absence (LF-6T3N has no VOE → no past end date), not a
tag-production failure.

## Activation

**ACTIVE 34 → 35.** IN-15 is `no-ai-dependency` (deterministic, `input_resolves: true`) → eligible on the gate,
no Priya sign-off needed (the ADR-334 escape hatch). The LP-389 invariant holds; the 34 previously-live rules
are identical (IN-11/IN-12 especially).

## Assumptions / decisions

- Deterministic (ADR-339 Decision 1) → no calibration; activates immediately.
- The reason asks for the document, never asserts unemployment (ADR-339 Decision 2).
- Any-employer + dated-after clearing pay stub, pay-stub-only (ADR-339 Decision 3 — flagged for Priya).
- A future end date → not_applicable (IN-13's continuation territory); an unreadable-end-date-on-present-VOE →
  not_applicable (the no-AI limitation, D4).

## New gaps

- **Unreadable-end-date-on-a-present-VOE** is not distinguishable from no-VOE without the AI `voe_present` tag →
  IN-15 treats it as `not_applicable`. A future AI-gated variant could `couldnt_check` it (at the cost of the
  no-calibration property).
- **The D3 qualifiers** (same-vs-any employer; VOE/offer-letter acceptability) await Priya's confirmation.

## ADR

**ADR-339** — a documentation-standard check spun off a judgment tag is a deterministic per-borrower producer
(no calibration, ADR-334 applied); the reason asks for the document, never asserts a fact about the borrower;
and the documentation-sufficiency qualifiers (any-employer, dated-after, pay-stub-only) are a recurring Priya
decision, flagged not silently chosen.
