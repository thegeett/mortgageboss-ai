# LP-377-A — The classifier gap: what the 4 untyped documents on LF-6T3N actually are

## OUTCOME: the premise was wrong — STOP and REPORT (no classifier change)

The ticket's premise was *"4 documents the classifier could not type are the single root behind ID-7/ID-9's
8 rows, the suppressed sentence, and ID-4's poisoned filter — fix the classification and all three collapse."*
**I read the 4 real documents from the DB. The premise does not hold.** The classifier is doing its job; the
4 documents are three unrelated things, none of them a title commitment, deed, or POA — the documents ID-7/
ID-9 actually need. **Making the classifier type them would resolve none of the three symptoms, and for two of
the four it would require fabricating a type that should not exist.** Per the ticket's own instruction — *"if
the honest answer is 'these 4 are genuinely untypeable and the classifier is right', SAY SO"* — this ticket is
a diagnosis, not a fix.

## WHY THE THREE SYMPTOMS DO NOT COLLAPSE (verified on the persisted run `01039e93`)

The persisted governed findings on LF-6T3N: **ID-7 couldnt_check ×4, ID-9 couldnt_check ×4** (one per untyped
document), reason *"applicability tag 'document.document_type' is unknown — cannot confirm the rule applies."*
And the document census: **`title_commitment` present: False; `power_of_attorney` present: False.**

1. **ID-7 / ID-9's 8 rows.** ID-7 (title vesting) and ID-9 (POA acceptability) are per_document rules that
   `couldnt_check` on each untyped document (*"it might be the one I need"*). But **LF-6T3N genuinely has no
   title/deed and no POA** (confirmed). So even in the BEST achievable classifier outcome the rows do not
   clear: the 2 screenshots **cannot** be typed (they are not borrower documents), the W2 **cannot** be typed
   (no text — an extraction failure), and the wire is domain-ambiguous. At best ID-9 drops from 4 to ~2–3
   couldnt_check, and **there is still no POA to check.** The premise's "collapse at the source" does not happen.
2. **The suppressed "no title commitment is in the file" sentence.** It is suppressed because ≥1 document is
   untyped (LP-330 — an untyped doc might BE the title commitment). Two of the four are lender screenshots that
   **cannot** be legitimately typed, so the suppression would persist. **The suppression is correct and was not
   touched** (LP-330's contract — the engine's honesty is not the bug).
3. **ID-4's filter gate.** Already settled by **LP-372**: ID-4's `unknown`s are `id.current_address_type =
   unknown` on **correctly-typed** property documents (purchase_agreement, mortgage_statement, property_tax_bill
   — a property address is not a residence). **Not the untyped documents at all.** Symptom #3 is unrelated to
   the classifier gap.

**One root, three symptoms — disproven.** The three symptoms have three different roots, none a fixable
classifier gap: (1) the file lacks title/POA documents; (2) the suppression is correct; (3) LP-372 settled it.

## THE ASYMMETRIC RISK (why not to "fix" this by guessing)

`unknown` is **honest and blocks** — a `couldnt_check` a human sees. A **wrong** type is **silent** — it routes
a rule at the wrong document and produces a confident verdict from the wrong source, with nothing to catch it
(LP-333's IN-8/IN-9 class). Making the classifier guess a type for the 2 screenshots (to eliminate `unknown`)
would trade an honest blocker for a false-green — **strictly worse.** `unknown` must remain reachable and
first-class. **This ticket makes no classifier, catalog, or prompt change.**

## NEW GAPS DISCOVERED (each its own ticket — reported, not fixed here)

- **Extraction/OCR failure — "Akash W2 Wells 2024.pdf" (root d).** A genuine W2 whose extraction produced
  **0 characters** → `unknown` at conf 0.0, status `needs_review`. The root is the extraction/OCR pipeline, not
  the classifier. Worth its own ticket (why did this one PDF yield no text — a scan/format issue?).
- **Non-borrower documents in a real loan file (the real design question).** The 2 UWM dashboard screenshots
  are confidently identified as *"NOT a loan document"* yet sit in the file as `unknown`, blocking ID-7/ID-9's
  informative sentence **forever** via the (correct) LP-330 suppression. The open question: should the engine
  have a state for *"confidently identified as not a borrower document"* — distinct from `unknown` (*"a
  borrower document I could not type"*) — so a screenshot does not masquerade as *"might be the title
  commitment"*? **This is the "a rule must not be blocked forever by an untypeable document" question the ticket
  named — a different design ticket, out of scope here.**
- **The EMD wire request vs `earnest_money_receipt` (domain / sister).** Whether a due-diligence-fee wire
  *request* should classify as `earnest_money_receipt`, or broaden that indicator, is a domain question and
  UNMEASURED — not made here (it would resolve none of the three symptoms and risks the LP-335/340 fit-to-one-
  case mistake).

## ASSUMPTIONS / DECISIONS

- **The premise was falsified by the data, so the fix belongs elsewhere.** A reported "the premise was wrong"
  beats a fix aimed at nothing (the ticket's own instruction).
- **No classifier guess.** The risk is asymmetric; `unknown` is the correct answer for 3 of the 4 (2 screenshots
  + the text-less W2) and domain-ambiguous for the 4th.
- **The suppression is correct** (LP-330) and untouched.
