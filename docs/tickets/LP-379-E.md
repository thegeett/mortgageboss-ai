# LP-379-E — Widen txn.apparent_category (Priya's labels revealed two missing categories)

## What this ticket is

Calibrating the real LF-6T3N (LP-379), Priya reached for `txn.apparent_category` values the enum cannot hold —
third-party transfers and payments to creditors. **Where a tag cannot express a risk, no downstream rule can
ever catch it: the information dies at the tag layer.** This widens the vocabulary so those risks can be
recorded. It changes what the system can PERCEIVE, not how accurately it perceives — and not any live verdict.

## Acceptance criteria

- The enum can hold a third-party transfer (distinct from `transfer_own`) and a payment to an apparent
  creditor, each DEFINED in the prompt.
- Every new category is flagged as a Priya-pending default (an undefined category is LP-340's bug).
- The two prompt copies stay byte-identical (the LP-344 convergence guard); the coercion value-sets stay in
  sync.
- **No live rule shifts.** `ACTIVE_RULE_IDS` unchanged; full suite green.

## The two gaps (quoted from Priya's notes)

- **Third-party transfers** — *"Looks like a person Ravi transferred money to Akash"* (inbound $147.70,
  `INBOUND PAYMENT RECEIVED`); *"Akash transferred money to Anand Patel, must be his friend"*; *"Too many
  transfers to other people."* The enum has only `transfer_own` (the borrower's OWN accounts). A third-party
  INBOUND could be a gift (needs a gift letter + donor sourcing) or an undisclosed loan (a problem). Forced
  into `transfer_own` (wrong — implies own money) or `unknown` (signal lost).
- **Payments to creditors** — *"Must be American Express CC payment"* ($14,316); *"Some kind of mortgage
  payment — make sure this is not a monthly obligation, if so then should flag?"* ($2,231, twice); *"This
  looks like some kind of scheduled obligation."* The enum lumps these into `fee`/`vendor`. A recurring
  mortgage/CC payment = an undisclosed monthly obligation that changes DTI — but no category records it, so no
  rule can ever catch it. **Priya's *"should flag?"* is an underwriter seeing a risk the system is
  structurally blind to.**

## Phase 0 — the argument (D1–D4)

### D1 — the tag reports the OBSERVABLE; recurrence is a rule's job
"Recurring" needs MULTIPLE statements — a single-statement AI cannot see recurrence. And Priya's descriptions
are **generic**: `CARD PURCHASE / PAYMENT` $14,316 → she inferred "American Express" from the **amount +
judgment**, not the text. So the honest, single-statement-observable tag is **`debt_payment`** — "a payment to
an apparent creditor" (a credit-card issuer, mortgage servicer, or loan), identifiable when the description
names a lender-like payee — **not** the ticket's proposed `recurring_obligation`. A **future DTI rule** detects
recurrence across statements and sizes the undisclosed obligation (noted, NOT built). The tag reports
payee-is-a-creditor; the rule judges recurrence.

### D2 — gift/loan_proceeds are rule conclusions, kept because dormant rules read them
Per LP-335, a bank statement rarely SAYS "gift"/"loan_proceeds"; those are conclusions after sourcing (AS-5
needs a signed gift letter; AS-2 an undisclosed-loan finding). The honest tag for an inbound is
`transfer_third_party_in`, and a RULE decides gift-vs-loan. **But** the gate of record shows dormant **AS-2**
(`apparent_category == loan_proceeds`), **AS-5** (`== gift` / `!= gift`), and **AS-12** read these values —
removing them would break those specs. So they are **kept**; the prompt reserves them for when the description
ITSELF states a gift/loan (rare); and the full simplification (gift/loan → rule conclusions, rewire AS-2/AS-5)
is flagged as a Priya-item + a future ticket. **This may simplify the enum later, once its dormant consumers
are re-architected — not in this ticket.**

### D3 — AS-1 equivalence is STRUCTURAL (the ticket's premise refuted)
AS-1's deterministic body reads `[txn.is_money_in, txn.amount, txn.has_identified_source,
txn.source_strength]` — it **never reads `apparent_category`** (the `fact_tags.csv` "used_by_rules: AS-1,…" is
optimistic; AS-1's actual spec does not reference it). **No LIVE rule reads `apparent_category`** — only
dormant AS-2/AS-5/AS-12 do. So widening the enum **cannot change any live verdict**. On LF-6T3N no committed
`apparent_category` value changes (the AI is not re-run; the frozen fixture tags stand). A test asserts no
`ACTIVE_RULE_IDS` spec references the tag.

### D4 — the convergence guard
The prompt lives in two byte-identical copies — the standalone `STAGE_A_TRANSACTION_SYSTEM_PROMPT` (app/ai,
what LIVE AS-1 runs) and the `txn_stage_a` YAML (what calibration measures) — pinned by
`test_txn_stage_a_prompt_convergence`. The enum also lives in `APPARENT_CATEGORY_VALUES` (standalone coercion)
and `fact_tags.csv` `allowed_values` (generic coercion). All four are updated together; a test asserts the two
coercion sets are equal, and the convergence guard stays green.

## The widened enum

`payroll | transfer_own | **transfer_third_party_in** | **transfer_third_party_out** | **debt_payment** |
gift | loan_proceeds | refund | interest | fee | vendor | unknown`

| New category (⚠ Priya-pending) | Definition (in the prompt) | Risk it records |
|---|---|---|
| `transfer_third_party_in` | Money IN from another person/entity the description names (Zelle/wire/check from a named individual). | Gift-or-undisclosed-loan candidate — a rule judges which after sourcing. |
| `transfer_third_party_out` | Money OUT to a named person/entity that is not a merchant or creditor. | An outbound obligation / undisclosed support. |
| `debt_payment` | A payment to an apparent CREDITOR (card issuer, mortgage servicer, or loan), from a lender-like payee — NOT from the amount. | An undisclosed monthly obligation that changes DTI (recurrence = a future rule). |

**Every new category is flagged for Priya** (in `fact_tags.csv`, `APPARENT_CATEGORY_VALUES`, the ADR) — these
are defensible defaults to confirm, not the final vocabulary.

## The prompt change (both copies, convergence intact)

`apparent_category` (item 2) now opens with *"Report ONLY what the description shows; a downstream RULE decides
the consequence (gift-vs-loan, whether a debt recurs)"* and DEFINES each new category, reserving
`gift`/`loan_proceeds` for descriptions that explicitly state them. The existing `transfer_own` / `vendor`
definitions (asserted by the convergence test) are preserved verbatim; `unknown` stays first-class; no
downstream-concern exemplar. Encoded as PRINCIPLE, not Priya's 50 cases.

## AS-1 before/after on LF-6T3N

**Identical.** AS-1 does not read `apparent_category`; the committed fixture tags are not re-run. No real
transaction's category changes in any committed artifact. (The worksheet is deliberately NOT regenerated — it
would destroy Priya's 35 in-progress notes; re-scoring her labels against the widened enum is LP-379-D.)

## Real off-path probe (reported, not predicted)

Materialized `txn_stage_a` (real model, no persist) on named-payee transactions mirroring Priya's cases:

```
ZELLE FROM RAVI KUMAR              -> transfer_third_party_in
ZELLE TO ANAND PATEL              -> transfer_third_party_out
WELLS FARGO HOME MORTGAGE AUTOPAY -> debt_payment
AMEX EPAYMENT ACH PMT            -> debt_payment
PAYROLL DIRECT DEPOSIT           -> payroll         (existing category unaffected)
```

The perception gap is closed **when the description names the third party / creditor**. On Priya's generic
`CARD PURCHASE / PAYMENT` descriptions the AI still cannot tell — that is a description limitation of the
de-identified fixture, not a vocabulary one; the point is the enum can now HOLD the distinction that real
statements (with named payees) reveal.

## Assumptions & decisions

- **New categories are Priya-pending defaults**, defined to be honest and testable; Priya confirms the final
  set (name, boundaries, whether `transfer_third_party_out` earns its keep).
- **`debt_payment`, not `recurring_obligation`** (D1) — the tag reports the observable creditor payment; a
  future DTI rule detects recurrence across statements.
- **gift/loan_proceeds kept** (D2) — dormant AS-2/AS-5/AS-12 read them; the LP-335 simplification (make them
  rule conclusions) is recommended for a future ticket, not forced here.
- **fact_tags.csv edited directly** — it is generated from `docs/snapshot-fact-tags.xlsx`; the xlsx should be
  updated to match (flagged). `vocabulary_extra.yaml` cannot override an existing tag's domain.
- **Worksheet not regenerated** — preserves Priya's notes; re-scoring is LP-379-D.

## The future DTI rule this enables (noted, not built)

`debt_payment` gives a cross-statement rule the observable it needs: gather a borrower's `debt_payment`
transactions across statements, detect a monthly cadence + a stable payee, and size the undisclosed monthly
obligation against the 1003 / DTI. That rule is out of scope here — this ticket only makes the observable
recordable.

**Gate-of-record note:** the vocabulary ALREADY carries dormant `txn.is_recurring` ("recurring debit/credit
pattern across statements", used by dormant FR-5/CR-1) and `txn.implies_obligation` ("undisclosed monthly
obligation", FR-4/FR-5/CR-1) — separate tags, produced AI-at-rule-time, not by the live Stage-A pass. They
reinforce D1: recurrence/obligation is already modeled as a rule-time judgment, distinct from Stage-A's
`apparent_category`. `debt_payment` complements them — it lets the LIVE Stage-A pass record the observable
"payment to a creditor" (today `fee`/`vendor`/`unknown`) that a future DTI rule would consume alongside
`is_recurring`.
