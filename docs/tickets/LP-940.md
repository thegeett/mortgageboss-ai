# LP-940 — Withdraw a condition entered in error

- **Ticket:** LP-940 · Phase 4.5 Stage 3 follow-up
- **Source:** the product owner, 2026-09-29:
  - only for conditions added by hand (`origin = manual`); sheet conditions keep ADR-404's rule;
  - "Withdraw" on the detail sheet asks for a short reason. It soft-deletes (`deleted_at`), writes a
    `condition_withdrawn` event with the reason, and removes the condition from the list, counts, plan,
    drafts and package;
  - it stays in the history, in a collapsed "Withdrawn (n)" section, with Undo;
  - it is refused, with a sentence saying why, if the condition has a recorded lender verdict or was in
    a submitted package;
  - recorded in `decisions.md` as an amendment to ADR-404.

## Design

**Service** (`app/services/condition_withdraw.py`):
- `withdraw(condition, reason, actor)`. It refuses, each with its own sentence:
  - a sheet condition: "Only a condition you added by hand can be withdrawn. One from the lender's sheet
    stays until the lender clears or waives it.";
  - a recorded lender verdict (any `condition_verdict_recorded` event): "The lender's answer is recorded
    on it, so it stays on the file.";
  - a submitted package holding it: "It went to the lender in the package submitted on 09/09/2026, so
    it stays on the file.";
  - an empty reason: "Say why — the reason is kept in the history.". The reason is at most 500 characters.

  Otherwise it sets `deleted_at`, writes `condition_withdrawn` with `{"reason": …}`, and takes it out of
  the drafts (below).
- `restore(condition, actor)`, which is Undo: clears `deleted_at` and writes `condition_restored`. It
  refuses a condition that is not withdrawn, and re-syncs the drafts so its asks return.
- `withdrawn_for_file(loan_file)`: the withdrawn hand-added conditions, newest first, each with its
  reason and when it was withdrawn (from its latest `condition_withdrawn` event).

**Taking it out.** Every reader already skips soft-deleted conditions, except these, which are fixed here
(census in the Verification section):
- **Drafts.** The round sync skips it, but its items stay linked to an unsent draft. `withdraw` unlinks
  its items from unsent drafts, discards an unsent question draft of its own, and re-syncs. Sent drafts
  are history and stay.
- **Package.** A built but unsubmitted package's stored rows can hold it. The view, the download and
  Mark submitted skip rows whose condition is withdrawn. The stored rows are not rewritten.
- **Figures check.** `_funds_evidence` loads the condition by id and now skips a withdrawn one.

**Routes:**
- `POST /conditions/{id}/withdraw {reason}`, behind `get_scoped_condition` (active only);
- `POST /loan-files/{id}/conditions/{condition_id}/restore`, behind the file's gate, because a withdrawn
  condition is not found by `get_scoped_condition` by design;
- `GET /loan-files/{id}/withdrawn-conditions`.

Refusals are 409 with the sentence.

**Migration:** the event-kind CHECK gains `condition_withdrawn` and `condition_restored`, 31 kinds, all
listed, in raw `op.execute`. The downgrade refuses while any such event exists, rather than deleting
history.

**Frontend:**
- **The sheet:** a "Withdraw" control only on a hand-added condition. It asks for the reason inline
  ("Why — for example, added twice by mistake") and confirms with the reason's button. A refusal shows
  its sentence.
- **The list view:** a collapsed "Withdrawn (n) · codes" section below the list. Each row shows the
  code, the words, "Withdrawn 09/29 — <reason>" and **Undo**.
- **History:** a line for each kind (as built: without the reason, see below). `ALL_KINDS` goes to 31.

**ADR-404 amended:** a hand-added condition is the processor's own entry, not the lender's demand, so
she may withdraw it with a reason, and nothing is deleted. Only conditions she typed qualify, and never
once the lender has answered on it or it has gone to the lender.

**Built as designed, with one change:** `restore` refuses only a condition that is not soft-deleted. A
first version also checked that the deletion came from a withdrawal. Measured: nothing else in the app
sets `conditions.deleted_at`, so that check could never fire, and a test for it would have had to invent
the state. It is replaced by `test_only_a_withdrawal_deletes_a_condition`, which fails the day a second
writer appears.

**History:** the lines read "Withdrawn as entered in error" and "Put back". Her reason is not in the
history's allow-list (LP-923's precedent for her free text); it shows on the Withdrawn section's row,
from its own endpoint.
