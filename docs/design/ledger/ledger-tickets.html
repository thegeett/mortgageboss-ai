02 — The critical pathWhere the order actually matters

Only three things in forty tickets are genuinely sequential. Everything else can move.

Epic A must land in order and must land first. Everything after it assumes tokens exist and no gray-* remains. Two backend tickets — saved views and the reconciliation read model — can start immediately and in parallel, because the two screens that need them come later. And one ticket gates an entire epic: until LP-UI-029 is answered, the document reviewer cannot be scheduled honestly.

Expect this, and don't fight it The app will look worse between LP-UI-001 and LP-UI-004. 784 hardcoded greys are still fighting the new tokens during those three tickets. Do not tune the palette to compensate — LP-UI-004 is what resolves it.

Run biome, tsc and the tests. Check light and dark. Write
docs/tickets/LP-UI-XXX.md per this repo's convention. Stop and show me
before the next ticket.

Start with LP-UI-001.

06 — Before LP-UI-001Four answers

These are baked into the token file, so they are cheaper to settle now than after five tickets have been built on them. My recommendations are in the table in README.md; the short version is petrol over the default blue, dark mode now, stay on Radix, and yes to Needs as its own route.

And one more, which is not a preference but a fact I don't have: does the extraction pipeline store bounding-box coordinates? SourceLocation carries page and snippet today. If the answer is no, LP-UI-029 starts in the backend and the reviewer moves out of week seven.
